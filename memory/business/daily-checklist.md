# Business Checklist - Irvin's Tech Services

## Daily Tasks
- [ ] Check emails & respond to inquiries (morning + end of day)
- [ ] Review lead pipeline / new client inquiries
- [ ] Update job tracker / project status
- [ ] Log completed work & invoice if ready
- [ ] Social media: 1 post (LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook)
- [ ] Review calendar for next-day appointments

---

## Weekly Tasks
- [ ] Financial review: income, expenses, outstanding invoices
- [ ] Follow up on pending proposals/quotes
- [ ] Update website/portfolio with completed projects
- [ ] Job site visits or client meetings (as scheduled)
- [ ] Equipment/supply inventory check
- [ ] Marketing: plan next week's content
- [ ] Review KPIs: leads converted, revenue, jobs completed

---

## Monthly Tasks
- [ ] Full financial reconciliation (income statement, expenses)
- [ ] Pay quarterly estimated taxes (if applicable)
- [ ] Review pricing: adjust for material/labor cost changes
- [ ] Client follow-up: check in with past clients for referrals
- [ ] Update service offerings & pricing sheets
- [ ] Tax prep: organize receipts & deductions
- [ ] Marketing audit: what's working, what's not

---

## Quarterly Tasks
- [ ] Strategic review: goals vs actuals, adjust roadmap
- [ ] Business development: identify new markets/services
- [ ] Insurance & licensing renewal check
- [ ] Equipment depreciation & replacement planning
- [ ] SEO/website audit & optimization
- [ ] Review contractor/partner relationships
- [ ] Set goals for next quarter
