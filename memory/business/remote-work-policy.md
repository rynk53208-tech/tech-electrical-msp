# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** March 2026  
**Business:** Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services  
**Owner:** Irvin Avitia

---

## 1. Security Requirements

### Network & Access
- **VPN Required:** All remote work must be conducted through a company-approved VPN when accessing client systems, internal networks, or sensitive data.
- **Home Network:** Employees must ensure their home Wi-Fi is WPA3 or WPA2-AES encrypted. Open networks are prohibited.
- **Firewall:** Local firewall must be enabled on all work devices.

### Device Security
- **Encryption:** All devices must have full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault) enabled.
- **Antivirus:** Company-provided or approved endpoint protection must be active and current.
- **Patching:** OS and software updates must be applied within 72 hours of release. Critical security patches require installation within 24 hours.
- **Passwords:** Use company password manager. Minimum 16-character passphrases for accounts, changed annually or immediately if compromised.
- **MFA:** Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts (email, VPN, ticketing systems, cloud services).

### Data Handling
- **No Local Storage:** Sensitive client data must not be stored locally on personal devices. Use approved cloud storage only.
- **Clear Screen:** Lock screens when stepping away (auto-lock set to 5 minutes or less).
- **Incidents:** Report any suspected security incident within 1 hour to the security team.

## 2. Equipment Policy

### Company-Provided Equipment
- Laptop/desktop assigned by the company for work purposes
- Company-licensed software and tools
- Mobile device (if role requires)
- Peripherals as needed (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset)

### Employee Responsibilities
- Equipment must be used primarily for business purposes
- Report hardware issues within 24 hours
- Return all equipment upon termination in working condition
- Personal devices may only be used for work if enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM) and approved by management

### Maintenance & Support
- IT provides remote support during business hours
- Hardware repairs handled on-site or via mail with 48-hour turnaround
- Replacement equipment provided for failed hardware (under warranty)

### Remote Work Setup Stipend
- Employees may qualify for a monthly internet/utility stipend if working remotely full-time, subject to manager approval

## 3. Communication Expectations

### Availability
- **Core Hours:** 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Pacific) — all remote employees must be reachable
- **Response Times:**
  - Urgent client issues: Within 30 minutes
  - Internal messages: Within 2 hours during business hours
  - Email: Within 4 hours during business hours
- **After-Hours:** On-call rotation as assigned; critical alerts must be acknowledged within 15 minutes

### Tools & Channels
- **Primary:** Slack or Teams for instant messaging
- **Email:** Formal communication, documentation, external clients
- **Phone/Video:** Client calls, meetings, urgent issues
- **Ticketing System:** All client work must be logged in the PSA/PSA tool

### Meetings
- Camera on for client meetings and team syncs (unless bandwidth-limited)
- Schedule meetings during core hours when possible
- Send agendas 24 hours in advance for recurring meetings
- Meeting notes distributed within 24 hours

### Status Updates
- Update Slack status when away from keyboard > 30 minutes
- Notify team of planned time off at least 48 hours in advance

## 4. Performance Metrics

### Productivity Benchmarks
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Ticket Resolution | Meet SLA commitments | PSA system |
| Billable Hours | 32-40 hrs/week (role-dependent) | Time tracking |
| First Contact Resolution | ≥60% | Ticket analysis |
| Client Satisfaction | ≥4.5/5.0 | Post-job surveys |
| Documentation | 100% ticket notes within 24 hrs | PSA audit |

### Remote Work-Specific Metrics
- **Attendance:** 90%+ attendance at required meetings
- **Response Rate:** >95% of messages acknowledged within SLA
- **Availability:** Core hours coverage ≥95%

### Review Cycle
- **Weekly:** Quick sync with manager (15 min)
- **Monthly:** Review metrics, blockers, priorities
- **Quarterly:** Full performance review with feedback

### Performance Improvement
- Missed SLAs trigger a performance review after 3 occurrences in a 30-day window
- Corrective action plan created within 5 business days
- Termination for repeated policy violations or gross negligence
