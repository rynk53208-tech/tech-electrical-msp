# Customer Success Playbook

> **Overseer Strategic Document** | Created: 2026-03-18
> Focus: Complete customer lifecycle management

---

## Overview

This playbook covers the full customer journey — from first contact to long-term partnership. Each phase has specific goals, actions, and metrics to track success.

**Core Principle:** Customer success isn't a department — it's a mindset. Every interaction shapes whether a client stays, grows, or refers others.

---

## 1. Onboarding Success

### 1.1 Pre-Onboarding (Before They Sign)

**Objective:** Set the foundation for a successful relationship

- [ ] **Send welcome packet** — Include service agreement, pricing, SLA terms, emergency contacts, and what to expect first
- [ ] **Assign account owner** — Who owns this relationship? Introduce them before day one
- [ ] **Gather critical info:**
  - Business goals and priorities
  - Key contacts and decision-makers
  - Current systems, pain points, and history
  - Emergency procedures and escalation paths
- [ ] **Set expectations** — Timeline, communication cadence, what happens first

### 1.2 First Week

**Objective:** Make them feel like they made the right choice

- [ ] **Day 1: Confirmation call** — "Here's what happens next, here's who you contact for X"
- [ ] **Day 2-3: Initial assessment** — If service involves auditing their systems, do it fast and document findings
- [ ] **Day 5: First deliverable** — Show progress early. Send a status update even if nothing "finished"
- [ ] **First check-in call** — Ask: "Any questions? Anything we missed?"

### 1.3 First 30 Days

**Objective:** Establish trust and demonstrate value

- [ ] **Complete initial scope** — Finish whatever onboarding project you promised
- [ ] **Document everything** — Create their client folder: contacts, systems, credentials (securely), preferences
- [ ] **First quarterly goal discussion** — Understand what success looks like for them this quarter
- [ ] **Introduce the full team** — If they might interact with others, make intros now
- [ ] **30-day check-in** — "How are we doing? What's working? What could be better?"

### Onboarding Checklist

| Day | Action | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| -3 to -1 | Send welcome packet, assign owner | Sales |
| 0 | Contract signed, schedule kickoff | Account Owner |
| 1 | Kickoff call, confirm scope | Account Owner |
| 3 | Initial assessment complete | Tech lead |
| 7 | First progress update | Account Owner |
| 14 | Mid-onboarding check-in | Account Owner |
| 30 | 30-day review, set QBR schedule | Account Owner |

### Onboarding Success Metrics

- **Time to first value (TTFV):** How fast do they see results?
- **30-day satisfaction score:** Survey at day 30
- **Documentation completeness:** Is their folder complete?
- **Escalation rate:** How many issues in first 30 days?

## 2. Ongoing Engagement

### 2.1 Regular Communication Cadence

**Objective:** Stay top-of-mind without being annoying

| Client Tier | Check-ins | Proactive Outreach |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Premium/High-value** | Monthly | Weekly updates, quarterly business reviews |
| **Standard** | Quarterly | Monthly newsletter, as-needed |
| **Basic/Maintenance** | Bi-annual | Quarterly, when renewal near |

### 2.2 Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)

**Objective:** Strategic partnership, not just service reporting

**Pre-QBR:**
- Pull metrics: uptime, tickets resolved, projects completed, response times
- Review their business: Any growth? New challenges? Industry changes?
- Prepare 3 discussion topics: what's working, what's not, what's next

**QBR Agenda (45-60 min):**
1. **Retrospective** — Review last quarter's goals and results
2. **Current state** — Metrics that matter to their business
3. **Forward look** — Upcoming needs, projects, seasonality
4. **Partnership health** — Any concerns? Feedback?
5. **Next quarter commitments** — What we'll deliver, what they need to do

**Post-QBR:**
- Send summary email with action items and owners
- Schedule next QBR before leaving

### 2.3 Proactive Engagement

**Objective:** Be the advisor, not just the vendor

- **Security alerts** — Forward relevant CVE notices, threat intel
- **Industry news** — "Saw this about [their industry] — might affect you"
- **Technology tips** — "New tool that could streamline X"
- **Business reminders** — "Your SSL certs expire in 30 days" (before they know it)
- **Holiday/seasonal check-ins** — "Year-end backup check — want us to verify?"

### 2.4 Communication Standards

- **Response time:** Same business day (4-hour target)
- **Status updates:**主动 (proactive) — don't wait for them to ask
- **Escalation clarity:** Know who to loop in and when
- **Tone:** Professional but human. No jargon. Answer the question, then explain if needed.

## 3. Upsell Opportunities

### 3.1 When to Pitch

**Natural Upsell Triggers:**

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Client mentions a pain point | Listen, empathize, then pitch solution |
| After successful project delivery | Trust is high — suggest next steps |
| Business growth (new office, hires) | They're expanding = new needs |
| Equipment aging/lifecycle | "We noticed X is 4 years old — want to plan replacement?" |
| New service launched | Intro relevant new offerings |
| Competitor complaint | "We can do X better — want a demo?" |
| Renewal approaching | Bundle incentives for multi-year |

### 3.2 Upsell Framework

**Step 1: Identify the need**
- Ask questions: "What's the biggest challenge right now?"
- Listen for gaps: "We wish we had someone who could also handle..."

**Step 2: Connect to value**
- Don't lead with price. Lead with outcome.
- "This would reduce your downtime by X%"
- "This saves you roughly Y hours per week"

**Step 3: Make it easy**
- Bundle with existing services (discount for packaged)
- Offer trial period: "Try it for 30 days, no commitment"
- Scope creep protection: Clear boundaries on what's included

**Step 4: Follow up**
- If they say no, ask why. Document it.
- Revisit in 90 days. Needs change.

### 3.3 Upsell Scripts

**Service Expansion:**
> "We noticed you're handling more of [X] in-house. We'd be happy to take that off your plate — free up your time and it's covered under your existing agreement."

**New Project:**
> "Since we wrapped up [recent project], we've been thinking about [related opportunity]. Want to spend 15 minutes exploring if it makes sense?"

**Upgrade Tier:**
> "Based on your growth, you'd get more value from our [higher tier]. Here's what that unlocks..."

**Referral + Upsell Combo:**
> "We're expanding our [new service] — if you know anyone who could use it, we'd love to help. And as a current client, we'll prioritize your implementation."

### 3.4 Upsell Metrics

- **Attachment rate:** % of clients with 2+ services
- **Upsell conversion rate:** % of pitches that close
- **Revenue per client:** Track over time
- **Average time to first upsell:** How long until they buy more?

## 4. Retention Strategies

### 4.1 Preventing Churn

**Early Warning Signs:**
- Reduced engagement (slower responses, fewer check-ins)
- Renewal delays or "re-reviewing"
- Questions about competitors
- Complaints or dissatisfaction signals
- Budget cuts mentioned

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Reach out personally — Don't email. Call or visit.
2. Acknowledge the issue — "We noticed things have been quiet..."
3. Ask directly — "Are we still meeting your expectations?"
4. Fix fast — Whatever the problem is, solve it now
5. Follow up — Confirm it's resolved

### 4.2 Building Stickiness

**Make yourself indispensable:**
- Own something they can't easily replace (custom configs, integrations, tribal knowledge)
- Be the first call — when something breaks, you're #1 in their phone
- Solve problems they didn't know they had — show value they didn't expect

**Relationship depth:**
- Know their business as well as you know their systems
- Remember personal details (birthdays, holidays, milestones)
- Connect them with other valuable contacts in your network

### 4.3 Renewal Management

**60-90 Days Before Renewal:**
- [ ] Schedule renewal conversation
- [ ] Review past year's performance (wins, metrics)
- [ ] Prepare proposed plan for next term
- [ ] Address any outstanding issues
- [ ] Discuss upcoming needs/growth

**30 Days Before:**
- [ ] Send renewal proposal with preferred tier
- [ ] Include incentives for early renewal (discount, bonus services)
- [ ] Clear any final obstacles

**Renewal Conversation:**
> "It's been a great year. We want to make sure we're delivering value. Here's what we accomplished together, and here's what we're proposing for next year."

### 4.4 Exit Management

If they do leave:
- **Ask why** — Exit interview, honest feedback
- **Offer transition help** — Don't burn the bridge
- **Stay in touch** — Past clients can refer or return
- **Learn and improve** — Document what went wrong
