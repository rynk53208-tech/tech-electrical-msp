## Proposed Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Standard Repair (Walk-in/Quick Jobs)
- **$75-100 flat** for common issues (screen, battery, simple board)
- 30-day warranty
- 24-48hr turnaround
- Includes labor + standard parts

### Tier 2: Premium Repair (Priority Service)
- **$150-200 flat**
- Same-day/next-day service
- 90-day warranty
- Premium parts option
- Diagnostic fee waived with repair

### Tier 3: Board-Level/Micro Solder (Your Differentiator)
- **$200-400+** (depending on complexity)
- This is YOUR northrop-level skill - price accordingly
- 90-day warranty
- Detailed before/after imaging
