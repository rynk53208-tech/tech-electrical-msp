# KPI Dashboard - Irvin's Tech Business

## Revenue & Sales Metrics

| KPI | How to Measure | Goal | Frequency |
|-----|----------------|------|-----------|
| **Monthly Revenue** | Total invoiced amount from all services | $15K/mo (Yr1), $30K/mo (Yr2) | Weekly |
| **New Clients Acquired** | Count of new customers signed | 4-6 new clients/mo | Weekly |
| **Average Invoice Value** | Total revenue ÷ # of invoices | $800-$1,500 per job | Monthly |
| **Project Pipeline Value** | Sum of all pending proposals × close rate | $50K+ qualified pipeline | Weekly |
| **Service Revenue Mix** | % from each service line (MSP, repairs, consulting) | MSP: 40%, Repairs: 30%, Consulting: 30% | Monthly |

---

## Service Delivery Metrics

| KPI | How to Measure | Goal | Frequency |
|-----|----------------|------|-----------|
| **First-Time Fix Rate** | Repairs resolved on first visit ÷ total repairs | 85%+ | Weekly |
| **Ticket/Job Completion Time** | Avg hours from intake to completion | <24hrs (standard), <4hrs (urgent) | Weekly |
| **Customer Satisfaction Score** | Post-job surveys (1-10 scale) | 9.0+ average | After each job |
| **RTO (Return Rate)** | % of clients who re-purchase within 90 days | 60%+ | Monthly |
| **Service Agreement Renewal** | % of MSP clients renewing annual contracts | 90%+ | Quarterly |

---

## Marketing & Lead Metrics

| KPI | How to Measure | Goal | Frequency |
|-----|----------------|------|-----------|
| **Lead Volume** | Incoming inquiries (phone, web, referral) | 20+ leads/mo | Weekly |
| **Lead-to-Client Conversion** | Closed deals ÷ total leads | 25%+ | Weekly |
| **Cost Per Lead** | Marketing spend ÷ # of leads | <$25/lead | Monthly |
| **Referral Rate** | % of new clients from word-of-mouth | 40%+ | Monthly |
| **Website Traffic** | Unique visitors to business site | 500+ visits/mo | Monthly |

---

## Operational Efficiency

| KPI | How to Measure | Goal | Frequency |
|-----|----------------|------|-----------|
| **Billable Utilization** | Billable hours ÷ total work hours | 70%+ | Weekly |
| **Technician Productivity** | Revenue generated per technician | $3K+/week per tech | Weekly |
| **Inventory Turnover** | Parts used ÷ parts stocked | 4x/quarter | Monthly |
| **No-Show Rate** | % of appointments missed | <5% | Weekly |
| **Average Response Time** | Time from inquiry to first response | <2 hours | Weekly |

---

## Financial Health

| KPI | How to Measure | Goal | Frequency |
|-----|----------------|------|-----------|
| **Gross Profit Margin** | (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue | 50%+ | Monthly |
| **Net Profit Margin** | (Revenue - All Expenses) ÷ Revenue | 20%+ | Monthly |
| **Days Sales Outstanding** | Avg days to collect payment | <30 days | Weekly |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost** | Sales/marketing spend ÷ new clients | <$200/client | Monthly |
| **Customer Lifetime Value** | Avg revenue per client × avg relationship years | $5K+ | Quarterly |

---

## Quick Reference: Priority KPIs

**Daily:** Lead response time, no-shows, ticket volume  
**Weekly:** Revenue, new clients, utilization, conversion rate  
**Monthly:** Profit margins, pipeline value, satisfaction score, inventory  
**Quarterly:** LTV, CAC, contract renewals, revenue mix

---

## Recommended Tools

- **Finance:** QuickBooks or Wave (free)
- **CRM/Tracking:** Notion, Airtable, or HubSpot free tier
- **Scheduling:** Jobber, ServiceTitan (free tier), or Google Calendar
- **Surveys:** Typeform or Google Forms for post-job CSAT

## Notes

- MSP/MSSP contracts provide recurring revenue → prioritize these
- Board-level repair is a differentiator → price premium, market it
- Electrical + IT combo is rare → bundle services for higher ticket sizes
