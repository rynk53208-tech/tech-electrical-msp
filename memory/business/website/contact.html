FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer emergency support?

+

Yes. MSP clients get 24/7 emergency support as part of their plan. For non-MSP clients, we offer emergency on-call service — call (951) 555-0100 and select the emergency option. Emergency rates apply.

How far do you travel for on-site work?

+

We're based in Temecula and regularly serve the entire Inland Empire (Riverside and San Bernardino counties) plus North San Diego County — Fallbrook, Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore, and beyond. For larger projects, we travel further. Just ask.

Can you handle both IT and electrical on the same project?

+

That's our specialty. When you're building a server room, doing an office build-out, or installing infrastructure, we handle the IT design AND the electrical work — no hand-offs between two separate contractors. One point of contact, one invoice.

What's the diagnostic fee for computer repair?

+

Our diagnostic fee is $49 and is applied toward the cost of any repair. If you choose not to proceed after diagnosis, you pay the $49 and get a full written report of what we found.

Do MSP contracts require a long-term commitment?

+

We offer month-to-month and annual contracts. Annual contracts receive a 10% discount. We believe in earning your business every month — not locking you in.

Are you licensed and insured?

+

Yes. Our electrical partner Charles Garcia is a licensed electrician in California. We carry general liability insurance. Ask us for proof of license and insurance — we'll provide it immediately.
