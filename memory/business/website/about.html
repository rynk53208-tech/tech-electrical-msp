Our Story

From Help Desk to
Defense Contractor

Irvin started in IT at USGS on the help desk — the ground floor. Over the next decade, he worked his way through Desktop Support at Tree Line Biosciences, PC Technician roles (II and III) at Northrop Grumman, and eventually into Cyber Systems Engineering and DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure at one of the world's leading defense contractors.

Along the way, he earned a BS in Information Technology and a MS in Computer Science, and picked up his CompTIA Security+ certification. He also taught himself micro soldering and board-level hardware repair — the kind of skills that let him fix what other technicians turn away.

Tech & Electrical Services LLC was born from a simple idea: bring that same level of expertise to local businesses in Temecula, but with the responsiveness and personal service that big IT firms can't offer.
