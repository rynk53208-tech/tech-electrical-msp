## 3. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 3.1 What Constitutes Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, either directly or indirectly, in writing, orally, or by inspection, including but not limited to:

- **Technical Data:** Network architectures, system configurations, IP addresses, software credentials, source code, API documentation
- **Security Information:** Vulnerability assessments, penetration test results, security policies, access credentials, encryption keys
- **Business Information:** Customer lists, pricing, marketing plans, financial data, supplier relationships
- **Operational Data:** Business processes, workflows, internal procedures, personnel information
- **Physical Materials:** Documents, diagrams, equipment, prototypes

### 3.2 Exceptions
Confidential Information does NOT include information that Recipient can demonstrate:
- Was publicly known at the time of disclosure or becomes publicly known through no fault of Recipient
- Was properly in Recipient's possession prior to disclosure (with documented proof)
- Is independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information
- Is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction
- Is approved for release by written authorization of Discloser

## 4. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

Recipient agrees to:

### 4.1 Protection
- Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- Use Confidential Information ONLY for the Purpose stated above
- Protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care used for its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care

### 4.2 Access Limitations
- Limit access to Confidential Information to employees, contractors, and agents who:
  - Have a need to know for the Purpose
  - Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement
  - Are informed of the confidential nature of the information

### 4.3 Data Security Requirements
When handling electronic Confidential Information, Recipient must:
- Use industry-standard encryption (AES-256 or equivalent) for data at rest and in transit
- Maintain multi-factor authentication for systems accessing Discloser's data
- Maintain current security patches and updates
- Implement role-based access controls
- Maintain audit logs of access (retained for minimum 90 days)
- Promptly notify Discloser of any security breach or suspected breach

### 4.4 Handling Physical Materials
- Store in locked, secure locations when not in use
- Return or destroy upon request or termination of the business relationship
- Not make copies unless necessary for the Purpose
