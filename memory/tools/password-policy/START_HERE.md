## 🔐 6 Compliance Frameworks

### 🏥 HIPAA (Healthcare)
For medical offices, dental clinics, any healthcare provider.  
Min 8 chars, 90-day expiration, audit logging, breach notification.

### 💳 PCI-DSS (Payment Cards)
For retail, restaurants, auto repair — anyone accepting credit cards.  
Min 7 chars, 90-day expiration, 6-attempt lockout, MFA required, penetration testing.

### 📊 SOX (Financial Reporting)
For publicly-traded companies, financial services.  
Min 10 chars, 60-day expiration, 12-password history, audit trail.

### 🔐 GDPR (EU Privacy)
For any company with EU customer data (global applicability).  
Min 12 chars, MFA mandatory, 30-day breach notification.

### 🛡️ NIST 800-63 (US Cybersecurity)
For government, defense contractors, critical infrastructure.  
Min 12 chars, **NO expiration** (modern guidance), breach-triggered reset, rate limiting.

### 🎯 ISO 27001 (Information Security)
For enterprise, security-conscious organizations.  
Min 12 chars, 120-day expiration, 10-password history, annual review.

## 🎓 10 Security Best Practices

The tool includes these best practices for client training:

1. **Use Passphrases** → "BlueSky!Coffee@Sunrise" (memorable & strong)
2. **Never Share Passwords** → Even IT staff should use session-based access
3. **Use Password Managers** → Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass
4. **Enable MFA** → 90% of breaches could be prevented with MFA
5. **Avoid Expiration Overload** → Modern NIST guidance: no expiration, breach-triggered reset
6. **Quality > Quantity** → Strong passwords > weak patterns from forced complexity
7. **Account Lockout** → 5–6 attempts, 30-min lockout prevents brute force
8. **Monitor Breached Passwords** → Check "Have I Been Pwned" on resets
9. **Regular Audits** → Quarterly review of failed attempts & privilege accounts
10. **User Training** → Annual security awareness & phishing simulations
