# Combo Pricing — MSP + Electrical Bundles

*ElectricPlanner — Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services*

---

## Pricing Philosophy

- **Stack services** — If client needs IT AND electrical, bundle them
- **Combo discount** — 10-15% off electrical when paired with MSP contract
- **Transparent** — No hidden fees, everything itemized
- **Profit in both columns** — Don't sacrifice electrical margins for MSP relationship

## Proposal Customization Notes

- Always baseline with the MSP proposal first, then layer electrical
- Lead with total value — "We handle everything from cybersecurity to code-compliant electrical"
- Use Package 3 for larger clients — it's the most profitable and locks them in
- Be ready to adjust based on site survey findings
- Upsell opportunity: If they're comparing MSPs, the electrical angle wins deals

## Profit Margins

| Service | Target Margin |
|---|---|

Don't underprice electrical to win MSP business. The bundle discount comes from efficiency, not margin sacrifice.
