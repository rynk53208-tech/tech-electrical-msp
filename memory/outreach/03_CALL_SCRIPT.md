# 60-Second Cold Call Script
## Tech & Electrical Services LLC

---

## THE SCRIPT (Practice this aloud—timing matters)

**[0-3 seconds] INTRO + PERMISSION**

"Hi [NAME], this is Irvin Avitia with Tech & Electrical Services. Do you have 60 seconds? I'll be quick."

*[Wait for yes—if no, "Perfect, I'll keep this to one minute"]*

---

**[3-12 seconds] CREDIBILITY HOOK**

"I'm calling local [BUSINESS TYPE] because most are overpaying on IT and exposed to cyber risk they don't know about. I used to be a Cyber Systems Engineer at Northrop Grumman, and now we help practices like yours get infrastructure right—plus we do the electrical side, which most IT shops can't."

---

**[12-25 seconds] SPECIFIC PROBLEM (Pick ONE based on business type)**

**→ Medical/Dental:**  
"HIPAA compliance is a nightmare. Most MSPs check a box; we actually audit you and fix it. And ransomware? That's not if, it's when."

**→ Restaurant:**  
"Your POS goes down, you lose $1,000+ an hour. Kitchen electrical infrastructure probably isn't even managed by your IT guy. We handle both."

**→ Auto Repair:**  
"Your diagnostic tools are only as good as your network. Plus, downtime kills you. We audit the whole infrastructure—diagnosis to electrical load."

**→ Retail:**  
"Your POS systems are vulnerable. One breach and you're liable. We secure them, plus make sure you never lose a sale to network downtime."

---

**[25-50 seconds] THE OFFER (Specific + Low Barrier)**

"Here's what I'd like to do: 30 minutes next week, I'll walk through your infrastructure, show you 2-3 specific risks you've got, and how much you're probably overpaying. It's a $500 value, and it's free. No pitch—just clarity."

*[Pause for response]*

---

**[50-55 seconds] CLOSE (Pick ONE)**

**→ If interested:**  
"Perfect. Can you do Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon?"

**→ If hesitant:**  
"Look, worst case you learn something. Best case you save thousands. Worth 30 minutes?"

---

**[55-60 seconds] CONFIRM + EXIT**

"Great. I'll send a quick email with times. [NAME], thanks for your time—talk soon."

*[Hang up professionally]*

---

## Key Timing Notes

- **Intro + permission:** Fast, polite, not pushy
- **Credibility hook:** Drop Northrop early (subconscious weight)
- **Problem statement:** Should land like "Yeah, that's us"
- **Offer:** Super specific (30 min, $500 value, free) = low friction
- **Pause points:** Wait for responses—script is a guide, not a robot read
- **Close:** Assume yes (calendar, not "can I call back?")

---

## OBJECTION HANDLERS

### "We already have IT support"

**Response (8 seconds):**  
"Got it. Quick question—are they handling your [SPECIFIC THING]? Because most generic MSPs miss [ELECTRICAL/SECURITY/HARDWARE] stuff."

*Listen. Then:*  
"That's exactly why I'm calling. 30-minute audit shows you if there's gaps. No pressure—just want to make sure you're covered."

---

### "Not interested"

**Response (5 seconds):**  
"Totally understand. One quick thought—if your systems went down tomorrow, how long would it take to get back up? And what's that cost you?"

*Listen.* If they admit pain:  
"That's exactly what we prevent. Worth a quick call?"

---

### "I don't make those decisions"

**Response (6 seconds):**  
"Fair enough. Who's the right person—is it [MANAGER/OWNER/IT PERSON]? I can reach out to them, or you can loop them on my email?"

*Get name + permission to reference them.*

---

### "Send me something"

**Response (4 seconds):**  
"Will do. Real quick—are you worried about [SECURITY/UPTIME/COSTS] most? I'll make sure I send what's relevant to you."

*Take that answer in the email subject line.*

---

## Success Indicators

✅ You scheduled a call or meeting  
✅ You got a specific person's name (even if not the decision-maker)  
✅ You uncovered a specific pain (compliance, downtime, cost)  
✅ You sent a follow-up email with dates/times  

❌ Bad: "Call me back sometime"  
❌ Bad: They took your info but you didn't move the needle  
❌ Bad: You stayed on the phone >2 minutes  

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Smile before dialing.** Seriously. It changes your tone.
2. **Use their name 1-2 times max.** Sounds authentic, not salesy.
3. **Own the pause.** Silence after your offer = let them think. Don't fill it.
4. **Stay local.** "I'm here in Temecula" adds credibility.
5. **One specific detail about their industry.** Shows you know them, not reading a script.
6. **Timing matters more than perfection.** Keep it under 60 seconds.
