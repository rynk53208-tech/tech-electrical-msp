60-SECOND CALL STRUCTURE
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[0-3 sec] INTRO
  "Hi [NAME], this is Irvin. Do you have 60 seconds?"

[3-12 sec] CREDIBILITY HOOK
  "[Northrop background + your unique combo]"

[12-25 sec] THEIR PAIN (pick ONE)
  Medical: "HIPAA is a nightmare. Most MSPs check a box; we audit."
  Auto:    "Your diagnostic tools are only as good as your network."
  Rest:    "When POS goes down, you lose $1,000+ an hour."

[25-50 sec] OFFER
  "30 minutes next week, I'll show 2-3 specific risks + savings. 
   $500 value, free. No pitch—just clarity."

[50-55 sec] CLOSE
  If yes: "Perfect. Tuesday or Wednesday?"
  If no:  "Worst case you learn something. Worth 30 min?"

[55-60 sec] CONFIRM
  "Great. I'll email you times. Thanks."

═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DO'S & DON'TS (QUICK REFERENCE)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ DO:
  - Personalize every email (name + industry angle)
  - Email 24 hours before calling (warm up prospect)
  - Practice script 3x before calling
  - Use prospect's name 1-2x per call
  - Book calls immediately (same day if yes)
  - Stay under 60 seconds on first call
  - Log every outcome in CRM

❌ DON'T:
  - Cold call without email first (5% answer rate)
  - Send generic templates (looks like spam)
  - Call outside 9am-5pm (lower answer rate)
  - Stay on phone >2 minutes (they say no faster)
  - Follow up >3 times (Email + 2 calls = enough)
  - Promise things you can't deliver
  - Give up after 1 no (ask why, try alternative)
