## Quick Personalization Tips

| Industry | Pain Point to Emphasize | Compliance Note |
|----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Medical | Patient data security, HIPAA | HIPAA required |
| Dental | Patient records, imaging systems | HIPAA required |
| Law | Client confidentiality, case files | Attorney-client privilege |
| Accounting | Financial data, tax documents | GLBA, IRS requirements |
| Retail | POS systems, inventory | PCI-DSS for payments |

## Subject Line Best Practices

**High Open Rate Options:**
- [Company Name] + quick question
- Who handles your IT? (Curiosity gap)
- 2-minute question for you
- Local IT support question
- [First Name], quick thought for you

**Avoid:**
- "Free consultation" (too salesy)
- "We can save you money" (lead with value, not price)
- All caps or excessive punctuation
- Vague subjects like "Partnership opportunity"

---

**Pro Tip:** Always verify the contact name and company before sending. A quick LinkedIn search or call to the front desk to get the right person's name dramatically increases response rates.
