# 2-Week Follow-Up Sequence
## Tech & Electrical Services LLC - Cold Outreach Campaign

---

## Overview

**Goal:** Move cold prospects from awareness → interest → commitment  
**Timeline:** 14 days  
**Touch frequency:** 3 emails + 2 calls (avoid harassment)  
**Conversion metric:** Book 30-minute audit call

---

## WEEK 1: BUILD AWARENESS + CREDIBILITY

### DAY 1 (Monday) — INITIAL EMAIL

**Send time:** 9:00 AM (business hours)  
**Template:** Email #2 (Medium) — "Cyber risk + Infrastructure audit (free)"

- Personalize with prospect name
- Industry-specific compliance angle
- Free audit offer + credibility hook

**Action in CRM:** Mark as "Sent – Email 1"

---

### DAY 2 (Tuesday) — CALL #1 (Follow email)

**Time:** 10:00 AM or 2:00 PM (avoid lunch)  
**Duration:** 60 seconds max  
**Script:** Use 60-second cold call script

**Goals:**
- Confirm receipt of email (soft permission)
- Uncover one specific pain point
- Suggest a time for audit call
- If interested → book immediately

**Outcomes:**
- ✅ **BOOKED:** Move to "Scheduled – Waiting for call"
- ⏳ **MAYBE:** "Need to think about it" → Move to "Warm Lead – Follow next week"
- ❌ **NO:** Move to "Not interested – Keep in database 6 months"

**Action in CRM:** Log call notes, next action, and sentiment

---

### DAY 3 (Wednesday) — SECOND EMAIL (Objection killer)

**Send time:** 9:00 AM  
**Recipient filter:** People who didn't answer call or said "send me something"

**Subject:** Re: Your infrastructure (just found this)

**Body:**

Hi [NAME],

Tried to reach you yesterday—didn't catch you. No worries.

Figured I'd send over a quick resource that might be useful: [CHOOSE ONE BASED ON INDUSTRY]

**→ For Medical/Dental:**  
"HIPAA Breach Cost Calculator" or "2024 Ransomware trends in healthcare"

**→ For Restaurants:**  
"Why POS downtime costs more than you think" + electrical load case study

**→ For Auto Repair:**  
"Diagnostic network reliability checklist" (2-minute read)

**→ For Retail:**  
"Retail POS security gaps report"

Thought you might find it useful. If you want to chat about any of it, I'm around.

[IRVIN NAME]

**Attachment:** PDF resource (industry-specific, 2-3 pages, not a sales doc)

**Action in CRM:** Mark as "Sent – Email 2 (value-add)"

---

### DAY 4 (Thursday) — QUIET DAY

**Action:** None. Let them digest.

**Use this time to:**
- Prepare for any booked calls
- Pre-screen other prospects
- Update campaign metrics

---

**Send time:** 10:00 AM  
**Recipient filter:** No response to call or emails yet (warm leads only)

**Body:**

Hi [NAME],

I've got 2 openings left this week for free infrastructure audits—30 minutes, shows you what you're exposed to and where you could save money.

Thought I'd reach out again since I didn't hear back.

Are you free Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon? If not this week, next week works too.

Let me know.

[IRVIN NAME]  
Tech & Electrical Services LLC  
(951) 555-XXXX

**Action in CRM:** Mark as "Sent – Email 3 (urgency)"

---

## WEEK 2: PIVOT TO PHONE + CLOSE

### DAY 8 (Monday) — CALL #2 (Hard call)

**Send time:** 2:00 PM (after lunch, less likely to interrupt)  
**Duration:** 90 seconds max  
**Script variant:** Use objection handler framework

**Goals:**
- Get a yes or no (not a maybe)
- If yes → Book the call
- If no → Ask permission to follow up in 6 months
- Don't waste time on stalling

**What to say if they're avoiding:**

"Hey [NAME], I know I've reached out a couple times. Here's the deal—I've got solid reason to think you could save money on infrastructure costs. 30 minutes and you'll know if we're worth talking to or not. Can we do Tuesday afternoon?"

*[If still no]:*  
"Fair enough. Mind if I check back with you in a few months? Things change, and this might be more relevant down the road."

**Action in CRM:** Log outcome. If booked, move to "Scheduled – Call confirmed."

---

### DAY 9-10 (Tuesday-Wednesday) — EXECUTE BOOKED CALLS

**Duration:** 30 minutes (stick to it)  
**Call structure:**
1. Intro + rapport (2 min)
2. Ask about their current setup (5 min)
3. Identify top 2-3 pain points (5 min)
4. Explain what an audit would cover (5 min)
5. Soft close: "Want to move forward?" (2 min)
6. Next steps + calendar (4 min)

**Success outcome:** Schedule full project assessment or ongoing MSP engagement

**Action in CRM:** Move to "Prospect – Qualified" or "Client – Onboarding"

---

### DAY 12 (Friday) — EMAIL TO WARM LEADS (Breakup email)

**Send time:** 4:00 PM (lower chance of read, but that's ok)  
**Recipient filter:** No response to any outreach

**Subject:** Not a good fit (probably)

**Body:**

Hi [NAME],

I've reached out a few times—clearly now's not the right time.

No worries. I'll drop this. If something changes (new systems, frustration with current IT, cyber audit comes up), my info is below.

Good luck with [INDUSTRY]-specific thing related to their business.

[IRVIN NAME]  
Tech & Electrical Services LLC  
(951) 555-XXXX

**Action in CRM:** Move to "Unresponsive – Recycle in 6 months"

## Pro Tips for Execution

1. **Batch your calls.** Make all calls in a 2-hour window (less context switching)
2. **Personalize every email.** 2-3 details = not a template
3. **Log everything immediately.** Memory is bad; CRM is truth
4. **Move fast on yeses.** Book the audit call same day if possible
5. **Don't pursue nos forever.** Cycle them out; come back in 6 months
6. **Track by prospect type.** Which industries respond better? Double down there
7. **Use the value-add email as a differentiator.** Most people don't send real resources

## Recycling Strategy

**After 14 days, sort into buckets:**

1. **Booked / Engaged** → Move to ongoing pipeline
2. **Warm leads** → Email every 30 days with new resource + soft "let me know if things change"
3. **Unresponsive** → Recycle in 6 months (market changes, new pain points emerge)
4. **Hard no** → Keep in database, trigger re-contact on milestone events (new hire, expansion, tech news)
