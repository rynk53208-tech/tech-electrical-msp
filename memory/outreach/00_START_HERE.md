## Key Principles (Remember These)

✅ **Email first, call second** — Warm call = 10x better answer rate  
✅ **60 seconds, max** — Longer = more objections, more nos  
✅ **Credibility first, pitch second** — They need to trust you before they listen  
✅ **Specific offer** — "Free audit" beats "I'd like to talk"  
✅ **Log everything** — CRM is your source of truth  
✅ **Speed matters** — Book calls same day if they say yes
