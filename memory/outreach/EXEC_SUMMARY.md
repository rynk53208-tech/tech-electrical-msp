## 🔄 Execution Tips (Do This Right)

✅ **DO:**
- Send email 24 hours before calling (warm up the prospect)
- Use prospect's name 1-2 times per call
- Have specific pain point identified before pitching
- Book calls immediately (same day if they say yes)
- Log every interaction (CRM is your source of truth)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Cold call without email first (5% answer rate)
- Make calls outside 9am-5pm business hours
- Stay on phone >2 minutes on first call
- Follow up more than 3x (Email + 2 calls = enough)
- Send the same generic email to everyone
