- [ ] **Day 1 (Mon):** Send Email #2 (Medium template) to all 10 prospects
  - Personalize with name, company, industry-specific angle
  - Subject: "Cyber risk + Infrastructure audit (free)"

  - Aim for 6-7 of the 10 prospects
  - Log every outcome in CRM/spreadsheet

  - Target those who didn't answer or seemed hesitant

- [ ] **Day 5 (Fri):** Send Email #3 (urgency angle)

  - Position for project engagement

- [ ] **Day 12 (Fri):** Breakup email to unresponsive leads
  - Move them to 6-month recycle

## 💡 Key Success Principles

### Credibility Hooks (Use Immediately)
- Northrop Grumman cyber background
- Board-level hardware repair capability
- Rare IT + electrical combo
- Local to Temecula (same-day service)

### Industry-Specific Angles
- **Medical/Dental:** HIPAA compliance, ransomware risk, patient data liability
- **Retail:** POS security, breach costs, downtime = lost sales
- **Restaurant:** POS + electrical infrastructure, kitchen downtime = $1k+/hr lost
- **Auto Repair:** Diagnostic tool networking, uptime = customer retention, electrical load

### Pressure Points (Pick 1 per prospect)
- Compliance risk (medical/dental)
- Downtime cost (restaurant/auto)
- Data breach liability (all)
- Hardware replacement cost (all)
- Overpaying current IT support (all)

### Call-to-Action (Always Specific)
- "Free 30-minute audit ($500 value)"
- "Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon"
- "Shows you 2-3 specific risks"
- "No pitch, just clarity"

**Targeting 10 prospects:**
- 6-7 calls answered (Week 1)

## 🎯 Optimization (After First Campaign)

### Test Variables
1. **Subject lines** — Track open rates
2. **Call timing** — Best time to reach (9am vs 2pm?)
3. **Email template** — #1 vs #2 vs #3 response rate
4. **Industry vertical** — Which converts best?
5. **Offer type** — "Free audit" vs "Free 15-minute call"

### Quick Wins (Immediate Implementation)
- **Referral angle:** "Barney's (auto shop) recommended I reach out" = warm intro
- **Social proof:** "Working with [similar local business] on their infrastructure"
- **Urgency play:** "Q1 is when most cyber incidents spike" (timing-based)
- **Industry event hook:** "Just closed a major security audit in Temecula"

### Scaling Strategy
- Once 1 prospect is qualified: Use as case study reference
- Once 1 client signed: Use in email as social proof
- Automate email sequences in tool (HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.)
- Build referral loop (satisfied clients = warm intros)

## 📞 Do's & Don'ts

### ✅ DO
- Personalize every email (at least the first line)
- Call 24 hours after email (warm follow)
- Book calls immediately (same day if possible)
- Use prospect name 1-2 times per call
- Have specific pain point before pitching
- Take notes on every interaction

### ❌ DON'T
- Send the same email to everyone (looks like spam)
- Call without emailing first (cold blind call = 5% connect rate)
- Stay on phone >2 minutes on first call
- Promise things Irvin can't deliver
- Follow up more than 3x (email + 2 calls = enough)
- Give up on a prospect after 1 no (ask why, offer alternative)

## Questions to Ask Yourself Before Each Call

- Do I know this prospect's main pain point? (If not, research first)
- Did they get my email? (Ask: "Did you see my email yesterday?")
- What's the specific compliance risk for their industry?
- What's the downtime cost per hour for this business?
- Do I have a specific time to offer (not just "sometime")?
- Am I solving their problem or selling my service?

**Remember:** You're not selling tech support. You're preventing risk, reducing cost, eliminating downtime.
