## Security

### How secure is my data?
We implement enterprise-grade security:
- AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit
- Multi-factor authentication required
- Regular security patching
- SOC 2 compliant backup procedures

### What happens if there's a security breach?
1. We notify you immediately
2. Isolate affected systems
3. Contain and assess the threat
4. Work with your team on recovery
5. Document lessons learned

### Do you handle compliance (HIPAA, PCI, etc.)?
We can help configure systems to meet compliance standards. For formal audits or certification, we partner with specialized compliance firms.

## Backups & Recovery

### How often are backups run?
- **Daily:** Incremental backups (changes only)
- **Weekly:** Full system image backups
- **Offsite:** Replicated to secure cloud storage

### How long are backups retained?
- 30 days standard
- 90 days available (add-on)
- Extended retention for archived data

### Can I access my backups directly?
Yes, for OneDrive and SharePoint files. Full system restores are handled by our team to ensure proper procedures.

### What's your recovery time?
- **File restore:** 4 hours
- **System restore:** 8-24 hours (depending on size)
- **Disaster recovery:** Defined in your SLA

## VPN & Remote Work

### When do I need to use VPN?
Use VPN when:
- Accessing internal company resources
- Using remote desktop
- Accessing file servers
- On public/untrusted WiFi

**Not required for:** Email, Microsoft 365, web-based apps

### Why is my VPN slow?
- Check your home internet speed
- Try connecting to a different server
- Restart your computer and router
- Contact support if persistent
