## Usage Tips

1. **Always quantify** — use specific numbers (hours, dollars, percentages)
2. **Get a quote** — client testimonials add credibility
3. **Include photos** — before/after or team photos when possible
4. **Keep it scannable** — use bullet points and bold text
5. **Add a CTA** — end with a clear next step for prospects
