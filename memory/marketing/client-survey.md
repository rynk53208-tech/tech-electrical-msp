# Client Satisfaction Survey - Irvin's Tech Business

## Overview
This survey is designed to measure client satisfaction, identify improvement areas, and generate referrals. It should be sent after project completion or during regular check-ins.

---

## Part 1: Survey Questions

### Section A: Overall Experience (Required)
1. **How satisfied are you with the overall service provided?** (1-10 scale)
2. **Would you recommend our services to a friend or colleague?** (Yes/No/Maybe)
3. **What specific service did we provide?** (Cybersecurity / Software Development / MSP/MSSP / Computer Repair / Electrical / Other)

### Section B: Quality & Timeliness
4. **How would you rate the quality of work delivered?** (1-10 scale)
5. **Was the project completed within the agreed timeframe?** (Yes / No / N/A)
6. **How would you rate our communication throughout the project?** (1-10 scale)

### Section C: Value & Support
7. **Did you feel the pricing was fair for the value received?** (1-10 scale)
8. **How satisfied are you with our response time for questions or issues?** (1-10 scale)
9. **Did we meet or exceed your expectations?** (Exceeded / Met / Fell Short)

### Section D: Open Feedback
10. **What did we do well?**
    - [Free text field]
11. **What could we improve?**
    - [Free text field]
12. **Any additional comments?**
    - [Free text field]

### Section E: Referrals (The Closer)
13. **Do you know anyone who might benefit from our services?** (Yes / No)
    - If Yes: Name + Contact (optional)
14. **Would you be willing to provide a testimonial or reference?** (Yes / No / Ask me later)

---

## Part 2: How to Send

### Method 1: Email (Recommended)
- **Subject Line:** "Quick Feedback on Your Recent Service — [Your Name]"
- **Body:** Keep it short (3-4 sentences max)
- **Link:** Use a Google Form, Typeform, or direct survey link
- **Timing:** Send 2-3 days after project completion

**Sample Email:**
> Hi [Client Name],
> 
> Thanks for trusting us with [project type]. I'd love to get your quick feedback — it only takes 2 minutes and helps us improve.
> 
> [Survey Link]
> 
> Thanks,  
> Irvin

### Method 2: Text Message (For Quick Jobs)
- Keep it casual and direct
- "Hey [Name], hope everything's working well! Quick 2-min survey? [Link] — really appreciate your feedback!"

### Method 3: In-Person
- For big projects, ask face-to-face: "While I have you — mind filling out a quick feedback form? Helps us get better."

### Tools to Use
- **Google Forms** (Free, easy, integrates with Sheets)
- **Typeform** (Cleaner UI, free tier available)
- **JotForm** (Good for embedded forms)

---

## Part 3: Follow-Up Process

### Timeline
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 0 | Send survey after project completion |
| Day 3 | Send gentle reminder if no response |
| Day 7 | Final follow-up (last attempt) |
| Day 14 | Log results, mark as "no response" if nada |

### Response Handling
- **Score 9-10:** Send thank you, ask for referral/testimonial within 48 hours
- **Score 7-8:** Send thank you, ask what could improve
- **Score Below 7:** Reach out personally (call, not email), understand the issue, offer to make it right

### Automated Follow-Up (Optional)
Set up email templates for each scenario so you're not writing from scratch.

---

## Part 4: Using Results for Referrals

### The Referral Pipeline
1. **Identify:** Anyone who scores 8+ is a potential referral source
2. **Ask:** "Know anyone who needs [service]?" — timing: right after they say they had a good experience
3. **Incentivize:** Consider a discount or thank-you for successful referrals ($50-$100 credit works well)

### Track Everything
Create a simple spreadsheet or use a CRM:
- Client Name
- Service Provided
- Satisfaction Score
- Referral Given? (Y/N)
- Referral Converted? (Y/N)

### Use Testimonials
- Collect quotes from 9-10 scores
- Put on website, proposal templates, Google Business profile
- Video testimonials = gold for credibility

### Report Quarterly
Review survey data monthly:
- Average satisfaction score
- Common complaints (fix these!)
- Referral conversion rate
- Number of new clients from referrals
