## The Program: "Know Someone?"

### Rewards

| For You (Referrer) | For Them (New Client) |
|---|---|
| **$100 cash** or **$150 service credit** | **20% off first job** (max $200 value) |

> **Why this works:** Cash is simple and motivating. Service credit keeps them loyal. 20% off is attractive without being desperate.

---

### How It Works (3 Steps)

1. **You refer someone** — Just tell them to mention your name or share your unique link/code
2. **They book and pay** — New client completes their first paid service
3. **You both get rewarded** — You receive your reward within 7 days; they get their discount applied to the invoice

**No limits:** Refer as many as you want. Each referral = reward.

---

### What New Clients Get

- 20% off their first service (labor + parts, up to $200)
- Free consultation/estimate ($75 value, normally)
- Priority scheduling (within 48 hours)

## How to Promote

| Channel | Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Send to past clients quarterly | Every 3 months |
| **Social** | Post launch + monthly reminder | 1x/month |
| **Invoicing** | Add referral blurb to invoice footer | Always |
| **Business cards** | Print on back or include slip | Hand out always |
| **On completion** | Ask for referral when job wraps | Every job |
