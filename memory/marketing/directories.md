# Local Business Directories - Quick Start Guide

Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services - Temecula, CA

---

## 1. Google Business Profile
**Cost:** FREE

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Click "Manage now" → Sign in with Gmail
3. Enter business name (e.g., "Irvin Tech Services" or your registered name)
4. Enter address (can hide if home-based)
5. Select category: "IT Support" + "Electrician" (both)
6. Add service area: Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore
7. Add phone number & website
8. Verify by postcard (5-7 days) or phone/video

**Why:** #1 local search. Shows in Google Maps. Critical for local credibility.

---

## 2. Yelp
**Cost:** Free to list, Paid ads optional ($0-$300+/mo)

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [yelp.com/business](https://www.yelp.com/business)
2. Click "Claim Your Business"
3. Search your business name
4. Verify ownership (phone or email)
5. Fill: hours, services, photos, description
6. Upgrade to Yelp Ads later if needed

**Why:** High intent for local services. Customers read reviews before calling.

---

## 3. BBB (Better Business Bureau)
**Cost:** Free to list, Accreditation $400-$1,200/year (optional)

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [bbb.org](https://www.bbb.org)
2. Click "Claim Your Business"
3. Search and select your business
4. Verify via email/phone
5. Complete profile: services, hours, licensing
6. Optional: Apply for accreditation

**Why:** Credibility signal for B2B and quality-conscious customers. Not required but helps.

---

## 4. Angi (formerly Angie's List)
**Cost:** Free to list, Lead fees $15-$50/lead

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [angi.com/contractor](https://www.angi.com/contractor/signup)
2. Create account with business email
3. Enter: name, address, services, licenses
4. Select membership: Free or Angi's List (paid membership program)
5. Pay per lead when customers request quotes

**Why:** Homeowners trust Angi. Strong for residential IT/electrical work.

---

## 5. Thumbtack
**Cost:** Free to list, Pay per lead $15-$100/lead

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [thumbtack.com](https://www.thumbtack.com)
2. Click "Pro" → "Join as a Pro"
3. Add business info, specialties, service area
4. Set your lead preferences and budget
5. Pay for leads when customers match with you

**Why:** Customers actively request quotes. Good for quick jobs.

---

## 6. HomeAdvisor
**Cost:** Free to list, Lead fees $15-$75/lead

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [homeadvisor.com](https://www.homeadvisor.com)
2. Click "Join as a Pro"
3. Enter business details, licenses, service area
4. Verify identity
5. Set lead spending budget

**Why:** Major national network. Good for consistent leads.

---

## 7. Craigslist
**Cost:** FREE (some categories paid)

**Quick Start:**
1. Go to [craigslist.org](https://craigslist.org)
2. Select your city (Temecula)
3. Post in "Computer Services" or "Electrical"
4. Use strong title, clear description, photos
5. Refresh every 48-72 hours to stay top

**Why:** Free, local, fast. Good for quick/one-off jobs. Low barrier.

---

## Priority Recommendation

| Priority | Platform | Cost | Effort |
|----------|----------|------|--------|
| 1 | Google Business Profile | FREE | High (verify) |
| 2 | Yelp | FREE | Medium |
| 3 | BBB | FREE | Low |
| 4 | Angi | Free + lead fees | Medium |
| 5 | Thumbtack | Lead fees | Low |
| 6 | HomeAdvisor | Lead fees | Low |
| 7 | Craigslist | FREE | Low (repost often) |

**Start with #1-3 (all free).** Add lead-gen platforms (#4-6) when you have bandwidth to handle inquiries. Use Craigslist for quick fills.

## Business Info to Prepare

Gather before you start:
- [ ] Business name ( DBA vs LLC)
- [ ] Address (can use P.O. Box or hide)
- [ ] Phone number (dedicated business line)
- [ ] Website URL
- [ ] Service area cities
- [ ] Services list (IT support, electrical, repairs, etc.)
- [ ] License numbers (C-10 electrical, CompTIA, etc.)
- [ ] Photos/logo
- [ ] Hours of operation
