# Video Content Strategy for Irvin's Tech Business

## 1. Types of Videos to Make

### Educational/Authority Content
- **"How It Works" Explanations** — Break down cybersecurity concepts, networking, cloud infrastructure for potential clients
- **Tutorial Snippets** — Quick 60-90 second tips (e.g., "3 signs your computer is compromised")
- **Myth Busting** — "Anti-virus is enough" vs reality, "my business is too small for hackers"

### Proof/Portfolio Content
- **Before & After** — Hardware repair transformations, network upgrade progressions
- **Case Study Shorts** — "Saved this business $X" or "Recovered lost data"
- **Behind the Scenes** — Micro soldering workflow, custom PC build time-lapses

### Lead-Focused Content
- **Service Spotlights** — Quick overviews of what you offer (MSP, MSSP, custom builds, electrical)
- **Client Testimonials** — Short quotes from satisfied customers
- **"What To Look For" Guides** — How to vet an IT provider, red flags in cybersecurity proposals

### Personality/Viral Potential
- **Day in the Life** — Show the variety of work (board repair to network config)
- **Tech Reactions** — React to industry news, new hardware, breach stories
- **"I Fixed It" Wins** — Satisfying repair reveals

## 4. Posting Strategy

### Platform Priorities
1. **YouTube Shorts** — Primary discovery platform, 30-60 sec educational clips
2. **TikTok** — Viral potential, younger demographic, same content repurposed
3. **Instagram Reels** — Local audience, business credibility
4. **LinkedIn** — B2B authority, professional services pitch
5. **Facebook** — Local community groups, older demographic

### Posting Cadence
- **Week 1-4:** 3x/week — Test what resonates, repurpose across platforms
- **Week 5+:** 5x/week — Once you find winners, scale those formats

### Content Mix (Weekly)
- 2 Educational (tips, how-tos)
- 1 Proof/Portfolio (builds, repairs, case studies)
- 1 Industry News/Reaction
- 1 Personality/Viral

### Best Times to Post (Local Audience - PDT)
- **TikTok/Reels:** 6-8 PM weekday evenings
- **YouTube Shorts:** 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM
- **LinkedIn:** 7-9 AM (Tuesday-Thursday)

### Hashtag Strategy
- Regional: #Temecula #InlandEmpire #SoCal
- Industry: #Cybersecurity #MSP #ITSupport #SmallBusiness #TechTips
- Niche: #ComputerRepair #DataRecovery #MicroSoldering #NetworkSecurity

### Repurposing Workflow
1. Record one long-form explanation (3-5 min)
2. Chop into 3-5 Shorts/Reels
3. Extract audio for podcast or LinkedIn carousel
4. Screenshot key frames for Instagram posts

### Call-to-Action
- Always end with soft CTA: "Need help with X? DM us or comment below"
- Link in bio to booking/contact page
