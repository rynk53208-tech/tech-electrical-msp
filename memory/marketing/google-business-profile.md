# Google Business Profile Setup Guide
## For Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services Business

**Time needed:** ~30 minutes  
**Goal:** Claim, optimize, and start getting found in local searches

---

## Step 1: Claim Your Listing (~5 min)

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with a Google account (create one if you don't have one dedicated to your business)
2. Search for your business name — if it exists, claim it. If not, create a new listing.
3. Enter your business info:
   - **Business name:** Use your official business name (no keywords stuffing)
   - **Business category:** See categories section below
   - **Address:** Physical address (if home-based, you can hide the exact address)
   - **Service area:** Temecula, CA + surrounding areas
   - **Phone:** Use a dedicated business number (not your personal cell if possible)
   - **Website:** Your website URL

4. Verify by postcard or phone — postcard takes 5-7 days, phone is instant

---

## Step 2: Choose the Right Categories

**Primary category** (pick ONE — this is most important):
- **"Computer Repair Service"** (best for general repairs)
- OR **"IT Support"** (if focused on business clients)
- OR **"Electrician"** (for electrical services)

**Secondary categories** (add up to 9):
- Cybersecurity Company
- Computer Store
- Computer Hardware wholesaler (if you sell equipment)
- Security System Supplier (if you do security installs)
- Electronics Repair Shop
- Network Equipment Supplier
- Web Hosting Company (if you offer hosting)
- Data Recovery Service
- Smart Home Automation Service

**Pro tip:** Choose categories that match what you actually want to get calls for. Don't add "Electrician" if you only do IT work.

---

## Step 3: Write Your Business Description (~3 min)

**Keep it under 750 characters** (Google truncates after this anyway).

**Template:**
> [Business Name] provides expert [main services] in Temecula and surrounding areas. With [X] years of experience, we specialize in [top 3 services]. Our services include [list 5-6 key offerings]. We combine enterprise-level expertise with personalized service. Licensed, insured, and committed to customer satisfaction. Call today for a free consultation.

**For Irvin's business, something like:**
> Avitia Tech Services provides expert IT support, computer repair, and cybersecurity solutions in Temecula, CA. Specializing in board-level repair, custom builds, MSP/MSSP services, and electrical/tech integration. Former Northrop Grumman cyber engineer bringing enterprise-grade security to local businesses and homeowners. Free consultations available.

## Step 4: Add Photos (~5 min)

Photos make you 2x more likely to get directions clicks and 35% more likely to get clicks to your website.

**Must-have photos:**

| Photo Type | What to Upload |
|------------|----------------|
| **Logo** | Your business logo (transparent background preferred) |
| **Cover photo** | 16:9 ratio — show your team, van, or a happy customer setup |
| **Interior** | Clean, professional shot of your workspace/shop |
| **Team** | You at work (professional but approachable) |
| **Services** | Photos of repairs, builds, or equipment you're working on |
| **Before/after** | Screen replacement, board repair, etc. |

**Photo tips:**
- Use natural lighting when possible
- No stock photos — Google penalizes fake images
- Upload at least 5-10 photos initially
- Update seasonally (show holiday availability, etc.)

---

## Step 5: Get Reviews (~10 min)

Reviews are #1 factor for local pack ranking. Aim for 10+ reviews in first month.

**How to ask:**
1. Complete a job → send follow-up text/email same day
2. Make it easy: "Would you mind leaving a quick review? Here's the link: [your GBP review link]"
3. Use Google's built-in review link: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[YOUR_PLACE_ID]`

**What to do with negative reviews:**
- Never argue publicly
- Respond professionally: "We're sorry to hear this. Please call us at [number] so we can make it right."

**Pro tips:**
- Set up a QR code on your invoices/cards that goes straight to review page
- Add "Review us on Google" to your email signature
- Respond to EVERY review (shows you care)

## Step 6: Local SEO Best Practices (~5 min)

### Fill out EVERYTHING
Google rewards complete profiles. Add:
- Business hours (including holiday hours)
- Holiday special hours
- Services menu (list each service with short description)
- Attributes: "LGBTQ+ friendly", "Veterans welcome", "Women-owned", etc.

### Use Posts (do this monthly)
Google Business Posts show up in search. Post about:
- New services
- Seasonal promotions
- Community involvement
- Before/after project highlights

**Post template:**
- Title: Short, catchy
- Body: 150-200 words max
- Call to action: "Book now", "Call us", "Learn more"
- Photo: Always include one

### Build citations
Ensure your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) is consistent across:
- Yelp
- Facebook
- BBB
- Any local directories

**Directory checklist:**
- [ ] Yelp
- [ ] Facebook Business
- [ ] Better Business Bureau
- [ ] Angi
- [ ] Thumbtack
- [ ] Nextdoor
- [ ] Local chamber of commerce

### Website integration
Add your Google Business profile to your website:
- Embed Google Map
- Add "Google Review" button
- Link from website footer to review page
