# Local Advertising Guide for Irvin's Tech Business

> Quick Reference: Google Ads & Facebook/Instagram for Temecula, CA

---

## 1. Google Ads Setup for Local IT

### Campaign Structure

| Campaign Type | Purpose | Recommended |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **Local Search** | Capture high-intent local searches | ✅ Primary |
| **Performance Max** | Brand awareness & retargeting | ✅ Secondary |
| **Display** | Retarget website visitors | Optional |

### Local Campaign Setup

1. **Business Profile**: Verify Google Business Profile (GBP) first — critical for local pack
2. **Location Targeting**: Temecula, CA + 15-25 mile radius
3. **Ad Extensions** (MUST have):
   - Location extension (link to GBP)
   - Call extension
   - Sitelinks (Services, About, Contact)
   - Callout extensions (24/7 Support, On-site Repairs, Free Estimates)

### Recommended Campaign Settings

- **Bidding**: Maximize Conversions (with conversion value) or Target CPA
- **Budget**: Start $30-50/day, scale based on results
- **Conversion Tracking**: Calls + Form submits + Clicks to Directions

## 2. Facebook/Instagram for Local

### Platform Strategy

| Platform | Best For | Format |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Facebook** | Local awareness, service articles | Carousel, Single image |
| **Instagram** | Behind-the-scenes, trust building | Reels, Stories, Single image |

### Audience Targeting (Temecula, CA)

**Core Audiences:**
- Residents within 25 miles of Temecula
- Interests: "Computer Repair", "IT Support", "Cybersecurity", "Electrician", "Small Business"
- Age: 25-65 (broad enough for B2B + residential)

**Lookalike Audiences:**
- Create from: Website visitors, Customer list (if available)
- 1% lookalike from your best customers

### Retargeting Audiences
- Website visitors (pixel)
- Engaged followers (28-day)
- Customer list (existing clients)

### Ad Formats Recommended
1. **Carousel** — Show 3-4 services (Cybersecurity, Repairs, MSP, Electrical)
2. **Single Image** — Problem/solution for common pain points
3. **Reels** — Quick tips, before/after board repairs, team intro

## 3. Budget Allocation

### Starting Budget: $500-1,000/month

| Channel | % of Budget | Monthly | Daily (avg) |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------------|
| **Google Ads** | 60% | $300-600 | $10-20 |
| **Facebook/IG** | 40% | $200-400 | $7-14 |

### Budget Scaling Path

| Phase | Spend | Goal |
|-------|-------|------|
| **Test** (Weeks 1-2) | $15-25/day combined | Find winning ads |
| **Scale** (Weeks 3-6) | $30-50/day combined | Double winners |
| **Optimize** (Month 2+) | Adjust based on CPA | Profitability |

### Key Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Google Ads CPA | Under $50 (lead) |
| FB/IG Cost per Result | Under $1.00 (lead) |
| Lead Value | Minimum $100 (to cover ad spend + profit) |

## 4. Keywords to Target

### Google Ads - Search

**High Intent (Capture):**
```
[city] computer repair
[city] IT support
[city] managed IT services
[city] cybersecurity services
[city] network security
[city] electrician near me
[city] circuit breaker repair
[city] electrical panel upgrade
best [city] IT company
[data recovery] [city]
```

**Service-Specific:**
```
microsoldering [city]
board level repair [city]
custom PC build [city]
virus removal [city]
business IT support [city]
server setup [city]
```

**Long-tail (Lower cost, higher intent):**
```
emergency computer repair [city]
same day IT support [city]
Affordable cybersecurity for small business
IT consultant [city] near me
```

### Match Types

| Keyword Type | Match Type | Use Case |
|--------------|------------|----------|
| Brand + Service | Exact | Core terms |
| [city] + service | Phrase | Local capture |
| "best IT support near me" | Exact | High intent |
| Computer repair + near me | Broad | Discovery |

### Negative Keywords (Block)
- free, job, career, salary, intern, parts only, wholesale, used
