# Local SEO Strategy for Irvin's IT Services

A practical guide to ranking higher in local searches and getting more calls.

---

## 1. Local SEO Tips

### Claim Your Google Business Profile
Priority #1. If you haven't already, claim and verify your GBP at business.google.com. This is the foundation of local SEO.

### NAP Consistency
Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be identical everywhere:
- Website
- Google Business Profile
- Yelp, BBB, Thumbtack, etc.
- Social media profiles

Any mismatch hurts your ranking. Use exactly: **Irvin's Tech Services** (or your business name), **Temecula, CA**, and one phone number.

### Get Reviews (Lots of Them)
- Ask every happy customer for a Google review
- Respond to ALL reviews (good and bad) within 48 hours
- Make review requests easy — send a direct link via text/email after job completion

### Local Citations
Get listed on:
- Yelp
- Better Business Bureau (BBB)
- Angi / Thumbtack
- Nextdoor
- Local chamber of commerce
- Riverside County business directories

### Geotarget Your Pages
If you have multiple service pages, include Temecula, Riverside County, Murrieta, Menifee naturally in:
- Page titles
- Headers
- First 100 words of content
- Image alt text

### Mobile-First Design
Most local searches happen on phones. Your site must:
- Load under 3 seconds
- Have clickable phone numbers
- Display a map with your location
- Have large, easy-to-tap buttons

### Service Area Pages
Create separate pages for each city you serve:
- `/computer-repair-temecula`
- `/cybersecurity-riverside-county`
- `/it-support-murrieta`

Each page targets one city + one service.

## 3. Google Business Profile Optimization

### Complete Every Field
- Business name (exact — no keywords stuffed in)
- Address (verify via postcard)
- Phone number (local area code — 951 works for Temecula)
- Website URL
- Hours (including holiday hours)
- Service areas (list cities you cover)

### Choose the Right Categories
Primary: **IT Consultant** or **Computer Repair Service**
Secondary:
- Network Administrator
- Computer Security Service
- Computer Store
- Electronics Repair

### Add Services List
List every service with brief descriptions:
- Managed IT Services
- Computer Repair & Diagnostics
- Cybersecurity & Network Security
- Custom PC Builds
- On-Site Support
- Data Recovery
- Board-Level Micro Soldering Repair

### Post Regularly
- Use the "Updates" feature weekly
- Post about: new services, promotions, industry tips, before/after repairs
- Add photos with every post

### Add Photos
- Logo (profile photo)
- Team photo (you at work)
- Office/shop exterior and interior
- Before/after repair shots
- Equipment/tools photos
- Keep them high-quality but not over-edited

### Respond to Reviews
Template for positive reviews:
> "Thanks [Name]! Great working with you. Glad we could get your [issue] resolved quickly. For any future tech needs, call us at [number]!"

Template for negative reviews:
> "We're sorry to hear about your experience. We'd like to make this right. Please call us at [number] so we can address this directly."

## 4. On-Page SEO Basics

### Title Tags (Every Page)
Format: **Primary Keyword | Business Name**
Example: `Custom PC Builds Temecula | Irvin's Tech Services`

- Keep under 60 characters
- Include city where possible
- One H1 per page only

### Meta Descriptions
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword + call to action
- Example: "Expert computer repair in Temecula. Same-day service, Northrop Grumman-certified technicians. Call now for free diagnostics."

### Header Hierarchy
```
H1: Computer Repair Temecula
  H2: Same-Day PC Repair Services
  H2: Our Repair Process
    H3: Free Diagnostics
    H3: Fast Turnaround
  H2: Why Choose Us for Computer Repair
```

### Content Structure
- First 100 words must contain your primary keyword
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Include bullet points and numbered lists
- Add 1-2 images with keyword-rich alt text

### Schema Markup
Add LocalBusiness schema to your homepage. Include:
- Name, address, phone
- Opening hours
- Geo coordinates
- Service areas
- Reviews aggregate

### Internal Linking
- Link to service pages from homepage
- Link related services to each other
- Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")

### Page Speed
- Compress images (use WebP)
- Minimize JavaScript
- Use a fast host (Cloudflare CDN helps)
- Target: under 3 seconds load time

### URL Structure
Keep URLs short and descriptive:
- `yoursite.com/computer-repair-temecula` ✓
- `yoursite.com/services/temecula/computer-repair` ✓
- `yoursite.com/p=123` ✗
