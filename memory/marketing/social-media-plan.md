# Social Media Marketing Plan
## Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services - Temecula, CA

---

## 1. Which Platforms

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| **LinkedIn** | #1 | B2B goldmine. Northrop Grumman cred = instant authority. Target IT managers, SMB owners, CTOs needing MSP/MSSP services |
| **Nextdoor** | #2 | Hyper-local. Homeowners & small businesses in Temecula/Murrieta/Menifee need computer repair, electrical, IT support |
| **Facebook** | #3 | Local business page + community groups. Good for word-of-mouth referrals |
| **Instagram** | #4 | Visual proof. Before/after hardware repairs, custom builds, wiring jobs. Lower priority but easy to maintain |
| **YouTube Shorts** | #5 | Quick tech tips, repair demos. Long-term authority play |

**Skip for now:** TikTok (too general), Twitter/X (not local intent), Reddit (low conversion for services)

---

## 2. Content Types

### LinkedIn (B2B Focus)
- **Case studies** — "How I secured a local company's network" (anonymized)
- **Industry insights** — Cybersecurity trends, ransomware warnings
- **Behind-the-scenes** — Explaining DevOps/Cloud architecture simply
- **Service announcements** — New offerings, certifications
- **Employee/staff spotlights** — If he hires, show the team

### Nextdoor (Local Residential/SMB)
- **Quick tips** — "3 signs your computer needs repair"
- **Seasonal reminders** — "Backup your data before the holidays"
- **Service promotions** — First-time customer discounts
- **Community involvement** — Sponsoring local events, supporting small biz

### Facebook
- **Same as Nextdoor** — Repurpose content
- **Customer testimonials** — Screenshots, short video reviews
- **Photo posts** — Completed jobs, custom PC builds

### Instagram
- **Before/after** — Water-damaged boards, dead laptops revived
- **Custom builds** — Showcase builds in progress
- **Time-lapses** — Soldering repairs, installations
- **Carousel posts** — "5 signs you need an electrician"

### YouTube Shorts
- **"Did you know?"** — Quick tech facts
- **Repair quick cuts** — 30-sec repair demos
- **Tool unboxing** — New equipment, soldering stations

## 3. Posting Frequency

| Platform | Frequency | Best Times |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| LinkedIn | **3x/week** | Tue-Thu, 8-10am |
| Nextdoor | **2-3x/week** | Mon & Wed evenings, 7-9pm |
| Facebook | **2x/week** | Tue & Thu, 6-8pm |
| Instagram | **3-4x/week** | Mon, Wed, Fri, 12-2pm |
| YouTube Shorts | **1-2x/week** | Weekends preferred |

**Total weekly content burden:** ~10-12 posts max. Batch-create on Sunday, schedule via Meta Business Suite + Later/Buffer.
