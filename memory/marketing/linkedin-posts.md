## Reusable Content Pieces

### 1. "Red Flag Checklist" (Lead Magnet)
```
🔴 Your IT provider...
- Can't explain what they're doing in plain English
- Bills by the hour for every little fix
- Dodges security questions
- Doesn't offer written documentation
- Uses offshore support
- Has no references from businesses like yours
```

Post format: Share this checklist with a short intro about what good IT support actually looks like.

### 2. "Before You Hire An MSP" (Guide/Value Add)
```
Questions to ask before signing with an MSP:
1. Who actually works on my account? (Name, not just "our team")
2. What's your response time guarantee?
3. Do you do security audits? How often?
4. What happens if there's a breach? What's your incident response?
5. Can you do electrical/low-voltage work in-house?
6. Can you fix hardware, or just replace it?
7. References from businesses my size?

The right MSP should welcome these questions.
```

Post format: Position this as a free resource. Offer to review their current MSP contract for free.
