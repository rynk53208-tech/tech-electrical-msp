# LinkedIn 30-Day Content Calendar

## Week 1: Introduction & Authority
- **Day 1:** "I spent 10 years at Northrop Grumman defending against nation-state hackers. Now I bring that same level of security to small businesses in Temecula."
- **Day 2:** Photo of your workstation/toolbench. "Here's my mobile office. I come to you."
- **Day 3:** Tip: "3 signs your computer is about to fail"
- **Day 4:** Case study intro: "Saved a local business $3,000 yesterday..."
- **Day 5:** "What I do + who I do it for"

## Week 2: Education & Value
- **Day 6:** "Why replace it when it can be fixed?" (board-level repair)
- **Day 7:** Security tip: "Password mistake #1"
- **Day 8:** Client testimonial
- **Day 9:** "The IT+Electrical combo - one invoice, twice the value"
- **Day 10:** Behind the scenes: micro-soldering equipment

## Week 3: Social Proof
- **Day 11:** Before/after repair photo
- **Day 12:** "What my competitors can't do"
- **Day 13:** Security warning: ransomware stats
- **Day 14:** Referral thank-you post
- **Day 15:** Q&A: "Ask me anything about IT"

## Week 4: Conversion
- **Day 16:** "Free security assessment" offer
- **Day 17:** Process post: "How I work"
- **Day 18:** Local connection: "Love working in Temecula"
- **Day 19:** Testimonial
- **Day 20:** Call to action: "Need IT help? Let's talk"
- **Day 21-30:** Repeat best-performing posts

## Posting Tips
- Best times: 8-10 AM PT, 12-1 PM PT
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Add a clear CTA
- Engage with comments within 1 hour
