# Irvin's Tech Business — Pitch Deck

> "What do you do?"

---

## 🗣️ 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> **"I run a tech and electrical services company. We handle cybersecurity, software development, and managed IT services—but what makes us different is we're one of the few shops that can actually do board-level hardware repair in-house. Most MSPs have to send your broken hardware out or just replace it. We fix it. Combined with my background in cyber defense at Northrop Grumman, we bring enterprise-level security to small and mid-size businesses—without the enterprise price tag."**

*That's 30 seconds. Punchy. Differentiator front-loaded.*

---

## 🗣️ 2-Minute Version

> **"I run a full-service tech company that covers a few things most IT shops can't touch:**
>
> **First, cybersecurity and compliance** — I came from Northrop Grumman doing cyber defense work, so we're not guessing. We build security the way the big boys do.
>
> **Second, software development and DevOps** — custom apps, automation, cloud infrastructure. If it needs to be built or deployed, we handle it.
>
> **Third — and this is our secret weapon — board-level hardware repair.** Most MSPs when your laptop or device dies, they just replace it. We can actually troubleshoot and fix the board itself. Micro soldering, component-level repair. We've saved clients thousands by fixing what others would toss.
>
> **And we do electrical work too, since we have that license. Low-voltage, networking cable runs, commercial and residential.**
>
> **Bottom line: we're a rare combo. Most companies have to hire an IT firm, a repair shop, and an electrician separately. We do all three — with enterprise-level credentials but local pricing."**

*That's ~2 minutes. Covers what, why us, and closes with the key differentiator.*
