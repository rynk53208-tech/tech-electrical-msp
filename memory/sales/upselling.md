# Upselling Strategy for Irvin's Tech Business

**Goal:** Generate more revenue from existing clients through strategic upsells.

---

## 1. How to Identify Upsell Opportunities

### Client Audit Triggers
- **Contract renewal approaching** — natural pause to reassess needs
- **Business growth signals** — hired new staff, opened new location, launched new product
- **Current solution gaps** — client mentions slow systems, security concerns, downtime
- **Industry compliance changes** — HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC2 updates
- **Competitive pressure** — client mentions a competitor offering "more"

### Service Usage Patterns
- Client only has basic support but has >10 employees → MSP upgrade opportunity
- Using consumer-grade equipment → Pro hardware/monitoring pitch
- No documented backup solution → Disaster recovery conversation
- Single-location business → Multi-site monitoring opportunity

### Conversation Leads
- "IT guy retired" / "We lost our sysadmin"
- "We had a close call with ransomware"
- "Our system is getting slow"
- "We need to comply with [X] for a new client contract"

## 2. What Services to Upsell

### Priority Upsells (Highest ROI)

| Current Service | Upsell To | Why It Works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| Break-fix / hourly | **MSP retainer** | Predictable revenue, higher margins |
| Basic antivirus | **Managed detection & response (MDR)** | Ransomware is top fear |
| No backup | **Cloud backup + disaster recovery** | Cheap to deliver, easy to sell |
| Consumer routers | **Enterprise networking + monitoring** | Visible improvement |
| No monitoring | **24/7 proactive monitoring** | Prevents emergencies = trust builder |

### Security Upsells (Most In-Demand)
- **Endpoint detection & response (EDR)** — replace basic AV
- **Email security / phishing training** — almost everyone needs this
- **Vulnerability scanning** — compliance driver
- **Security assessments** — billable audit + upsell pipeline

### Backup & Recovery Upsells
- **Offsite/cloud backup** — "What happens if your building burns down?"
- **Disaster recovery as a service (DRaaS)** — for critical systems
- **Backup testing/documentation** — prove the backup actually works

### Monitoring Upsells
- **24/7 network monitoring** — catch issues before they become outages
- **Performance dashboards** — show them what they're getting
- **Log management** — for compliance and incident response

## 3. Timing for Upsells

### Best Timing Windows
1. **During contract renewal (60-90 days before)** — they're already evaluating options
2. **After resolving an incident** — "Glad we fixed that. Here's how to prevent it."
3. **Q4 budget season** — businesses planning next year's spend
4. **After client news** (hiring, expansion, new office) — needs change
5. **Annual business review** — schedule this if you don't have it

### Avoid
- Right after onboarding (let them breathe)
- During their active crisis (fix first, upsell later)
- End of month when they're stressed about payroll

## 4. Scripts for Upsell Conversations

### Opening: The Annual Review
> "Hey [Name], I wanted to schedule our annual tech review. Things change fast — let's make sure you're not paying for stuff you don't need or missing something that could save you money / keep you safe."

### Security Upsell (Post-Incident)
> "We got lucky this time. But here's what a proper security setup would have done: [specific solution]. It's about [X]% of what that incident cost you. Want me to put together a quote?"

### Backup Upsell (The Fear Close)
> "If you lost everything tomorrow — customer data, financial records, your work orders — how long could you survive? Most businesses I talk to say 24-48 hours. We can set you up with automated cloud backup for [price] so that never happens."

### MSP Upsell (From Hourly)
> "You're on hourly support, which means we're both reacting. At your current usage, you'd save money on a retainer — plus you get proactive monitoring, priority response, and I can budget my time better. Win-win."

### Compliance Upsell
> "Since you're going after [healthcare/finance/government] contracts, you'll need [SOC2/HIPAA/PCI] compliance. I can handle that for you — it's billable, it's repeatable, and it opens doors you're currently locked out of."

### The Soft Close
> "This isn't about selling you stuff. It's about making sure you're not leaving money on the table or exposed to risks you don't know about. Can I put together a quick options sheet?"

## 5. Quick Wins

- **Create a "security scorecard"** — one-page audit you can email to any client. Free value, leads to paid fixes.
- **Set calendar reminders** for contract renewal dates on all clients
- **Package monitoring** as a low-cost add-on ("For $50/mo, I'll monitor your server and alert you before it dies")
- **Document everything** — every upsell conversation is content for case studies and testimonials
