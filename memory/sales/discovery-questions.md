# Sales Discovery Call Questions

## Irvin Avitia — Tech & Electrical Services

Use these questions to qualify prospects and uncover opportunities. Match the service category to the questions that matter most.

---

## 1. Questions to Ask Prospects (Qualification)

**General:**
- "What brings you in today?" / "What made you start looking for help?"
- "How did you hear about us?" (tracks marketing)
- "Is this something you're looking to solve right now, or more research?"

**Business Context:**
- "What's your role, and who else is involved in this decision?"
- "How big is your company/organization?"
- "What's your industry?"
- "Do you have an IT team already, or are you handling things internally?"

**Technical Scope:**
- "What are you currently using for [cybersecurity / IT support / software]?"
- "Have you worked with a managed services provider before?"
- "What's your current setup — in-house, cloud, hybrid?"

---

## 2. Questions to Identify Pain Points

**Cybersecurity & Compliance:**
- "Have you had any security incidents or breaches?"
- "Have you done a security assessment or penetration test before?"
- "What does your current incident response plan look like?"
- "Are you dealing with any compliance requirements (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, NIST, etc.)?"
- "How do you currently handle employee security training?"
- "What keeps you up at night regarding your security?"

**IT Support & Infrastructure:**
- "What's your biggest frustration with your current IT support?"
- "How often are you dealing with downtime or technical issues?"
- "Are you experiencing slow systems, frequent outages, or data loss?"
- "How are you handling backups and disaster recovery?"
- "What's your process for updating and patching systems?"

**Software Development:**
- "What processes or tools are you using that feel outdated or limiting?"
- "Is there something you wish your software could do but it doesn't?"
- "How are you handling [specific function — automation, reporting, etc.]?"
- "What would you do differently if you had unlimited resources?"

**Hardware Repair & Custom Builds:**
- "What hardware issues are you running into?"
- "Is equipment failing more often than it should?"
- "Do you have machines that are slow, outdated, or needing replacement?"

**Electrical:**
- "Are you dealing with any electrical issues — outlets, wiring, panel upgrades?"
- "Do you need installations, repairs, or inspections?"

---

## 3. Questions About Budget

**Opening:**
- "What kind of budget are you working with for this?"
- "Is this something you have allocated funds for, or do you need to get approval?"

**Cybersecurity:**
- "What's your current spend on security tools and monitoring?"
- "Are you looking for a one-time project or ongoing managed security?"
- "How much would a breach cost your business? (Helps frame ROI)"

**MSP/MSSP:**
- "What are you paying currently for IT support?"
- "Would you prefer a fixed monthly retainer or per-project pricing?"
- "What would it be worth to reduce downtime by [X]%?"

**Hardware/Software:**
- "Do you have a budget for new equipment, or are we looking at repairs first?"
- "Are you looking for the cheapest option, or the best value long-term?"

**Qualifying Budget Problems:**
- "Is budget the main blocker, or are there other factors?"
- "If we can show clear ROI, would budget be flexible?"

---

## 4. Questions About Timeline

**Urgency:**
- "When are you looking to have this resolved?"
- "Is there a deadline or event driving this?"
- "How soon do you need to make a decision?"

**Project Phases:**
- "Are you looking to roll this out all at once, or in phases?"
- "What's your ideal timeline for kicking this off?"

**Decision Process:**
- "Who else needs to be involved in the final decision?"
- "What's your typical process for evaluating vendors?"
- "Are you comparing us with anyone else?"

**Long-term:**
- "Is this a short-term fix or something you want to build out long-term?"
- "Where do you see your IT/tech needs in 1-3 years?"

---

## Quick Reference: Question Order

1. **Open** → "What brings you in today?"
2. **Qualify** → Role, company size, decision-making
3. **Pain Points** → "What's the biggest challenge?" / "What keeps you up at night?"
4. **Timeline** → "When do you need this?"
5. **Budget** → "What are you working with?" (ask after establishing need)
6. **Close** → "Does this process make sense? What's next?"

---

## Pro Tips

- **Listen more than you talk.** The best discovery calls are 80/20 (prospect/talk).
- **Mirror their language.** If they say "our network is a mess," use "network" in your follow-ups.
- **Take notes.** You'll forget details. Review before the next touchpoint.
- **Qualify hard.** If budget + timeline + need don't align, it's okay to walk away or reframe.
- **Match intensity.** Larger enterprise prospects = longer sales cycles, more stakeholders.
