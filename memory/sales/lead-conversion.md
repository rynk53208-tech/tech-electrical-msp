## 1. How to Close More Leads

### The Golden Rules

**Speed wins.** Respond within 5 minutes → 8x higher conversion. Within 1 hour → 5x higher. If you're not responding same-day, you're losing.

**Qualify early, not late.** Not every lead is worth pursuing. Use BANT:
- **B**udget: Do they have the money?
- **A**uthority: Are they the decision-maker?
- **N**eed: Do they actually need your services?
- **T**iming: Are they ready to buy now?

If they fail qualify on 2+ of these, nurture them differently — don't spend closing energy on cold leads.

### Close More by Solving, Not Selling

- Lead with problems they care about, not your services
- Mirror their language — if they say "my network keeps crashing," use those words back
- Use "sunk cost" psychology — get them committed small first (a 15-min call = investment)

### High-Conversion Closing Techniques

| Technique | When to Use | How It Works |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Assumptive Close** | When they show interest | "Great, let's get you scheduled for the onboarding." |
| **Summary Close** | After discovery | Recap their pain points + your solution, then ask for the business |
| **Scarcity/Time** | When there's a real reason | "I have 2 slots left this week" — creates urgency |
| **Social Proof** | When hesitant | "Company X was in the same situation — here's what we did" |
| **Risk Reversal** | When they stall | "If it's not a fit after 30 days, we adjust — no lock-in" |

### The Real Secret

**Follow up. Then follow up again.** 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups. Most salespeople quit after 2. You're not luckier than them — you're just more persistent.

## 2. Common Objections & How to Handle

### "It's too expensive"

**Don't:** Defend price or immediately offer discounts.
**Do:**
1. Acknowledge: "I hear you — budget matters."
2. Uncover: "Help me understand — is it the total price or the timing?"
3. Reframe: "Let's look at what [service] costs you in downtime/lost revenue vs. our fee."

*Example:* "The $3,000 repair vs. $15,000 downtime — that's a 5x return. Most clients see ROI in the first month."

### "I need to think about it"

**Don't:** Give them space to disappear.
**Do:**
1. Press for timeline: "What specifically are you weighing? And what's your decision timeline?"
2. Address the real concern: Usually this means "I'm not sure" or "I need more info."
3. Offer a next step: "Let's schedule a quick call next week to answer whatever comes up."

*Script:* "Makes sense. What would help you make that decision? I'll have my team put together a short summary you can review — can we touch base Thursday?"

### "I'm working with someone already"

**Don't:** Bash the competitor.
**Do:**
1. Validate: "Good — you should have a team that knows your setup."
2. Differentiate: "Where do you feel they're falling short? Often we see [specific issue] — that's where we specialize."
3. Create curiosity: "What's the one thing you'd change about your current setup?"

*Key point:* You're not competing on price. You're competing on the specific problems you solve better.

### "Can you just send me a quote?"

**Don't:** just send a price and wait.
**Do:**
1. Qualify first: "To make sure the quote is accurate, I need to understand [X, Y, Z]."
2. Get commitment: "Once you have the quote, what's the next step? Can we schedule 10 minutes to walk through it?"
3. Add value in the quote: Include ROI analysis, not just line items.

### "I'm not ready right now"

**Don't:** Give up.
**Do:**
1. Identify the timeline: "No problem. What's driving the timing? 3 months? 6 months?"
2. Plant seeds: "Between now and then, here's what I'd recommend watching for..."
3. Set a calendar reminder: "Let's connect in [X weeks] — I'll ping you."

## 3. Follow-Up Best Practices

### The Follow-Up Framework

**Rule of 7:** Lead needs to see you 7 times before they buy. Spread across:
- Email
- Phone call
- Text/SMS
- LinkedIn
- Voicemail
- Social engagement
- Value-add content

### Optimal Follow-Up Sequence

| Touch | When | Method | Content |
|-------|------|--------|---------|
| 1st | Same day | Call + Email | "Spoke with [X], here's what we discussed..." |
| 2nd | 2 days later | Email | Value-add: relevant case study or article |
| 3rd | 5 days later | Call | "Just checking — any questions on my last email?" |
| 4th | 10 days later | Voicemail + Email | "Quick update — we helped [similar client] with [result]" |
| 5th | 21 days later | LinkedIn | Connection + brief note |
| 6th | 35 days later | Call | "Checking in — any changes in your situation?" |

### Email Follow-Up Templates

**Short & Direct:**
> Subject: Quick question
> 
> [Name] —
> 
> Haven't heard back — everything good on your end? Happy to jump on a quick call if it makes sense.
> 
> Best,
> Irvin

**Value-First:**
> Subject: Thought this might help
> 
> [Name] —
> 
> Saw this case study on [their industry] — reminded me of your situation with [their problem]. Happy to walk through what we did and see if it applies.
> 
> [Link]
> 
> Let me know.

**The "Last Chance" (use sparingly):**
> Subject: One more thing
> 
> [Name] —
> 
> I know you're busy. I'll stop reaching out after this — but I wanted to make sure you had my direct number in case something comes up: [phone].
> 
> If things change, I'm here.
> 
> Best,
> Irvin

### Follow-Up Rules

- **Always provide value** — don't just "checking in"
- **Vary your method** — email fails, try phone, try LinkedIn
- **Use their name** — personalization breaks through
- **Make it easy to say no** — "If now's not the right time, just say so — I'll stop bugging you" builds trust
- **Track everything** — CRM, notes, dates. Missing follow-up = lost deal

## 4. What to Say on Discovery Calls

### Discovery Call Framework (15-20 min)

**1. Warm Open (2 min)**
- Thank them for their time
- Brief intro: "I specialize in [X] for businesses like yours"
- Set agenda: "I want to understand your situation and see if we're a fit. I'll ask some questions, and feel free to interrupt."

**2. Qualify (3-5 min)**
- "What's driving your search right now?"
- "What's your timeline?"
- "Who else is involved in this decision?"
- "What's your budget range — rough idea?"

**3. Deep Dive (5-8 min)** — This is where you win or lose
- **Pain mapping:** "Walk me through what happens when [the problem] occurs."
- **Impact:** "How much does that cost you — in time, money, stress?"
- **Attempts:** "What have you tried before? What worked / didn't work?"
- **Vision:** "If we solved this completely, what would that look like for you?"

**4. Qualify Them On You (2-3 min)**
- "Before I share how we work — any concerns about working with someone like us?"
- "What would make this a success for you?"

**5. Pitch & Next Steps (3-5 min)**
- **Condensed solution:** "Based on what you said, here's what I'd recommend..."
- **Proof point:** Share 1 relevant case study
- **Ask for the business:** "Sounds like a fit. Here's how we move forward — [proposal/call-to-action]"

### Power Questions (Use These)

- *"What's the biggest challenge with [their problem] right now?"*
- *"How long has this been an issue?"*
- *"What have you already tried?"*
- *"What would happen if you didn't solve this?"* (uncovers urgency)
- *"Who's involved in deciding?"*
- *"What's your timeline — is this urgent?"*
- *"What does success look like for you?"*
- *"Any reason we wouldn't be a good fit?"* (handles objections early)

### What NOT to Do on Discovery Calls

- **Don't pitch early** — qualify first, pitch second
- **Don't talk more than 40%** — you're there to listen
- **Don't assume** — ask "tell me more" constantly
- **Don't rush** — if they're talking, let them
- **Don't present without understanding** — no solution until you know the problem

### Discovery Call Cliff-Notes

> **Goal:** Understand their problem deeply enough that they think, "This person gets it."
> 
> **Win condition:** They say something like, "That's exactly what we're dealing with."
> 
> **Close condition:** You've identified a clear fit AND they've committed to next step.

## Quick Reference

### The Lead Conversion Mindset

1. **Lead → Prospect:** They respond to outreach
2. **Prospect → Opportunity:** They've got need + budget + timeline
3. **Opportunity → Customer:** You solved their problem better than anyone else

Don't waste time on leads that aren't prospects. Move fast on ones that are.

### One Thing to Remember

**People buy from people who understand their problem.** The more you listen, the more you learn, the better you pitch. Every discovery call is a chance to become the expert they already want.
