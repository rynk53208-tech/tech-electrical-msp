## 🎯 Start Here (5 Minutes)

1. **Read:** `EMAIL-OUTREACH-README.md` (implementation overview)

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track

After first week, you should see:
- **Open Rate:** 25-35% (goal: 35%+)
- **Click Rate:** 5-10% of opens
- **Reply Rate:** 2-5% of sends
- **Demos Booked:** 1-3% of sends (goal: 3%+)

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Don't over-personalize yet.** Start with standard templates, then customize once you see opens.
2. **Subject lines matter most.** A/B test 2-3 variations per wave.
3. **Reply speed matters.** Respond to demos within 2 hours to lock in commitment.
4. **Track everything.** Use `email-tracking.csv` or import to your CRM.
5. **Update as you go.** Replace example case study with your own results ASAP.

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

- ❌ Sending all 4 waves in one day (space them 3-5 days apart)
- ❌ Generic subject lines (personalize with name/company)
- ❌ Landing page with no CTA or unclear offer
- ❌ Not responding to replies (you're leaving money on the table)
- ❌ Sending to purchased lists (verify emails, respect opt-in)
