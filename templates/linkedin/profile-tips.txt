LinkedIn Profile Optimization Tips:
- Lead with Northrop-level credibility: mention Northrop experience and dual IT/electrical credibility.
- Focus on outcomes: uptime improvement, mean time to recovery, incident reduction.
- Include a short, value-based headline and a summary that mentions DevSecOps, cybersecurity, and hardware repair capabilities.
- Add media: case studies, whitepapers, or a 1-page micro-case study.
- Include clear CTAs: book a time, download content, or connect for a quick chat.
