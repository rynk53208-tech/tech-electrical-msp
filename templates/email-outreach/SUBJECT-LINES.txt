GUIDELINES FOR USE
===================
- Length: Keep under 50 characters for best mobile display (test in iPhone)
- Personalization: Always include {{FIRST_NAME}}, {{COMPANY}}, or {{INDUSTRY}} when possible
- Avoid: Excessive caps, too many punctuation marks, misleading subject lines
- Rotation: Use 2-3 variations per wave before rotating
- Timing: Space follow-ups 3-5 days apart
- Tracking: Note which subjects generate opens/replies in your CRM
