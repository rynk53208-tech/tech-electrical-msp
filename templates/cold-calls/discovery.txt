Discovery Script (2-3 minutes):
- Purpose: qualify fit and uncover pain points around uptime, incidents, backup disaster recovery, and security posture.
- Probing questions:
  - What systems keep you up at night (critical apps, backups, remote access)?
  - How do you currently handle incident response and change management?
  - What would a 2x improvement in uptime mean for your business?
  - Do you have regulated compliance concerns (PCI, HIPAA, etc.)?
- Suggested next steps: share a tailored 30-min DevSecOps cadence; schedule a demo or pilot.
