How often are backups performed and stored? ▼

We follow the 3-2-1 backup rule:

• 3 copies: Original data + 2 backup copies
• 2 media types: On-site disk storage + cloud redundancy
• 1 off-site: Geographically isolated cloud backup

Backup frequency varies by plan (typically daily to hourly for critical systems). Recovery time is typically 1–4 hours for most data. We test restores monthly to ensure reliability.

What is two-factor authentication (2FA) and why do I need it? ▼

2FA requires two verification steps to access your accounts: something you know (password) and something you have (phone, hardware key, app). Even if your password is compromised, hackers can't access your account without the second factor.

We recommend 2FA for all critical accounts (email, cloud, VPN, admin panels). Implementation is simple — typically 2–5 minutes per account — and we provide setup guidance for your team.

Popular options include Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, and hardware keys like YubiKey.

How is our data encrypted? ▼

We use industry-standard encryption across multiple layers:

• In transit: TLS 1.3 (256-bit) for all network communications
• At rest: AES-256 for file encryption (locally and in cloud)
• Backups: Encrypted before leaving your premises
• Device-level: BitLocker (Windows) and FileVault (Mac) enabled by default

All encryption keys are managed securely, and we comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and other standards. Full documentation is available upon request.
