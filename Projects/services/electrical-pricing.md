## How to Quote

1. **Identify scope** — What does the client need? Which services overlap?
2. **Build line-item** — Use pricing above as baseline
3. **Apply bundle discount** — If MSP client, knock off 10-15%
4. **Add permit buffer** — 10-15% for permit-heavy jobs
5. **Round up smart** — Clean numbers close better
