# Break-Fix Ticket Process — Tech & Electrical Services

**Purpose:** Standardize how we handle one-off repair calls.

---

## TICKET LIFECYCLE

```
NEW → IN PROGRESS → WAITING → RESOLVED → CLOSED
                ↓
            ESCALATED
```

---

## STEP 1: RECEIVE REQUEST

**How client reaches us:**
- Phone call
- Text
- Email
- Client portal
- Social media

**What we capture:**
- Client name/company
- Contact phone
- Issue description
- Urgency level
- Preferred time to respond

---

## STEP 2: CREATE TICKET

**Ticket #:** [Auto-increment]
**Date:** [Today]
**Client:** [Name]
**Contact:** [Phone]
**Reported by:** [Name]

**Issue:**
[Brief description of problem]

**Urgency:**
- [ ] Low — Convenience issue
- [ ] Medium — Affects work but has workaround
- [ ] High — Work stoppage
- [ ] Critical — Security breach/data loss

---

## STEP 3: TRIAGE

**Initial assessment:**
- Can we fix remotely?
- Parts needed?
- On-site visit required?
- Estimated time?

**Response time targets:**
| Priority | Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 15 min | 2 hours |
| High | 1 hour | Same day |
| Medium | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Low | 24 hours | 48 hours |

---

## STEP 5: FOLLOW-UP

**Same day:**
- Send completion email/text
- Confirm issue is resolved
- Ask for feedback

**If not resolved:**
- Document why
- Set follow-up appointment
- Escalate if needed

---

## STEP 6: CLOSE & BILL

**Close ticket:**
- Mark as resolved
- File documentation

**Billing (if applicable):**
- Invoice client
- Apply to their preferred payment method

**Post-service:**
- Ask for Google review
- Ask for referral
- Note in CRM for follow-up

---

## ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Escalate To |
|---|---|
| Not comfortable with fix | Senior tech or vendor |
| Need parts >$200 | Manager approval |
| Client unhappy | Owner (Irvin) |
| Security incident | Owner immediately |
| Can't resolve in 3 tries | Team huddle |

---

## TEMPLATES

### Initial Response (Text)
"Hi [name], this is Irvin. Got your message about [issue]. We're on it — expect to hear from [tech] within [X] hours. Ticket #[number] created."

### Completion Message
"Hi [name], [issue] is fixed! Here's what we did: [brief]. Let us know if you have any other issues. Thanks!"

### Request Feedback
"Thanks for letting us help today! Quick favor — could you leave us a Google review? It helps other small businesses find us: [link]"

## QUICK REFERENCE CARD

**Ticket #:** Write down
**Client:** Get full name + company
**Issue:** Write exactly what they say
**Urgency:** Ask "How urgent is this?"
**Callback:** Tell them when you'll be in touch

**Do:**
- Be polite
- Set expectations
- Document everything
- Follow up

**Don't:**
- Over-promise
- Leave hanging
- Bill before confirming
