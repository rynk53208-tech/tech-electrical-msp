# 5-Touch Outreach Sequence

## Overview

**Total Sequence:** 5 touches over 21 days  
**Touch Types:** 3 Emails + 2 LinkedIn/Phone attempts  
**Goal:** Book a discovery call or IT assessment

---

## Touch 1: Cold Email (Day 0)

**Channel:** Email  
**Template:** Choose based on primary pain point (A, B, or C)  
**Timing:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9-11 AM PST

**What to include:**
- Personalized subject line
- Industry-specific value prop
- One specific pain point
- Low-commitment ask ("quick question")

**Expected response:** 5-10% reply rate

---

## Touch 2: LinkedIn Connection + Message (Day 3)

**Channel:** LinkedIn  
**Timing:** 3 days after email

**Action Steps:**
1. Send connection request with personalized note:
   > "Hi [First Name], noticed we're both in the [Temecula business community/healthcare industry/legal field]. Would love to connect."

2. After connection (or if already connected), send brief InMail:
   > "Thanks for connecting! I reached out via email earlier re: IT support for [Company Name]. If it's not a fit, no worries—but if you've got 2 minutes, I'd love to hear about your biggest IT challenges. Either way, best of luck with [current project/season/etc]!"

**Purpose:** Multi-channel touch increases credibility

---

## Touch 3: Follow-up Email (Day 7)

**Channel:** Email  
**Timing:** 7 days after first email

**Subject:** Following up - [Company Name] + IT question

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Just following up on my earlier email about your IT support.

I know you're busy—so I'll keep this quick.

If things are going well with your current IT support, ignore this and best of luck!

If not, happy to chat about what a proactive managed IT solution could look like for [Company Name].

Either way—no pressure.

Best,  
Irvin  
[Phone]

---

## Touch 4: Phone Call + Voicemail (Day 14)

**Channel:** Phone call  
**Timing:** 14 days after first email, mid-week

**Script:**

> "Hi [First Name], this is Irvin Avitia. I sent you an email last week about IT support for [Company Name]. I know you're busy, so I won't take much of your time. If you're open to it, I'd love to have a quick 10-minute conversation about your IT needs—no pitch, just a chat. You can reach me at [phone] anytime. Thanks!"

**If gatekeeper answers:**
> "Hi, I'm trying to reach [First Name] regarding their IT support. Would you be able to let them know Irvin called and can be reached at [phone]?"

**Purpose:** Phone calls have higher conversion for B2B, shows persistence

---

## Touch 5: Final Email (Day 21)

**Channel:** Email  
**Timing:** 21 days after first email

**Subject:** Last try - [Company Name] + IT support

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

One last try (I promise).

I've reached out a few times regarding IT support for [Company Name], and I understand you might not be in the market right now.

Here's the deal: I genuinely believe I could help [Company Name] [reduce downtime/ improve security / get better IT support], but I also know timing is everything.

If now isn't the right time, I'll leave you alone. But if things change or you hear from another business owner who's frustrated with their IT, I'd be grateful for the referral.

Either way, best of luck with [upcoming event/season/project].

Cheers,  
Irvin

**P.S.** If you know someone who might benefit, I'd really appreciate the intro.

## Sequence Customization by Industry

### Medical Offices (HIPAA Focus)
- Lead with Template B (security/compliance)
- Touch 4 mention: "I know HIPAA compliance is a big deal..."
- Follow-up: Reference specific compliance concerns

### Law Firms (Data Protection Focus)
- Lead with Template B (security)
- Touch 4 mention: "Attorney-client privilege and case file protection..."
- Emphasize confidentiality

### Dental Practices (Templates A + B combo)
- Lead with Template A (IT support)
- Touch 3 mention: "HIPAA for patient records..."
- Patient data angle resonates strongly

### Accountants (Financial Data Focus)
- Lead with Template B (security)
- Touch 4 mention: "GLBA requirements and client financial data..."
- Emphasize trust and data protection

### Retail (Uptime Focus)
- Lead with Template C (reliability)
- Touch 3 mention: "POS systems and transaction processing..."
- Focus on revenue protection

## Pro Tips

1. **Batch your outreach:** Do 10-15 prospects at once so you can refine messaging
2. **Track everything:** You'll learn what works within 2-3 sequences
3. **Respond fast:** If someone replies within 2 hours, your close rate jumps 50%+
4. **Personalize the follow-ups:** Reference something specific about their business
5. **Don't give up too early:** The magic is in the follow-up—most people quit after 1 touch
6. **Mix it up:** Some prospects respond to email, others to LinkedIn—cover both bases
7. **Set reminders:** Use a CRM or simple calendar reminders for follow-ups
