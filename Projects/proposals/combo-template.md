## Customization Tips by Client Size

#### Small Business (5-20 employees)
- Lead with Package 1 pricing
- Focus on: Basic UPS, one circuit for server area, annual inspection
- Keep it simple — they're not technical buyers

#### Mid-Market (20-50 employees)
- Lead with Package 2
- Focus on: Server room dedicated circuit, UPS management, temperature monitoring
- They're comparing you to other MSPs — emphasize the electrical angle

#### Enterprise (50+ employees)
- Lead with Package 3
- Focus on: Full infrastructure, generator coordination, thermal imaging
- They have facilities teams — position as complementary, not replacing
