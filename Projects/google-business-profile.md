## STEP 7: REVIEWS

### How to get reviews:
1. Ask in person after successful job
2. Follow-up email: "Would you leave us a Google review? Here's the link: [your review link]"
3. Text: "Thanks for the business! Here's a quick link to review us: [link]"

### Respond to EVERY review:
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐: "Thanks so much! Great working with you."
- ⭐⭐⭐: "Sorry we didn't meet expectations. Please call us directly so we can make it right."
- ⭐⭐: "We'd love the chance to improve. Please contact us directly."
