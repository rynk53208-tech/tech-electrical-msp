# Referral Partners Strategy

**For:** Irvin Avitia's Tech & Electrical Services  
**Created:** 2026-03-18  
**Purpose:** Build passive lead flow through strategic partnerships

---

## The Opportunity

Referral partners = **passive leads**. When you build relationships with professionals who already serve your ideal clients, they send you clients while you focus on delivering great work.

---

## Target Partner Categories

### 1. Commercial Real Estate Agents

**Why they fit:** Their clients need:
- Office IT setup / network infrastructure
- Security systems for new buildings
- Technology consultation for tenant improvements
- Emergency tech support for property managers

**Ideal partner:** Agents specializing in office/industrial properties, property managers

**Value to them:** Add tech expertise to their service offering. Their clients trust them — a recommendation from their agent carries weight.

---

### 2. Accountants & CPAs

**Why they fit:** Their small business clients need:
- Cybersecurity (especially for financial data)
- Business technology consulting
- Network/infrastructure for growing businesses
- Compliance-related IT support

**Value to them:** Offer their clients a trusted tech partner. Reduces their liability when clients have proper IT security. Cross-sell: they can refer tax clients who need bookkeeping systems.

---

### 3. Lawyers (Business/Corporate)

**Why they fit:** Their clients need:
- Cybersecurity assessments
- Data protection / compliance
- Digital forensics prep (if litigation)
- Secure document management

**Value to them:** Tech due diligence for M&A, proper data security shows "reasonable care" in case of breach.

---

### 4. Insurance Agents (Commercial P&C)

**Why they fit:** Their commercial clients need:
- Risk assessments (cyber risk is a growing category)
- Technology infrastructure evaluation
- Documentation for cyber insurance applications

**Value to them:** Help clients meet insurance requirements. Their clients get better outcomes = fewer claims.

---

### 5. Business Brokers / M&A Advisors

**Why they fit:** Their clients (seller/buyer) need:
- IT due diligence
- Cybersecurity audits
- Valuation of tech infrastructure
- Post-sale tech transition support

**Value to them:** Adds credibility to their M&A process. Smooth tech handoffs = happier clients.

---

## What Irvin Can Offer Partners

### Referral Fee Structure

| Partner Type | Suggested Rate | Notes |
|--------------|---------------|-------|
| Real Estate Agents | 10-15% of first invoice | Paid after client pays |
| Accountants/CPAs | 10% of first invoice | Recurring clients common |
| Lawyers | 10-15% of project | Often larger projects |
| Insurance Agents | 10% of first annual contract | Steady stream |
| Business Brokers | 10-15% of deal value | Higher ticket, one-off |

**Alternative:** Flat fee instead of percentage (e.g., $200-500 per referral) — simpler, transparent.

### Reciprocal Benefits

- **Free initial consultation** for their clients (value: $150-300)
- **Priority scheduling** for referred clients
- **Bonus:** If they refer 3+ clients/year, offer a percentage of lifetime value
- **Co-marketing:** Feature them as a "trusted partner" on your site/social
- **Warm introductions** to their other network (real estate agent → property managers, etc.)

## Outreach Plan

### Phase 1: Research & List Building (Week 1)

1. **Local focus:** Temecula/Murrieta/South Riverside County
2. **Build target list:**
   - Search: "commercial real estate agents Temecula CA"
   - Search: "CPA firm Temecula small business"
   - Search: "business attorney Temecula CA"
   - Search: "commercial insurance agent Temecula"
   - Search: "business broker Riverside County"
3. **Priority:** Real estate agents & accountants first (highest volume, most relatable)

### Phase 2: Initial Contact (Week 2-3)

**Method: Warm email + LinkedIn connection**

Sample outreach (adapt to each):

> Subject: Tech partner for your clients?
> 
> Hi [Name],
> 
> I help [target: small business owners / property investors / etc.] with [cybersecurity / IT infrastructure / tech solutions].
> 
> I'm building relationships with [accountants / agents / etc.] who have clients that could use reliable tech support. When I get a referral from someone I trust, I make sure they look good to their client.
> 
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if there's a fit? No pressure, just exploring.
> 
> — Irvin
> [Phone / LinkedIn link]

### Phase 3: Relationship Building (Ongoing)

1. **Coffee chats** (in-person or Zoom) — treat them as peers, not just lead sources
2. **Send relevant content:** Articles on cyber risk, tech trends for small biz, etc.
3. **Ask about their business:** What challenges do their clients face? How can you help?
4. **Start small:** Offer a free consultation for their first referral — earn trust

### Phase 4: Formalize (After 2-3 Referrals)

- Send a simple agreement: "We appreciate your referral. Here's our promise:"
  - We will credit you [10%] when your referred client pays
  - We will treat your client like they'd be your own
  - We'll keep you updated on their project

## Key Messaging Points

When talking to potential partners, emphasize:

- **Credibility:** Northrop Grumman background, DevOps + cyber experience
- **Differentiation:** Rare combo of IT + electrical (can handle full-stack: network + hardware)
- **Reliability:** Someone who actually shows up and does the work
- **No headache:** You deal with the tech, they look good to their client
