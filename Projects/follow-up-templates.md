# Automated Follow-Up System — Templates

**Purpose:** Pre-written messages for consistent client communication.

---

## 📧 Email Templates

### 1. Quote Follow-Up (Sent 2 days after quote)
**Subject:** Following up on your IT quote — Questions?

Hi [Name],

Just wanted to check in on the quote I sent you for [service]. 

Have any questions? I'm happy to explain any part of it or adjust based on your needs.

Let me know either way so we can move forward.

Best,
Irvin
[Phone] | [Email]

---

### 2. Invoice Past Due (7 days overdue)
**Subject:** Friendly reminder — Invoice #[number] due

Hi [Name],

Just a friendly reminder that Invoice #[number] for $[amount] was due on [date].

If you've already sent payment, thanks! If not, let me know if you need anything.

Best,
Irvin

---

### 3. Service Completed (Same day)
**Subject:** Service complete — [Ticket #]

Hi [Name],

[Service performed] is complete. 

[Any notes about what was done / recommendations]

If you have any issues, just reply to this email or call [phone].

Thanks for your business!

Best,
Irvin

---

### 4. Monthly MSP Report (Sent 1st of month)
**Subject:** [Company] IT Summary — [Month Year]

Hi [Name],

Here's your monthly IT summary:

✅ **Uptime:** [X]%
🔧 **Tickets resolved:** [X]
🛡️ **Security status:** All clear

[Notes on any issues / upcoming renewals]

Questions? Just reply.

Best,
Irvin

---

### 5. Re-engagement (30 days no contact)
**Subject:** Quick check-in — How's everything running?

Hi [Name],

It's been about a month since we talked. Just checking in — how's everything running on the IT side?

Anything you need help with? I'm here if you have questions or want to chat about new services.

Best,
Irvin
