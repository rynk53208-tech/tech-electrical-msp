| Stage | When to Use |

## 📈 Quick Tips

**Color Coding (Google Sheets conditional formatting):**
- New Lead = Blue
- Attempted = Yellow
- Contacted = Orange
- Qualified = Light Green
- Quote Sent = Green
- Negotiating = Dark Green
- Closed Won = Bold Green background
- Closed Lost = Gray/Strikethrough

**Weekly Review Routine:**
- Monday: Check all "Quote Sent" - follow up if 5+ days
- Wednesday: Review "Attempted" - retry no-response leads
- Friday: Update "Last Contact" dates, plan next week
