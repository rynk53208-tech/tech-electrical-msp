## GAPS IN MARKET

### Opportunities We Can Fill

1. **Security-First MSP** — Most local guys treat security as add-on. We lead with it (Northrop background).

2. **IT + Electrical Combo** — NONE of them offer this. Huge differentiator for:
   - Office relocations (need network + electrical)
   - EV charger installs (need electrical + network for monitoring)
   - Security cameras (need electrical + network)

3. **Board-Level Repair** — Very few do micro soldering. This is a premium niche.

4. **Government Contracting** — Most local MSPs don't pursue gov contracts. SAM.gov opportunity.

5. **Spanish-Speaking** — Temecula has significant Spanish-speaking business community. This could be a market gap.
