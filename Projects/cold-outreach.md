# Cold Outreach Sequences — MSP Sales

**Purpose:** Scripts and emails for reaching out to potential MSP clients.

---

## 🎯 TARGET PROFILES

1. **Medical Offices** — HIPAA compliance = need security
2. **Law Firms** — Sensitive data, need reliability
3. **Accounting Firms** — Client data protection
4. **Real Estate Offices** — Many agents, multiple devices
5. **Retail Shops** — Point of sale systems
6. **Property Management** — Multiple locations

---

## 📧 EMAIL SEQUENCE (5-touch)

### Email 1: The Value Proposition
**Subject:** Quick question about [Company]'s IT

Hi [Name],

I help [industry] companies in Temecula with their IT — from day-to-day support to cybersecurity.

Most of my clients come to me because:
- They're tired of IT issues slowing down their team
- They had a security scare (or want to avoid one)
- They're paying too much for reactive break-fix

I’m not looking to sell you something today — just thought I'd introduce myself in case you ever need a second opinion on your setup.

No pressure. 

Best,
Irvin
[Phone] | [Email]

---

### Email 2: The Problem Reminder (Day 4)
**Subject:** One more thing on IT support

Hi [Name],

Just a quick follow-up. If you're happy with your current IT support, that's great — definitely stick with what works.

But if you've ever had:
- An IT guy no-show
- A slow response on an emergency
- A surprise bill for a simple fix

...then it might be worth a 15-minute chat. No commitment, just a conversation.

Either way, best of luck with [current project/season/etc].

Best,
Irvin

---

### Email 3: Social Proof (Day 10)
**Subject:** How we helped [Similar Company]

Hi [Name],

Wanted to share a quick story. We recently took over IT for a [similar company type] in [nearby city] — they were spending $X on break-fix calls every month.

After moving to managed support, they now have:
- Predictable monthly costs
- 24/7 monitoring (caught 2 issues before they became problems)
- A dedicated support team who knows their setup

Not saying this is your situation — but if it resonates, let's chat.

Best,
Irvin

If IT isn't top of mind right now, no worries — just save my number in case it ever comes up:

[Phone]

If it IS top of mind, happy to do a quick 15-min call to see if we might be a fit.

If things are running smoothly — awesome.

If not, or if anything has changed, I'm here.

## 📞 COLD CALL SCRIPT

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], this is Irvin with Tech & Electrical Services. Do you have 30 seconds?"

**If yes:**
"I'm reaching out because we help [industry] companies with their IT — managed support, security, that kind of thing. I'm curious — who's handling your IT right now?"

**If they have in-house:**
"Cool, sounds like you've got it covered. Mind if I ask what your biggest IT challenge is? Just for my own learning."

**If they use a provider:**
"No worries. What do you like about them? What frustrates you?" *(Listen for pain points)*

**If they have no one / frustrated:*
"That's actually why I'm calling. A lot of companies in [industry] are in the same boat. We do [specific benefits]. Would it make sense to send over some pricing so you have it for comparison?"

**Close:**
"Can I send you a quick email with our service overview? You can look at it when you have 2 minutes. Then if it makes sense, we can schedule a quick call."

**Initial text:**
"Hi [Name], this is Irvin — we talked about IT support for [Company]. Quick question — would a $X/mo fixed IT cost be better than $X,XXX in surprise repairs? Let me know if you want to chat."

**Follow-up (if no response):**
"No worries if now's not the right time. Just wanted to make sure you saw this. My number is [Phone] if you ever want to chat."
