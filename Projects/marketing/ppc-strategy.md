# PPC Advertising Strategy
## Irvin's Tech Business — Paid Advertising Plan

> Last updated: 2026-03-18

---

## Overview

This document outlines a paid advertising strategy across Google Ads (search) and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for Irvin's tech business in the Temecula, CA area. The strategy focuses on lead generation for MSP, MSSP, cybersecurity, computer repair, and electrical services.

**Geographic Targeting:** Temecula, CA + surrounding Riverside County (Murrieta, Menifee, Wildomar, Perris, Hemet, Lake Elsinore)

**Primary Goal:** Generate qualified leads at a target CPA of $50-150 depending on service type.

---

## 2. Facebook/Instagram Ads Strategy

### Campaign Structure

#### Campaign 1: "Awareness + Retargeting" (Conversion)
- **Objective:** Traffic + Retargeting
- **Funnel:** Cold audience → Landing page → Retarget with offer

#### Campaign 2: "Lead Gen - Consultation" (Lead Generation)
- **Objective:** Lead generation via Facebook Lead Forms
- **Offer:** Free IT Assessment ($500 value)

### Audience Targeting

#### Cold Audiences (Lookalike + Interest)
| Audience | Targeting | Size |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Business Owners | Interest: Small business, Entrepreneurship | 500K-1M |
| IT Decision Makers | Job titles: CTO, IT Director, IT Manager | 200K-500K |
| Real Estate Agents | Interest: Real estate (for office tech) | 100K-200K |
| Medical Practices | Industry: Healthcare (for HIPAA IT) | 50K-100K |
| Lawyers/Legal | Industry: Legal services | 50K-100K |

#### Lookalike Audiences
- **1% Lookalike:** Top 500 customers/lead (once available)
- **2% Lookalike:** All converted customers

#### Retargeting Audiences
| Audience | Description | Pixel Event |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Website Visitors | Last 30 days | ViewContent |
| Lead Form Starts | Didn't complete | Lead |
| Engagement | Page likes, video views (50%+) | Engagement |
| Warm Lead List | Email list uploaded | Custom Audience |

### Ad Creative Templates

#### Carousel Ad (Services Showcase)
- **Card 1:** "Managed IT Services" + icon
- **Card 2:** "Cybersecurity/MSSP" + shield icon
- **Card 3:** "Computer Repair" + laptop icon
- **Card 4:** "Custom PC Builds" + desktop icon
- **Card 5:** "Electrical Services" + bolt icon
- **Card 6:** "Get Free Assessment" + CTA button
- **Caption:** "IT + Electrical. One team. Temecula's only Northrop-trained tech company. 👇"

#### Single Image Ad (Retargeting)
- **Image:** Your team/office photo or before/after repair
- **Headline:** "Tech Problems? We Fix That."
- **Description:** "Free IT Assessment — $500 Value — Limited Time"
- **CTA:** "Claim Your Free Assessment"

#### Video Ad (Story/Intro)
- **Length:** 15-30 seconds
- **Content:** Quick intro showing your workspace, some repairs in progress, your certifications
- **Caption:** "Enterprise experience. Local dedication. Meet your new tech partner."
- **CTA:** "Learn More"

#### Lead Form Ad
- **Headline:** "Free IT Security Assessment"
- **Questions:** Name, Email, Phone, Company (optional), What do you need help with? (dropdown)
- **CTA:** "Get My Free Assessment"

- Goal: 3x ROAS minimum
