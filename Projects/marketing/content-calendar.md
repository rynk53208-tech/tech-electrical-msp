## Posting Best Practices

- **Best times:** 7:00am, 12:00pm, 5:00pm (PT)
- **Optimal days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Weekend posts:** Saturday mornings work for local/community
- **Frequency:** Start 3x/week, scale to 5x if engagement builds
- **Reply to EVERY comment** within 2 hours — algorithms reward interaction

---

## Content Mix Rule (40/30/30)

- **40% Value** — Helpful, educational, not salesy
- **30% Authority** — Shows expertise, credentials, proof
- **30% Conversion** — Clear CTAs, lead magnets, offers
