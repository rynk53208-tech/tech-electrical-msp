# Tech & Electrical Services LLC - Business Directory Listings

## Overview
This document lists 25+ business directories relevant for IT/electrical contractors in Temecula and Southern California. These platforms help increase visibility, build credibility, and generate leads.

**Tier 1: Must-Have Directories** (Highest ROI for service companies)

---

## 1. **Google Business Profile (GBP)**
- **Status:** ✅ Already Completed
- **Cost:** Free
- **Type:** Local Search / Maps
- **URL:** https://www.google.com/business/
- **Notes:** Essential for local SEO and appearing in Google Maps. Manages customer reviews and local visibility.

---

## 2. **Yelp for Business**
- **Cost:** Free (basic) / Paid (advertising available)
- **Type:** Review Platform & Directory
- **Registration URL:** https://biz.yelp.com/
- **Key Features:** Customer reviews, photos, business hours, appointment booking
- **Notes:** High traffic, especially for local service searches. Free listing, but paid ads amplify visibility.
- **Best For:** Electrical services, computer repair, IT support

---

## 3. **HomeAdvisor**
- **Cost:** Free (basic) / Paid (lead generation)
- **Type:** Service Professional Network
- **Registration URL:** https://www.homeadvisor.com/
- **Key Features:** Customer project matching, lead generation, background checks
- **Notes:** Strong for home-based services. Paid membership gets prioritized leads.
- **Best For:** Residential electrical work, IT home services

---

## 4. **Angi (formerly Angie's List)**
- **Cost:** Free (basic) / Paid (Angi Advantage membership)
- **Type:** Service Provider Directory & Reviews
- **Registration URL:** https://www.angi.com/
- **Key Features:** Professional ratings, customer reviews, job matching
- **Notes:** Rebranded from Angie's List. Well-established in home services market.
- **Best For:** Electrical contractors, IT support

---

## 5. **Thumbtack**
- **Cost:** Free (basic) / Paid (leads and ads)

- **Registration URL:** https://www.thumbtack.com/pro/

## 6. **Better Business Bureau (BBB)**
- **Cost:** Free (listing) / Paid (accreditation)

- **Registration URL:** https://www.bbb.org/
- **Key Features:** Business verification, customer reviews, complaint resolution
- **Notes:** Strong trust signal. Accreditation requires verification but builds credibility. List at local BBB chapter (San Diego County / Riverside County).
- **Best For:** B2B credibility, large commercial contracts

## Tips for Success

- **Consistency:** Use same business name, phone, address, photos across all platforms
- **Complete Profiles:** Fill 100% of available fields on each platform
- **Professional Photos:** Use high-quality images of team, work samples, equipment
- **Reviews:** Actively solicit reviews from past clients on all platforms
- **Local Keywords:** Emphasize "Temecula," "Southern California," service specializations
- **Response Time:** Reply to inquiries and reviews within 24 hours
- **NAP Data:** Keep Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistent everywhere
- **Categorization:** Choose accurate service categories (Electrical Contractor, IT Services, Cybersecurity, etc.)
