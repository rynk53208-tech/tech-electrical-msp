## 1. How to Ask for Reviews

### Timing is Everything
Ask at the **moment of delight** — right after you've:
- Fixed a complex issue (especially board-level repair)
- Completed a project ahead of schedule
- Saved them money or prevented data loss
- Went above and beyond (emergency call, weekend work)

### Script: In-Person / On-Site

> "Hey [Name], glad we got this working. If you're happy with the service, I'd really appreciate a quick review — it helps small businesses like mine get found online. Here's a link: [Google link]. Takes 30 seconds."

**Follow up via text same day:**
> "Thanks again for the call! If you have 30 seconds, a quick review on Google helps us out: [link]. Appreciate it!"

### Script: Post-Job Email

**Subject:** Quick favor — a review would help us out

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for letting us handle your [issue/project]. Hope everything's running smoothly now!
>
> If you have 30 seconds, we'd love a quick review on Google. It really helps small businesses get found online:
> [Google Business Profile Link]
>
> Even a single sentence makes a big difference.
>
> Thanks,  
> Irvin

### Script: After Positive Feedback

If a client says "thank you" or expresses satisfaction:

> "Glad I could help! If you want to pay it forward, a quick Google review really helps us out — it only takes a moment."

### Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask when they're happy | Ask when billing or during issues |
| Make it frictionless (direct link) | Ask for a "favor" without context |
| Be specific: "Did we exceed expectations?" | Be generic |
| Follow up within 24 hours | Wait days/weeks |
| Ask in person first, then text/email | Only email (low response) |

## 3. Response Templates

### Positive Review — Thank You (Google/Yelp)

**Short version:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! Glad we could help. We appreciate the kind words and the time to leave a review. See you next time!

**Extended version (if detailed):**
> Hi [Name], really appreciate you taking the time to share this! Board-level repair is tricky work — glad we could get your [device/system] back up and running. Thanks for trusting us with your [equipment]. Hope to work with you again down the road!

### Positive Review — Specific Mention (e.g., "fast," "fair price")

> Thanks [Name]! We aim to be both fast and fair. Appreciate the review — it helps other folks find us when they need IT help in Temecula. Cheers!

### Review with Specific Feedback

> Thank you, [Name]! Really glad you mentioned [specific thing] — that's exactly what we strive for. If you ever need us again, you've got our number. Thanks for the support!

### New Review on BBB

> Thank you for the review, [Name]! We appreciate you taking the time to share your experience. At [Business Name], we take pride in [specific value]. Thanks for the support!

## 4. Handling Negative Reviews

### The Golden Rule: Never Ignore or Argue

A thoughtful response to a negative review — even if the reviewer is wrong — shows **professionalism to every future customer who reads it**.

### Response Framework

1. **Acknowledge** — Thank them for feedback, show you heard
2. **Apologize** (even if not "your fault") — Show empathy
3. **Explain** (if appropriate) — Brief, factual, no excuses
4. **Invite offline** — Move to phone/email to resolve

### Template: Service Issue

> Hi [Name], thank you for your honest feedback. I'm sorry the experience didn't meet your expectations — that's not the standard we strive for. I'd like to make this right. Please reach out directly at [phone/email] so we can discuss and find a solution. We value your business and want to fix this.

### Template: Pricing Complaint

> Hi [Name], thanks for sharing your perspective. We aim to be transparent with pricing, and I understand [issue]. We'd welcome the chance to discuss this further. Please contact me directly at [phone] so we can address your concerns. Thank you for the feedback — it helps us improve.

### Template: Misunderstanding / Wrong Review

> Hi [Name], this appears to be about a different company or experience — we don't have any record of service to this address/date. Please reach out directly so we can clarify. We'd never turn away the chance to earn your business if you do need IT or electrical services.

### Template: Resolved Offline

> UPDATE: We connected with [Name] directly, resolved their concern, and they've updated their review. Thank you for the follow-up, [Name]!

*(Only use this if they've updated or you actually resolved it.)*

### What NOT to Do

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Arguing or being defensive | Future customers see it |
| Making excuses | Sounds unprofessional |
| Ignoring it | Looks like you don't care |
| Getting personal | Reflects poorly on you |
| Premature "apology" without context | Seem insincere |

### Prevention is Key

- **Address complaints before they become reviews** — follow up after every job
- **Set clear expectations** on timeline, pricing, process
- **Document everything** — if a client threatens a bad review, you have context
