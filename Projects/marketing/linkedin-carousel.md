## Formatting Tips for Canva/Adobe Express

1. **Font Recommended:** 
   - Headline: Bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or Poppins) — 40-48pt
   - Body: Clean sans-serif (Open Sans, Lato) — 24-28pt
   - Caption: Same family, slightly lighter weight — 18-22pt

2. **Color Palette Suggestions:**
   - Primary: Professional blue (#0066CC or #1A5490)
   - Accent: Trust/security orange or green (#FF6B35 or #2ECC71)
   - Background: Clean white or light gray (#F8F9FA)
   - Text: Dark gray or near-black (#2C3E50)

3. **Layout Formula:**
   - Top 40%: Headline + visual
   - Middle 40%: Bullet points (plenty of padding)
   - Bottom 20%: Caption (or swipe indicator for non-first slides)

4. **Consistency:**
   - Keep logo/branding element in corner of every slide
   - Use slide numbers (1/10, 2/10, etc.) for engagement tracking
   - Repeat color scheme and typography across all 10

## Posting Strategy

**Optimal Days/Times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8:00 AM–9:00 AM or 5:00 PM–6:00 PM PDT

**Caption Variations:**

- **Slide 1 (Lead Post):** Ask a pain-point question; mention swipe
- **Slides 2–9:** Brief context + curiosity bridge to next slide ("Swipe to see...")
- **Slide 10:** Direct CTA with link (website, Calendly, email, or form)

**Engagement Goal:** Get first 2–3 swipes (Slides 1–3). First comments usually come by then.

**Follow-Up Strategy:**
- Pin your own first comment with CTA link (Book now, Learn more, etc.)
- Respond to all comments within 2 hours
- Repost carousel in 2-3 weeks to reach different audience
