> Last updated: 2026-03-18

## 1. Logo Ideas

### Concept A: "The Shield + Bolt"
- **Visual:** A circuit-board-inspired shield with a lightning bolt running through it
- **Meaning:** Protection (cybersecurity) + Power/Energy (electrical/hardware repair)
- **Best for:** MSP/MSSP services, security-focused positioning

### Concept B: "Binary Bridge"
- **Visual:** Two interlocking brackets forming a bridge shape, with nodes at connection points
- **Meaning:** Connecting hardware to software, bridging IT + electrical divide
- **Best for:** Full-service positioning, custom builds, integration work

### Concept C: "The Chip"
- **Visual:** Stylized integrated circuit chip, but the traces form an "A" (for Avitia)
- **Meaning:** Deep hardware expertise, board-level repair credibility
- **Best for:** Computer repair, micro soldering niche

### Concept D: "Simple Wordmark"
- **Visual:** Your name — **IrvinTech** or **Avitia Systems** — clean, modern typography
- **Meaning:** Personal brand, trusted individual relationship
- **Best for:** Consultant/contractor positioning, local market

### Recommended First Step
Start with **Concept D** (wordmark) + **Concept A** (shield icon) as a sub-mark. Keeps it simple, professional, and lets you test messaging before committing to a complex design.

## 2. Color Scheme

### Primary Palette — "Cyber Trust"

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary | Deep Navy | `#0A1628` | Backgrounds, headers, text |
| Secondary | Electric Blue | `#00D4FF` | CTAs, accents, links |
| Accent | Circuit Green | `#00FF88` | Success states, highlights |
| Neutral | Cool Gray | `#64748B` | Body text, borders |

### Secondary Palette — "Power & Precision"

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Warm Accent | Amber | `#FFB800` | Warnings, electrical/power themes |
| Hardware | Copper | `#B87333` | Subtle hardware/electrical nods |
| Alert | Red | `#EF4444` | Errors, security alerts |

### Dark Mode Default
The tech/cybersecurity audience expects dark-mode aesthetics. Design primary materials dark with bright accents — feels premium and security-forward.

---

## 3. Typography

### Headlines: **JetBrains Mono** or **Space Grotesk**
- Tech-forward, readable, slightly distinctive
- JetBrains Mono = developer credibility
- Space Grotesk = modern SaaS feel

### Body Text: **Inter** or **DM Sans**
- Clean, professional, highly legible
- Inter = standard, safe choice
- DM Sans = slightly more character

### Code/Tech Elements: **Fira Code** (if displaying code snippets)
- Monospace with ligatures
- Shows technical depth

### Font Sizing Scale
| Element | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|--------|
| H1 (Hero) | 48-64px | 700 |
| H2 (Section) | 32px | 600 |
| H3 (Subsection) | 24px | 600 |
| Body | 16px | 400 |
| Caption | 14px | 400 |
