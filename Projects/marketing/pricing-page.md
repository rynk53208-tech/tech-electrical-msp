## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does billing work?

MSP plans are billed monthly per user. You can add or remove users at any time — we prorate. A la carte services are quoted upfront and billed upon completion.

### Do you require contracts?

**For MSP tiers:** We offer month-to-month for flexibility, but annual prepay gets you 2 months free. For Premium tier, we recommend annual for the best value.

**For a la carte:** No contract — just the agreed scope.

### What if I only need help once?

That's what a la carte is for. Many of our best clients started with a single repair or assessment. No commitment required.

### Can I switch tiers?

Absolutely. Upgrade anytime (pro-rated). Downgrade at your next billing cycle. We don't lock you in.

### Do you offer on-site support?

Yes. Remote is included in all MSP tiers. On-site visits for Standard/Premium use your monthly support credits. Additional on-site is $100/hour.

### What areas do you serve?

Primarily Temecula, Murrieta, and surrounding Riverside County. Remote support — we can help you from anywhere. On-site available within 50 miles of Temecula. Outside area? Let's talk — we may be able to make it work.

### How quickly can you respond?

| Tier | Response Time |
|------|---------------|
| Basic | Next business day |
| Standard | 30 minutes (24/7) |
| Premium | 15 minutes (24/7) |

### What's not included?

The following are billed separately:
- Hardware/software purchases
- Third-party SaaS licenses
- On-site travel beyond 30 miles
- After-hours work beyond MSP tier limits
- Disaster recovery or large-scale data restoration

### Do you work with home users?

For complex builds, micro soldering, or data recovery — yes. For routine home support, we focus on small business. Call us and we'll tell you straight if we can help.
