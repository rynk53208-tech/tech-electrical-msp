# Retargeting Ad Strategy for Irvin's Tech Business

## 1. How Retargeting Works

**Core Mechanism:**
Retargeting (also called remarketing) shows ads to people who've already visited your website or interacted with your brand but didn't convert. It works by placing a tracking pixel/code on your site that drops a cookie when a visitor lands on a page.

**The Funnel:**
1. **Awareness** → They land on your site (e.g., MSP services page)
2. **Consideration** → They browse but don't book a call
3. **Retargeting** → They see ads across the web reminding them
4. **Conversion** → They return and convert (book service, request quote)

**Why It Works:**
- Prospects need 7-13 touches on average before converting
- Retargeted users are 3x more likely to convert vs. cold audiences
- You're targeting warm leads who've already shown intent

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- **CTR** (click-through rate): 0.5-1.5% is good for retargeting
- **Conversion Rate**: Should be higher than cold traffic (2-5%)
- **ROAS**: Target 3-5x return on ad spend as a service business
- **Frequency**: Cap at 4-6 impressions/week to avoid ad fatigue

## 2. Platforms to Use

### Primary Platforms (Recommended):

| Platform | Best For | Min Budget |
|----------|----------|------------|
| **Google Ads (Display + Search)** | High-intent searchers, service keywords | $500/mo |
| **Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)** | Visual services, local targeting | $300/mo |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B MSP services, enterprise clients | $400/mo |

### Platform Breakdown:

**Google Ads**
- Use Display Network for "past visitors" audiences
- Use Search Network with "custom intent" audiences (people searching competitors)
- Best for: Emergency/repair services, cybersecurity, cloud services
- Setup: Install Google Tag Manager + conversion tracking first

**Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)**
- Great for visual展示 (custom builds, before/after repairs)
- Lookalike audiences from customer lists
- Best for: Computer repair, custom PC builds, residential services
- Pixel required on website

**LinkedIn**
- Higher CPM but better B2B targeting
- Use "Matched Audiences" for website visitors
- Best for: MSP contracts, cybersecurity services, enterprise IT

### Secondary Options:
- **Microsoft Ads**: Lower CPC, good for B2B/local
- **TikTok**: If targeting younger demographics for repairs/gaming PCs
- **YouTube**: Video testimonials, explainer ads

## 3. Ad Creative Ideas

### Google Display/Performance Max:

**Dynamic Retargeting (if you have a catalog):**
- "Computer Repair" category → Show relevant service ad
- "Cybersecurity" page → Show security-focused creative

**Static Display Ideas:**
- "Still thinking about your IT support?" + CTA
- "Free IT audit" reminder (if they visited pricing page)
- "Emergency repair available" for after-hours visitors

### Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram):

**Carousel Ads:**
- Slide 1: Problem ("Slow network? Security gaps?")
- Slide 2: Solution (Your service)
- Slide 3: Social proof (5-star review)
- Slide 4: CTA ("Book free consultation")

**Video Ideas:**
- 15-second: "We fixed this in 24 hours" quick turnaround
- 30-second: Behind-the-scenes board-level repair
- Testimonial: Happy business client talking about your service

**Single Image:**
- Before/after: Cracked screen → fixed
- "Your IT guy on vacation?" → "We never sleep"
- Local landmark + your truck/team = trust

**Lead Gen Ads:**
- "Get free IT assessment" → Direct lead form
- "Download MSP pricing guide" → Gated content

### LinkedIn Ads:

- "Other IT guys ghosted you?" → B2B pain point
- Case study carousel: "How we saved Company X $50k/yr"
- "Enterprise-grade security for SMB budgets"

## 4. Budget Allocation

### Recommended Monthly Budget: $1,200 - $2,500/mo

**Split by Platform:**

| Platform | % of Budget | Monthly Amount | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|----------------|---------|
| Google Ads | 40% | $480 - $1,000 | High-intent search + display |
| Meta Ads | 35% | $420 - $875 | Nurture, visual, local |
| LinkedIn | 25% | $300 - $625 | B2B, enterprise |

**Allocation by Funnel Stage:**

- **Cold (prospecting)** → 20% of budget
- **Retargeting (warm)** → 60% of budget ← PRIMARY FOCUS
- **Conversion (hot)** → 20% of budget

**Retargeting Segments to Build:**

1. **All website visitors (30 days)** - Broad retargeting
2. **Service page visitors** - Specific intent (e.g., "visited /cybersecurity")
3. **Pricing page visitors** - High intent, price-sensitive
4. **Cart/quote abandoners** - Requested quote but didn't convert
5. **Past customers** - Upsell, repeat business

**Pacing Strategy:**
- Start conservative: $20-30/day per platform
- Ramp up winners: Double budget on ads with CTR > 1%
- Pause losers: Kill ads with CTR < 0.3%
- Frequency cap: Max 4 impressions per user per week
