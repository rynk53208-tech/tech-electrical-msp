Frequently Asked Questions

What size business is this for?

We specialize in small businesses with 10–50 employees. Big enough to need real IT infrastructure, small enough that a bloated MSP with 500 clients won't give you the time of day. You'll get direct access to an engineer, not a ticket queue.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. We do offer a 10% discount for annual commitments, but we'd rather earn your business every month than trap you in a contract. If we're not delivering value, you should be free to leave.

What happens when something breaks at 2 AM?

Our monitoring systems run 24/7 and auto-alert on critical issues. For Professional and Enterprise Shield clients, critical incidents trigger immediate response regardless of time. Foundation clients receive next-business-day response for non-critical issues, with critical alerts still monitored around the clock.

We already have antivirus. Why do we need managed security?

Antivirus catches known threats — that's about 40% of modern attacks. Managed security includes EDR (behavioral detection), email filtering, dark web monitoring, phishing training, and an actual human analyzing alerts. It's the difference between a door lock and a full security system with cameras, guards, and a panic button.

Can you help with compliance (HIPAA, PCI, CMMC)?

Yes. Our Enterprise Shield plan includes full compliance management. Professional includes quarterly assessments to identify gaps. Irvin's background in defense contracting (Northrop Grumman, CMMC environments) means compliance isn't an afterthought — it's built into how we operate.

What about hardware? Do you sell computers/servers?

We handle full hardware lifecycle management on Enterprise Shield, including procurement, deployment, and end-of-life. On all plans, we provide vendor-agnostic recommendations — we don't mark up hardware to pad margins. We also do board-level repair (micro soldering), which means we can often fix equipment other shops would tell you to replace.

Do you also handle electrical work and cabling?

Yes — Tech & Electrical Services is a licensed electrical contractor. We do structured cabling (Cat6/fiber), server room power, UPS installation, and full office buildouts. One vendor for IT and infrastructure means fewer headaches and faster project timelines.

How is this different from other MSPs in the area?

Three things: (1) You get a Security+ certified, Northrop Grumman-trained engineer — not a dispatcher routing your ticket to a junior tech. (2) We combine IT, cybersecurity, hardware repair, and electrical under one roof — nobody else in the Temecula Valley does that. (3) We're small by design — you'll never be client #847 waiting in a queue.

What's the onboarding process?

1. Free Assessment (15 min call) — We learn about your business and current IT setup.
2. Site Audit (on-site or remote) — We document your network, devices, and security posture.
3. Proposal — Custom recommendation with a clear plan and timeline.
4. Deployment (1–2 weeks typical) — We install monitoring agents, configure backups, harden your network, and onboard your team.
5. Ongoing Management — Proactive monitoring, regular reports, and a direct line to your engineer.
