## 💡 Pro Tips for Multiple Case Studies

1. **Vary the story arc:** Different industries/challenges tell different stories
2. **Use consistent metrics:** Track the same KPIs across cases so patterns emerge
3. **Keep a case study database:** Record metrics as projects finish (easier than asking clients later)
4. **Update old ones:** Revisit case studies after 6-12 months for long-term ROI
5. **Get permission early:** Schedule testimonial calls while the client is satisfied (don't wait 6 months)
