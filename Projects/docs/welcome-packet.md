## 3. What to Expect

### The Onboarding Process

1. **Initial Consultation** — We'll discuss your needs, goals, and timeline. No pressure, just fact-finding.

2. **Proposal/Quote** — You'll receive a clear breakdown of scope, timeline, and pricing. We explain everything in plain English.

3. **Project Kickoff** — Once approved, we get to work. You'll have a direct point of contact throughout.

4. **Regular Updates** — We keep you in the loop with progress updates. You won't wonder what's happening.

5. **Completion & Handoff** — We deliver what we promised, test everything, and make sure you're satisfied.

### Communication
- **Phone/Text:** For urgent matters
- **Email:** For detailed requests and documentation
- **Response Time:** Same business day for most inquiries
