# Client Communication Scripts

_Phone scripts for Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services_

---

## 1. Answering Service Greeting

**When you answer the phone:**

> "Thanks for calling [Company Name], this is [Your Name]. How can I help you?"

**If you're taking a message:**

> "Let me make sure I get you to the right person. Can I get your name and phone number? [Take details] I'll have [Irvin/tech] get back to you within [X hours]. Is there anything urgent I should pass along?"

**Key points:**
- Answer within 3 rings
- Speak clearly, professional tone
- Get caller info (name, number, reason for call)

---

## 2. Taking Support Calls

**Initial response:**

> "I can help you with that. Can you walk me through what you're seeing?"

**Gathering info:**
- What system/device is affected?
- When did the issue start?
- Any error messages?
- Has anything changed recently (updates, new equipment, etc.)?

**If you can resolve it:**
> "Try this: [step-by-step]. Let me know if that works."

**If it needs remote support:**
> "I can remote in to take a look. Can you give me [teamviewer ID / anydesk address / remote access credentials]?"

**If it needs on-site:**
> "Looks like we'll need to come out. I can schedule you for [day/time]. [Irvin/technician] will bring the tools needed for [describe issue]. Does that work?"

**If it's not urgent:**
> "I want to make sure we get this right. Can I schedule you for a proper look-see?"

---

## 3. Handling Complaints

**Stay calm, listen first. Don't interrupt.**

> "I hear you, and I'm sorry you're dealing with this."

**Acknowledge the issue:**
> "That's not the service we aim to provide."

**Get the facts:**
> "Help me understand what happened — when did you first notice [issue]? What did [employee/team] tell you?"

**Solve or escalate:**
- If it's something you can fix → fix it
- If it's a billing issue → clarify and correct
- If it's a service failure → offer correction (revisit, discount, etc.)

> "Here's what I can do: [offer]. Will that work for you?"

**If they want to speak to Irvin:**
> "Absolutely, let me get you to Irvin directly." [Transfer or take message]

**Never say:**
- "That's not my department"
- "You'll have to..."
- "Calm down"

---

## 4. After-Hours Emergency

**If caller is an existing client with emergency:**

> "I see you're on our emergency line. Tell me what's going on."

**Assess urgency:**
- System down / security breach / data loss → Priority 1
- No access / partial outage → Priority 2
- Not urgent but worried → Priority 3

**Priority 1 (real emergency):**
> "I'm alerting Irvin now. Can you stay by your phone? He'll call you back within [15-30] minutes."

**Priority 2:**
> "We can handle this tomorrow, but if you need us tonight, there's an after-hours fee of $[X]. Want me to schedule you for [time]?"

**Priority 3 (not really an emergency):**
> "I understand. Let's get you scheduled for tomorrow. What works for you — morning or afternoon?"

**If not a client:**
> "We handle emergencies for existing clients only. Can I get you scheduled for a regular appointment?"
