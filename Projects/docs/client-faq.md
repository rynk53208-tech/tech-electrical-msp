# Client FAQ

## How does billing work?

We offer flexible billing options to fit your needs:
- **Hourly** — Pay for the time spent on your project or issue
- **Flat-rate** — Fixed price for defined services (repairs, installs, standard jobs)
- **Retainer** — Monthly agreement for ongoing IT support and maintenance

We'll always give you a clear estimate before starting work. No surprise charges.

---

## What hours are you available?

**Standard hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Pacific)

**Emergency support:** Available outside standard hours for existing clients with critical issues. Call or text for urgent situations.

Response times vary — see below.

---

## How quickly do you respond?

- **Initial response:** Within 24 hours during business days
- **Urgent issues:** Same-day response for critical problems
- **Emergencies:** Call or text — we prioritize speed for downtime situations

We'll keep you updated on timeline once we assess the issue.

---

## What areas do you serve?

Primarily **Temecula, CA** and surrounding areas (Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore, Winchester, Fallbrook).

Remote support available statewide and beyond — we can handle many issues remotely to save you a trip.

*Not sure if you're in our service area? Ask.*

## What brands do you work with?

We work across all major brands and platforms:

- **Computers:** Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple (Mac/iMac/MacBook), ASUS, Acer, custom builds
- **Networking:** Cisco, Ubiquiti, Netgear, TP-Link, Meraki
- **Security:** CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender, various firewall platforms
- **Cloud:** AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, O365, Google Workspace

If it's tech, we can handle it — even if it's not listed here.
