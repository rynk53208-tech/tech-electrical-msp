# One-Page Business Plan
## Irvin's Tech & Electrical Services

---

### 1. Mission Statement
Deliver enterprise-grade cybersecurity, IT infrastructure, and electrical solutions to small businesses and homeowners who need Northrop-level expertise without the enterprise price tag. We bridge the gap between corporate security and local service.

---

### 2. Target Market
- **Primary:** Small businesses (5-50 employees) needing MSP/MSSP services, network security, and compliance
- **Secondary:** Home users and prosumers seeking high-end custom PC builds, board-level repair, and smart home integration
- **Tertiary:** Property managers/contractors needing reliable electrical services with tech-savvy coordination

**Differentiation:** Most MSPs lack deep cybersecurity pedigree. Most electricians don't understand network infrastructure. Irvin's combo of IT + electrical + security clearances fills both gaps.

---

### 3. Services
| Category | Offerings |
|----------|-----------|
| **Cybersecurity** | Vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, security audits, compliance consulting (SOC2, HIPAA) |
| **Managed Services** | 24/7 monitoring, help desk, cloud infrastructure, backup/disaster recovery |
| **Hardware** | Custom PC builds, laptop/board-level repair, micro soldering, data recovery |
| **Electrical** | Residential/commercial electrical, low-voltage wiring, smart home installs |

---

### 4. Revenue Goals
- **Year 1:** $60K - $80K (establish reputation, land first 10-15 SMB clients)
- **Year 2:** $120K - $150K (scale MSP contracts, add 2-3 recurring revenue streams)
- **Year 3:** $200K+ (team expansion, passive income via templates/consulting products)

**Key Metrics:**
- 15-20 managed service clients @ $1.5K-3K/month
- 5+ custom build/repair jobs/month
- Electrical as overflow/seasonal revenue

---

### 5. Growth Strategy
1. **Specialize to differentiate** — Lead with "cybersecurity-first MSP" positioning. Use Northrop background as proof of expertise.
2. **Recurring revenue model** — Shift from hourly to retainers. Offer tiered MSP packages ($500/$1K/$2K/mo).
3. **Content + authority** — Post case studies, security tips, build showcases. Capture local search intent.
4. **Strategic partnerships** — Align with accountants, lawyers, property managers who refer IT needs.
5. **Leverage rare skills** — Board-level micro soldering is a moat. Advertise as "the only game in town" for advanced repair.
6. **Hire gradually** — Year 2: part-time help desk. Year 3: junior tech. Protect margins until volume justifies.
