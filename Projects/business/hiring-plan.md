# Hiring Plan - Irvin's Tech Business

**Prepared by:** Axiom  
**Date:** 2026-03-18  
**Purpose:** Growth plan for scaling from solo operator to team

---

## 📊 Current Business State

- **Stage:** Solo operator (Irvin does all delivery + sales + admin)
- **Revenue Target:** $9,500/mo MRR to replace $114k/year salary
- **Client Goal:** 15 MSP clients @ ~$633/client average
- **Services:** MSP/MSSP, cybersecurity, computer repair (including micro-soldering/board-level), electrical services, software development

## 1. When to Hire - Revenue Milestones

### Phase-Based Triggers

| Phase | Revenue Trigger | Trigger Description |
|-------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Phase 0** | $0 - $4,999/mo | Survival mode. Solo only. No hire. |
| **Phase 1** | $5,000/mo sustained (3+ months) | Irvin at capacity on delivery. Consider first hire. |
| **Phase 2** | $8,000/mo sustained | Need sales bandwidth. First sales/lead gen hire. |
| **Phase 3** | $12,000/mo sustained | Full team structure. Admin needs. |
| **Phase 4** | $18,000/mo sustained | Department leads. Second tech. |

### Specific Triggers (Not Just Revenue)

Hire when **ANY** of these hit:
- [ ] Irvin working >50hrs/week consistently
- [ ] Turning away jobs due to capacity
- [ ] Missing follow-ups because no time
- [ ] Quality slipping on delivery
- [ ] 3+ months of $5k+ revenue with no growth in sight

## 2. First Hire: Helpdesk Tech vs Sales

### Recommendation: **Helpdesk Tech FIRST**

**Rationale:**

| Factor | Helpdesk Tech | Sales/Lead Gen |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| Revenue impact | Frees Irvin to sell more | Needs product/market fit first |
| Risk | Lower - pays for itself gradually | Higher - no pipeline to close |
| Irvin's bottleneck | Currently does everything | Can do sales himself initially |
| Client satisfaction | Improves (faster response) | Doesn't fix delivery |

### When Sales Becomes #1 Priority

Hire dedicated sales when:
- Irvin is at capacity delivering AND still has qualified leads going cold
- Lead conversion is >20% but he's too busy to follow up
- Has 10+ MSP clients and steady inbound

### First Hire Profile: Helpdesk Tech

**Role:** Level 1/2 Helpdesk Technician  
**Hourly rate:** $25-40/hr (depending on skill level)  
**When:** At $5k/mo sustained  

**What they handle:**
- T1/T2 support tickets
- Remote helpdesk (80% of work)
- On-site for simple deployments
- Monitoring alerts
- Client onboarding basics

**What Irvin keeps:**
- Complex troubleshooting (L3)
- Sales/consulting
- Cybersecurity work
- Micro-soldering/board repair
- Electrical jobs
- High-level strategy

## 3. Outsource vs In-House

### 🔴 Keep In-House (Core Competencies)

| Service | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Cybersecurity consulting | Irvin's differentiator. Northrop-level credibility. |
| Micro-soldering/board repair | Rare skill, high margin, hard to outsource quality. |
| Electrical services | Licensing/liability issues. Keep close. |
| MSP architecture | Core IP. Don't let someone else own this. |
| Sales conversations | Relationship-based. Irvin closes best. |
| High-level strategy | Founder work. Can't delegate yet. |

### 🟢 Safe to Outsource

| Service | What | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Bookkeeping** | Monthly reconcile, invoicing | Low skill, repetitive |
| **Payroll** | If employees hired | Liability/compliance |
| **Lead generation** | Cold outreach, email scripts | Can measure ROI |
| **Content creation** | Blog posts, social | Time sink for low impact |
| **Graphic design** | Proposals, marketing materials | Commodity skill |
| **Phone answering** | VA for after-hours | Missed calls = lost revenue |
| **Basic SEO** | Local listings, reviews | Can be measured |
| **Hardware procurement** | Ordering parts | Time-consuming, no differentiation |

### 🟡 Outsource with Caution

| Service | Caution |
|---------|---------|
| Tier 1 helpdesk | Can outsource if you own the process. Quality risk. |
| Social media management | Brand voice issues. Overlook instead. |
| Email marketing | Easy to mess up brand. Write templates, VA sends. |

### Detailed Timeline

#### Phase 1: Foundation (Now - $5k/mo)
**Timeline:** Now until sustainable $5k/mo

**Actions:**
- [ ] Nail product-market fit (15 MSP clients target)
- [ ] Document all processes (onboarding, support, billing)

#### Phase 2: First Hire - Helpdesk Tech ($5k-$8k/mo)
**Timeline:** ~Month 6-9 of $5k+ revenue

**Role:** Part-time or full-time L1/L2 tech

**Cost:** $3,000-5,000/mo (25-40hrs @ $25-40/hr)

**What changes:**
- Irvin does 30% delivery, 70% sales/strategy
- Response time drops from hours to minutes
- Capacity: +15-20 hrs/week of billable work

**Hiring process:**
1. Post on Indeed ($50-100), Craigslist, local tech groups
2. Look for: CompTIA A+, Network+, or real experience
3. Test: Have them walk through a remote support scenario
4. Trial: 2-week paid trial on easy tickets

---

#### Phase 3: Sales/Admin Support ($8k-$12k/mo)
**Timeline:** Month 9-15

**Options:**

**Option A: Part-time VA ($500-800/mo)**
- Handles: email, scheduling, follow-ups, CRM data entry
- Best for: Still doing sales yourself

**Option B: Commission-based sales ($2k-4k base + 10-15% commission)**
- Handles: Outbound prospecting, follow-up, some closing
- Best for: Enough leads to justify dedicated follow-up

**Recommendation:** Start with VA first. Sales hire only if pipeline is overflowing.

**Specialist hire** (cybersecurity, etc.) - only if:

- Can charge premium rates

## 📋 Decision Framework

### Before Any Hire, Ask:

1. **Can I afford it?** (3-month runway of full salary)
2. **Will they pay for themselves?** (Calculate: revenue they generate or save - their cost = positive?)
3. **Do I have work for them?** (At least 30hrs/week minimum)
4. **Can I manage them?** (Have time to train/oversee?)
5. **What's the worst case?** (Can I survive if they don't work out?)

### Hire Fast, Fire Faster

- 30-day trial period for everyone
- Clear metrics from day 1
- If not working out by day 45, let go
- Don't let one bad hire sink the business
