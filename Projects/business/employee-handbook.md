# Employee Handbook Outline

## 1. Company Values

### Core Principles
- **Excellence Over Speed** – Quality work beats rushed work. Northrop-level standards apply.
- **Integrity First** – Security isn't just what we do; it's who we are. No shortcuts.
- **Client Obsession** – We solve problems, not just tickets. Understand the "why."
- **Continuous Learning** – Tech changes fast. Stay ahead or get left behind.
- **Own It** – If you see something wrong, fix it. Don't pass the buck.

### Culture
- Hands-on, no-nonsense attitude
- Resourceful over reliant
- Direct communication, no drama

---

## 2. Expectations

### Professional Conduct
- Arrive prepared and on time
- Represent the company professionally (client-facing or not)
- Keep credentials secure – never share passwords, access keys, or client data
- No subcontracting of sensitive work without approval

### Performance
- Meet deadlines or communicate blockers early
- Document your work – if it's not documented, it didn't happen
- Escalate when stuck – pride in self-sufficiency ≠ martyrdom
- Own mistakes – fix them fast, learn from them

### Physical & Remote Work
- Remote: Maintain a professional workspace, reliable internet, secure environment
- On-site: Follow client site protocols, sign in/out as required
- Availability: Respond to urgent issues within SLA windows

---

## 3. Communication

### Internal
- **Primary:** [Slack / Teams / Email]
- **Urgent:** Phone / text for P1/P2 issues
- **Meetings:** Weekly team sync, ad-hoc as needed – come prepared
- **Documentation:** Update tickets, notes, and shared docs in real-time

### Client-Facing
- Never promise outcomes without checking first
- Clear, jargon-free communication – clients trust what they understand
- Set expectations on timelines – under-promise, over-deliver
- Escalate client tensions to management immediately

### After-Hours
- On-call rotation applies – know your schedule
- Emergency contacts must be current
- Respond to alerts within SLA; investigate within 1 hour

## 5. Procedures

### Onboarding
1. Complete HR paperwork
2. Set up accounts (email, ticketing, communication)
3. Security training (mandatory – complete within first week)
4. Assign mentor/buddy
5. Review client-specific procedures

### Incident Response
1. Identify severity (P1-P4)
2. Document in ticketing system immediately
3. Notify lead/manager for P1/P2
4. Resolve or escalate – don't sit on it
5. Post-incident notes – what happened, root cause, prevention

### Change Management
1. Document proposed change
2. Get approval for production changes
3. Schedule during maintenance windows when possible
4. Test in staging/DEV first
5. Rollback plan required

### Time Tracking & Reporting
- Log hours daily
- Expense reports: Submit by [weekly/bi-weekly deadline]
- PTO/Leave: Request with minimum [1 week / 2 weeks] notice

### Offboarding
- Return all equipment
- Transfer knowledge/documentation
- Revoke access within 24 hours
- Exit interview
