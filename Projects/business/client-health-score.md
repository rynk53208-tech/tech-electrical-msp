# Client Health Scorecard

> **Overseer Priority Document** | Created: 2026-03-18
> Purpose: Proactively identify at-risk clients and prioritize retention efforts

---

## 1. Metrics to Track

### Revenue Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | Consistent income from retainer clients | Growing MoM |
| **Project Revenue** | One-time service income | Healthy mix with MRR |
| **Revenue per Client** | Client's total value to your business | Above break-even threshold |
| **Revenue Growth Rate** | Client's spending trend over 12 months | Positive or flat |

### Engagement Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| **Contact Frequency** | How often client reaches out/proactive touch | Min 1x/month (retainers) |
| **Ticket Volume** | Support requests — high can mean issues OR trust | Depends on service type |
| **Response Time** | Your speed — impacts their perception | <4 hours during business |
| **Meeting Attendance** | Show up to quarterly reviews? | 100% attendance |
| **Email Open Rate** | Engagement with your communications | >60% |

### Satisfaction Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| **NPS Score** | Net Promoter Score (0-10 scale) | 50+ (Good), 70+ (Excellent) |
| **CSAT Score** | Post-project satisfaction (1-5) | 4.0+ average |
| **Support Satisfaction** | Rating on individual tickets | 4.5+ |
| **Renewal Rate** | Clients who renew contracts | >85% |
| **Churn Rate** | Clients lost per quarter | <5% |

### Account Health Indicators

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| **Stakeholder Access** | How many contacts do you have? | 2+ decision makers |
| **Contract Status** | Active, expiring soon, expired | Always tracked |
| **Last Interaction** | Days since last contact | <30 days |
| **Upcoming Renewals** | Contract expiration timeline | Tracked 90 days out |
| **Payment History** | On-time vs. late payments | 100% on-time |

## 2. Scoring System

### Health Score Calculation (0-100)

```
HEALTH SCORE = (Engagement × 0.30) + (Satisfaction × 0.40) + (Financial × 0.30)
```

#### Scoring Breakdown

**Engagement Score (0-100) — Weight: 30%**
| Factor | Points | Calculation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Contact Frequency | 30 | 10 pts per month of active contact |
| Ticket Engagement | 25 | 5 pts if tickets resolved quickly; -10 if neglected |
| Meeting Participation | 25 | 10 pts per quarterly meeting attended |
| Stakeholder Breadth | 20 | 10 pts per additional decision-maker contact |

**Satisfaction Score (0-100) — Weight: 40%**
| Factor | Points | Calculation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| NPS | 35 | NPS score (already -100 to 100, normalize to 0-100) |
| CSAT | 30 | CSAT average × 20 |
| Support Ratings | 20 | Average ticket rating × 4 |
| Renewal History | 15 | 15 pts if renewed without pushback |

**Financial Score (0-100) — Weight: 30%**
| Factor | Points | Calculation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Payment Reliability | 40 | Full points if always on-time; prorated if late |
| Revenue Trend | 30 | Growing (+30), Flat (+15), Declining (0) |
| Contract Security | 30 | Signed multi-year (+30), Annual (+20), Month-to-month (+10) |

---

### Health Tiers

| Tier | Score Range | Interpretation |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| 🟢 **Healthy** | 80-100 | Client is solid. Maintain status quo. |
| 🟡 **At-Risk** | 60-79 | Some concerns. Monitor closely, proactive outreach. |
| 🔴 **Critical** | 40-59 | Significant issues. Immediate intervention required. |
| ⚫ **Churned** | 0-39 | Likely流失. Salvage or exit gracefully. |

## 3. Intervention Triggers

### Automatic Triggers (Immediate Action)

| Trigger | Condition | Action Level |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Payment Failure** | Invoice 30+ days overdue | 🔴 Critical — Contact immediately |
| **NPS Drop** | Score drops 20+ points | 🔴 Critical — Personal outreach within 48h |
| **Contract Expiry** | Within 30 days, no renewal talk | 🔴 Critical — Schedule renewal meeting |
| **Support Escalation** | 3+ escalated tickets in 30 days | 🟡 At-Risk — Quality review |
| **Stakeholder Gone** | Primary contact left company | 🟡 At-Risk — Re-establish relationships |
| **Zero Contact** | 60+ days with no interaction | 🟡 At-Risk — Outreach required |

### Trend-Based Triggers (Weekly Review)

| Trigger | Condition | Action Level |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Revenue Decline** | 20%+ drop in 3 months | 🟡 At-Risk — Discuss needs |
| **Ticket Spike** | 2x normal volume | 🟡 At-Risk — Check for systemic issues |
| **Low CSAT** | Average <3.5 last 10 tickets | 🟡 At-Risk — Quality audit |
| **Competitor Mention** | Client asks about alternatives | 🟡 At-Risk — Value conversation |
| **Meeting Miss** | 2+ quarterly meetings skipped | 🟡 At-Risk — Re-engage |

### Manual Flags (Ad-hoc)

- Client expresses dissatisfaction verbally
- Internal team flags a client as "difficult"
- Client merges,acquires, or restructures
- Industry disruption affecting client's business

## 4. Retention Actions

### 🟢 Healthy Client Actions

| Action | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Quarterly business review | Every 90 days | Strengthen relationship, identify opportunities |
| Proactive value check-in | Bi-annually | "Here's what we've saved you" |
| Referral request | Post-project | Leverage satisfaction |
| Upsell introduction | When relevant | Natural growth |
| Thank you/holiday gesture | Yearly | Relationship maintenance |

### 🟡 At-Risk Client Actions

| Action | Timeline | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Personal outreach call** | Within 48h | Understand concerns directly |
| **Service recovery plan** | Within 1 week | Address specific pain points |
| **Executive sponsor meeting** | Within 2 weeks | Show commitment at senior level |
| **Custom SLA or temp discount** | As needed | Rebuild trust |
| **Value recap presentation** | Within 30 days | Remind them of ROI |
| **Increased communication cadence** | For 90 days | Rebuild engagement |

### 🔴 Critical Client Actions

| Action | Timeline | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Same-day response** | Immediate | Show urgency |
| **On-site visit/assessment** | Within 1 week | Face-to-face, understand full picture |
| **Root cause analysis** | Within 1 week | Document issues formally |
| **Escalation to owner** | Immediate | Signal importance |
| **Contract revision/restructure** | Within 2 weeks | Address pricing/value mismatch |
| **Exit interview prep** | If churn is likely | Learn from loss, graceful exit |

### ⚫ Post-Churn Actions

| Action | Timeline | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Exit interview** | Before final | Understand why they left |
| **Leave-the-door-open letter** | After exit | Future re-engagement possible |
| **NPS exit survey** | After exit | Anonymous feedback |
| **Referral request (if amicable)** | After exit | Last ask |
| **Competitive analysis** | After exit | Learn for future |

## 5. Weekly Review Process

### Monday Morning (15 min)

1. Pull health scores from tracking system
2. Flag any Automatic Triggers from past week
3. Review Trend-Based Triggers
4. Assign action items for the week

### Friday Afternoon (10 min)

1. Review completed interventions
2. Update scores based on actions taken
3. Log notes for continuity

### Monthly

1. Full health score recalculation
2. Tier distribution review
3. Intervention effectiveness analysis

## 7. Implementation Notes

- **Use a simple tracker** — Spreadsheet or CRM with custom fields for scores
- **Update monthly minimum** — Weekly for at-risk/critical clients
- **One owner per client** — Assign accountability
- **Document everything** — Notes in CRM for continuity
- **Celebrate wins** — Healthy clients who stay are wins too
