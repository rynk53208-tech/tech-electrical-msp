## 2. Estimated Quarterly Taxes

### 2026 Tax Deadlines
| Quarter | Due Date |
|---------|----------|
| Q1 (Jan-Mar) | April 15, 2026 |
| Q2 (Apr-May) | June 15, 2026 |
| Q3 (Jun-Aug) | September 15, 2026 |
| Q4 (Sep-Dec) | January 15, 2027 |

### How to Calculate

**Self-Employment Tax (2026):**
- 15.3% on net earnings (12.4% SS + 2.9% Medicare)
- Only on 92.35% of net profit

**Safe Harbor Method:**
1. Calculate last year's total tax
2. Divide by 4
3. Pay that each quarter

**Current Year Method:**
1. Estimate annual net profit
2. Calculate self-employment tax (profit × 0.9235 × 0.153)
3. Add income tax (based on your bracket)
4. Divide by 4

## 3. Business Structure: LLC vs S-Corp

### Quick Comparison

| Factor | Single-Member LLC | S-Corp |
|--------|-------------------|--------|
| **Formation** | $50-800 (state) | $50-800 + Form 2553 |
| **Compliance** | Minimal | Annual meetings, minutes, payroll |
| **Taxes** | Pass-through (SE tax on all profit) | Pass-through (salary + distributions) |
| **SE Tax** | 15.3% on all net profit | 15.3% on salary only |
| **Salary Required** | No | Yes (reasonable) |
| **Deduction Options** | QBI only | QBI + reasonable salary deduction |
| **Audit Risk** | Lower | Slightly higher |

### When S-Corp Makes Sense
- Net profit exceeds ~$80,000/year
- You want to split income (salary + distributions)
- Save ~$3,000-8,000/year in SE taxes
- Willing to handle payroll/-compliance

### Recommendation for Irvin
**Start with Single-Member LLC** for simplicity. When annual profit hits $80K+, evaluate S-Corp election to save on self-employment taxes.

## 4. Simple Bookkeeping System

### Recommended Setup

**Bank Accounts:**
- Separate business checking account
- Business savings (for taxes/reserves)

**Tools:**
- **Wave Apps** (free) - invoicing, accounting, receipt scanning
- **QuickBooks Self-Employed** (~$15/mo) - tracks expenses, estimates taxes
- **Google Sheets** - simple tracker if you prefer manual

### Monthly Tasks (30 min/month)
1. **Categorize transactions** - Review all expenses, assign categories
2. **Reconcile** - Match bank statements to records
3. **Invoice clients** - Track unpaid invoices
4. **Check profit/loss** - Review monthly P&L

### Annual Tasks (4-6 hours/year)
1. **January:** Organize receipts by category
2. **February:** Gather Form 1099s, W-2s
3. **March:** Draft tax return or meet with accountant
4. **April:** File taxes, set aside for next year
