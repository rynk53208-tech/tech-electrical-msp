# Weekend & After-Hours Support Plan

**For:** Irvin Avitia's Tech & Electrical Services  
**Type:** Operational Playbook  
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-18

---

## 1. On-Call Rotation

### Structure
- **Primary On-Call:** Irvin (owner) — handles critical/level-1 issues
- **Secondary On-Call:** Contract tech or trusted employee (if applicable)
- **Rotation:** Weekly, Friday 5PM → следующий Friday 5PM

### On-Call Responsibilities
- Respond to emergency calls within **30 minutes** during on-call window
- Triage issue severity (see below)
- Dispatch or handle personally based on skill requirements

### Issue Severity Triage

| Level | Description | Response Time | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| **P1 - Critical** | Business down, data loss, security breach | 30 min | Ransomware, server offline, complete outage |
| **P2 - High** | Major feature broken, workarounds exist | 2 hours | Email down, VPN issues, partial system failure |
| **P3 - Medium** | Non-critical, can wait until next business day | Next business day | Slow performance, minor bugs, user access requests |
| **P4 - Low** | General questions, routine requests | 48 hours | How-to questions, software installs |

## 2. Emergency Contact Process

### For Clients

**Step 1: Client encounters emergency**  
→ Check your service agreement for emergency hotline number

**Step 2: Client calls/texts emergency line**  
→ Voicemail: "You've reached Irvin's tech support emergency line. Press 1 for critical issues, 2 to leave a non-urgent message. Critical issues get callback within 30 minutes."

**Step 3: On-call engineer responds**  
→ Assess severity, determine if remote fix possible or on-site required

**Step 4: Resolution**  
→ Log in CRM (date, time, issue, resolution, time to resolve)

### Contact Info Setup

```
Emergency Line: [YOUR PHONE NUMBER]

```

## 4. How to Sell as Premium Feature

### Messaging Strategy

Don't frame it as "we charge more on weekends." Frame as **value + reliability**.

#### Option A: Bundle with Managed Services
> "Our Premium Support package includes 24/7 emergency response. If your systems go down Saturday night, you're back up within 30 minutes — not waiting until Monday."

#### Option B: Standalone Add-On
> "Weekend support is available as an add-on for $X/month. Covers any emergency, any time. Most clients find one weekend incident pays for the whole year."

#### Option C: Tiered Service Agreement
| Tier | Response Time | Price | Best For |
|------|---------------|-------|----------|
| **Standard** | Next business day | Base rate | Low-critical businesses |
| **Enhanced** | 4-hour response | +$100/mo | Most SMBs |
| **Premium** | 30-minute response | +$250/mo | Healthcare, finance, e-commerce |

### Sales Tactics

1. **Lead with pain points:** "What happens if your POS goes down on a Saturday? With us, you call — we're in within 30 minutes. Without us, you wait until Monday."

2. **Quantify the cost of downtime:** "Your server being down for 4 hours on a Saturday = $X in lost revenue. For $Y/month, you never worry about that."

3. **Use existing clients as proof:** "Our Premium clients have saved an average of $3,200 in avoided downtime this year."

4. **Offer trial:** "Try premium support for 30 days. If you don't use it, downgrade to standard."

### Rejection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We never have emergencies" | "That's great — most clients say that until they do. One incident typically costs more than a year of premium coverage." |
| "Too expensive" | "What would 8 hours of downtime cost you? Our cheapest client recovered $2,000 in a single Sunday callout." |
| "We'll just call you anyway" | "Absolutely — but without the premium agreement, after-hours calls are billed at emergency rates ($150 minimum). Premium is predictable monthly, not surprise per-incident." |
