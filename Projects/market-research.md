### Contract Structures
- **Annual contracts** — Most MSPs push for 12-36 month agreements
- **Monthly retainer** — Becoming more common, 5-10% premium
- **A la carte** — Higher hourly, used by smaller businesses

### Irvin's Positioning Recommendation
- **Entry-level managed**: $99-125/user/mo (competitive entry)
- **Mid-market**: $150-175/user/mo (standard full-service)
- **Premium/security-focused**: $200+/user/mo (leverage cyber creds)
- **Project rates**: $150/hr (hourly), $2500+ (assessments)

## 3. Common IT Pain Points for Small Business

### Top Issues (by frequency)

1. **Cybersecurity fear** — Ransomware, phishing, data breaches
   - Pain level: HIGH
   - Willingness to pay: HIGH

2. **No dedicated IT staff** — "Who do we call?"
   - Pain level: HIGH
   - Willingness to pay: MEDIUM-HIGH

3. **Aging hardware/software** — Deferred upgrades, legacy systems
   - Pain level: MEDIUM
   - Willingness to pay: LOW-MEDIUM

4. **Data backup & recovery** — "What if we lose everything?"
   - Pain level: HIGH
   - Willingness to pay: MEDIUM

5. **Cloud migration** — Moving to 365, Google, AWS
   - Pain level: MEDIUM
   - Willingness to pay: HIGH

6. **Compliance** — HIPAA (medical), PCI (retail), etc.
   - Pain level: MEDIUM-HIGH
   - Willingness to pay: HIGH when enforced

7. **Slow network/performance** — User complaints
   - Pain level: MEDIUM
   - Willingness to pay: LOW

8. **Vendor management** — Too many logins, too many bills
   - Pain level: LOW-MEDIUM
   - Willingness to pay: LOW

## 4. Local Government/Municipal IT Opportunities

### Municipal Targets in Temecula Valley

| Entity | Type | Potential Opportunities |
|--------|------|-------------------------|
| **City of Temecula** | Municipal | Network upgrades, security, consulting |
| **City of Murrieta** | Municipal | Same |
| **City of Wildomar** | Municipal | Same |
| **Riverside County** | County | Larger contracts, specialized needs |
| **Temecula Valley USD** | School district | Security, infrastructure, filtering |
| **Murrieta Valley USD** | School district | Same |
| **Pechanga Band of Indians** | Tribal | Higher budget, strict compliance |

### How Municipal Contracts Work

- **RFP process** — Formal for larger purchases
- **Sole source** — Possible for specialized services (rare)
- **Cooperative purchasing** — Piggyback on existing county/state contracts
- **QBS (Qualifications-Based Selection)** — Used for professional services

### Quick Wins

1. **Security assessments** — Cities need these for compliance
2. **Network diagrams/documentation** — Most don't have current docs
3. **Gap assessments** — Simple report identifying vulnerabilities
4. **Incident response planning** — Required for many grants
5. **Cybersecurity training** — Employees are the weak link

### Getting Started with Municipal

- Attend city council meetings (public comment)
- Check websites for "Doing Business" or vendor registration
- Network with other vendors (electrical, construction) who service cities
- Start small — one assessment, one project
- Build relationships with city IT directors (often 1-2 person teams)
