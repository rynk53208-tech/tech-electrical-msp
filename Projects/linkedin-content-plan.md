## POST IDEAS

### 🔒 Cybersecurity Tips

**Post 1: Password Security**
"3 password mistakes I see in small businesses:

1. Reusing passwords across accounts
2. Not using 2FA
3. Sharing passwords via text

Fix these and you'll stop 90% of breaches. What other security questions do you have?"

**Post 2: Phishing**
"That 'urgent' email from your CEO asking for gift cards? It's probably a scam.

Here's how to check:
- Hover over the sender (not just the name)
- Check for misspellings
- When in doubt, call them directly

Forward this to your team."

**Post 3: Updates**
"Last week alone, Microsoft patched 50+ security vulnerabilities. That's why we do monthly patching for our clients.

If you're manually updating computers, you're one missed patch away from a breach."
