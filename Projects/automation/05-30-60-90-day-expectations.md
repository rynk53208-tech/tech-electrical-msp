# 30-60-90 Day Expectations
## Roadmap for New MSP Client Success

Share this framework with new clients to set clear expectations for the first quarter and establish measurable outcomes.

---

## Overview

The first 90 days define your relationship. This document outlines what the client can expect from your partnership, what you'll deliver, and how success is measured.

**Key principle:** Transparency + deliverables = trust.

---

## 30-DAY MILESTONE
### "Foundation & Quick Wins"

**Focus:** Establish monitoring, fix critical issues, build trust

#### Deliverables
- ✅ **Monitoring & alerting active** — All servers and critical systems monitored 24/7
- ✅ **RMM deployed** — Remote management installed on managed devices
- ✅ **Backup verification** — Tested existing backup, confirmed recovery capability
- ✅ **Initial security hardening** — Firewall rules reviewed, unnecessary ports closed
- ✅ **User access audit** — Local admin accounts documented, overprivileged accounts identified
- ✅ **Quick-wins completed** — 2-3 high-impact, low-effort improvements implemented

#### Sample Quick-Wins (Pick 3-5)
- Windows Update patching automation enabled
- Automatic antivirus updates configured
- Redundant DNS configured
- Unnecessary services disabled
- Basic firewall rules tightened
- Backup schedule optimized

#### Meetings & Communication
- [ ] Weekly status call (30 min) — Week 1, 2, 3, 4
- [ ] Initial findings presentation (60 min) — End of Week 1
- [ ] Quick-wins summary (30 min) — Mid-month
- [ ] 30-day checkpoint call (60 min) — End of month

#### Success Metrics
- Zero critical security vulnerabilities remain unpatched
- Backup tested and restorable
- RMM reporting shows system baseline
- All servers monitored with alerting active
- Client confidence building (informal feedback: "Yes, we're seeing value")

---

## 60-DAY MILESTONE
### "Strategy & Implementation"

**Focus:** Execute long-term improvements, create compliance roadmap

#### Deliverables
- ✅ **Comprehensive assessment report** — Detailed findings with risk ratings
- ✅ **12-month IT roadmap** — Prioritized recommendations with costs/timelines
- ✅ **Compliance roadmap** — If required (HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, CMMC, etc.)
- ✅ **Disaster recovery plan** — Documented recovery procedures & timelines (RTO/RPO)
- ✅ **Security policy documentation** — Access control, password policy, incident response
- ✅ **Major project kick-off** — Begin implementation of agreed-upon improvements

#### Sample Projects (Tier by Priority)
**Tier 1 (Urgent — Start now)**
- Multi-factor authentication deployment
- Backup solution upgrade
- Network segmentation
- Firewall appliance upgrade

**Tier 2 (Important — Next 3-6 months)**
- Server consolidation/virtualization
- Active Directory hardening
- Endpoint detection & response (EDR)
- Enhanced logging/SIEM

**Tier 3 (Strategic — 6-12 months)**
- Disaster recovery site
- Network redundancy
- Managed security services expansion
- Cloud infrastructure migration

#### Meetings & Communication
- [ ] Weekly status call (30 min) — Ongoing
- [ ] Assessment presentation (90 min) — Week 5-6
- [ ] Project planning session (60 min) — Week 7
- [ ] Budget/approval meeting (60 min) — Week 8

#### Success Metrics
- Formal assessment report delivered & reviewed
- Client has approved prioritized implementation roadmap
- First major project underway (measurable progress)
- Client Net Promoter Score (NPS) feedback: >7/10
- Support tickets decreasing (systems stabilizing)

## 90-DAY MILESTONE
### "Results & Renewal"

**Focus:** Demonstrate ROI, secure long-term relationship

#### Deliverables
- ✅ **90-day business review** — Comprehensive performance summary
- ✅ **Completed projects** — 2-3 major improvements completed or in-progress
- ✅ **Compliance certification** (if applicable) — Audits passed, gaps closed
- ✅ **Cost savings analysis** — Demonstrated value from optimizations/efficiency gains
- ✅ **User satisfaction survey** — Feedback from client IT team & end users
- ✅ **Renewal discussion** — Confirm satisfaction, discuss Year 2 strategy

#### Sample Business Review Contents
| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Highlights** | Projects completed, issues resolved, improvements made |
| **By the Numbers** | Uptime %, tickets resolved, response times, cost savings |
| **Security Posture** | Vulnerabilities remediated, compliance status, risk reduction |
| **Performance** | System speed improvements, backup success rate, etc. |
| **Team Feedback** | Client IT staff & user satisfaction |
| **Year 2 Roadmap** | Upcoming projects, budget planning, strategic initiatives |

#### Business Review Presentation
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Attendees:** Client executive, IT manager, your account manager + technical lead  
**Format:** 
1. Recap of first 90 days (15 min)
2. Key results & ROI (20 min)
3. Project updates & timelines (20 min)
4. Security & compliance summary (15 min)
5. Year 2 strategy & pricing (20 min)

#### Meetings & Communication
- [ ] Monthly status call (30 min) — Months 2 & 3
- [ ] Project steering calls (30 min) — As needed per project
- [ ] Executive business review (90 min) — Week 12-13
- [ ] Contract renewal discussion (60 min) — End of Month 3

#### Success Metrics
- 2+ major projects completed successfully
- Client NPS score: >8/10 ("Would recommend")
- Support ticket volume stable or decreasing
- All critical security issues addressed
- Client commits to Year 2 renewal
- Potential upsell identified (security, backup, cloud services)

## Common 90-Day Challenges & Solutions

| Challenge | Why It Happens | Solution |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| **Scope creep** | Client keeps adding "just one more" project | Revisit prioritization, get executive approval before adding work |
| **Budget approval delays** | Finance team needs time to approve spending | Plan budget requests 30 days in advance, provide ROI justification |
| **Staff turnover** | Key client contact leaves | Quickly identify and build relationship with replacement |
| **Technical roadblocks** | Unexpected compatibility issues or hardware failures | Have contingency plans, communicate delays transparently |
| **Unrealistic expectations** | Client expected faster results | Set expectations early, show incremental progress weekly |
| **Low adoption** | Users resistant to new tools/processes | Provide training, highlight benefits, get buy-in from champions |

## Renewal & Upsell Opportunities

By day 90, you should have identified:

1. **Natural upsells** — What additional services would benefit them?
   - Enhanced security (EDR, SIEM, managed security)
   - Backup/disaster recovery
   - Cloud infrastructure
   - Compliance consulting

2. **Risk mitigation** — What could they lose without it?
   - What happens if their backup fails?
   - What happens if ransomware hits?
   - What compliance penalties could they face?

3. **Efficiency gains** — What could save them money?
   - Automation of manual processes
   - Server consolidation
   - Cloud cost optimization

**Target:** Identify 1-2 upsell opportunities to discuss in Year 2 planning.
