# MSP Client Onboarding Package
## Professional Onboarding Flow for New Clients

This package contains the complete client onboarding workflow for your MSP/MSSP services.

### Contents
1. **Welcome Email Sequence** - 3 automated emails
2. **New Client Checklist** - Documents and info client must provide
3. **Service Agreement Process** - Signing workflow
4. **First Week Timeline** - Day-by-day onboarding schedule
5. **30-60-90 Day Expectations** - Milestones and success metrics

### Quick Start
- Copy email templates to your email marketing platform or CRM
- Customize bracketed fields `[Client Name]`, `[Your Company]`, etc.
- Use the checklist during onboarding calls
- Share timeline with client for expectations
- Review 30-60-90 milestones in monthly check-ins

---

*Customize all templates with your company branding, contact info, and legal disclaimers.*
