# Client Onboarding Flow - MSP Clients

## Welcome Email Sequence

### Email 1: Welcome & What's Next
**Subject:** Welcome to Tech & Electrical Services! 🛡️

> Hi [Client Name],
> 
> Thank you for choosing Tech & Electrical Services for your IT needs. We're excited to have you as a client!
> 
> Here's what happens next:
> - Day 1-2: You'll receive a welcome packet with our service agreement
> - Day 3: We'll schedule your initial assessment call
> - Day 7: Onsite visit (if needed) to map your network
> 
> In the meantime, please review the attached documents and let me know if you have any questions.
> 
> Looking forward to working with you!
> 
> Best,
> Irvin
> Tech & Electrical Services

### Email 2: Document Collection
**Subject:** Quick info to get you set up 📋

> Hi [Client Name],
> 
> To get your IT infrastructure fully optimized, we need a few things:
> 
> ☐ Current network diagram (if you have one)
> ☐ List of all devices (computers, servers, POS, etc.)
> ☐ Current software subscriptions
> ☐ IT support contacts (if any)
> ☐ Passwords/documents you'll be trusting us with
> 
> I've attached our secure document upload link. Everything is encrypted and safe.
> 
> Questions? Just reply to this email.
> 
> Best,
> Irvin

### Email 3: Assessment Scheduled
**Subject:** Your IT Assessment is Scheduled ✅

> Hi [Client Name],
> 
> Your initial assessment is confirmed for [DATE] at [TIME].
> 
> What to expect:
> - 30-45 minute call to discuss your pain points
> - Review of current infrastructure
> - Quick wins we can implement immediately
> - Roadmap for the first 30 days
> 
> Please have someone with admin access on the call.
> 
> See you soon!
> 
> Best,
> Irvin

## New Client Checklist

### Week 1
- [ ] Welcome email sent
- [ ] Service agreement signed
- [ ] NDA signed (if required)
- [ ] Initial assessment scheduled
- [ ] Client portal account created
- [ ] Documentation collected
- [ ] Network discovery scan completed
- [ ] Security baseline established

### Week 2
- [ ] Initial assessment completed
- [ ] Critical issues identified
- [ ] Quick wins implemented
- [ ] Monitoring agents deployed
- [ ] First invoice sent

### Month 1
- [ ] All devices inventoried
- [ ] Patches applied
- [ ] Backup solution verified
- [ ] Security training scheduled for staff
- [ ] 30-day review meeting

## 30-60-90 Day Expectations

### 30 Days
- All devices inventoried and monitored
- Security baseline established
- Quick wins completed
- No critical vulnerabilities

### 60 Days  
- Documentation complete
- Proactive maintenance running
- Staff training completed
- First quarterly report delivered

### 90 Days
- Full infrastructure optimized
- SLA performance reviewed
- Expansion opportunities identified
- Annual planning discussion
