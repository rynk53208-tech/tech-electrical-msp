# Invoice Email Templates

Professional email templates for Irvin's tech business. Customize brackets `[ ]` as needed.

---

## 1. Invoice Sent

**Subject:** Invoice #[INV-XXX] for [Service Name] – Due [DATE]

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for your business! Please find your invoice attached or below.

**Invoice Details:**
- **Invoice #:** [INV-XXX]
- **Date Issued:** [DATE]
- **Due Date:** [DATE]
- **Amount Due:** $[AMOUNT]

**Services Provided:**
- [Service 1] – $[AMOUNT]
- [Service 2] – $[AMOUNT]

**Total:** $[AMOUNT]

**Payment Options:**
- Bank Transfer: [BANK DETAILS]
- PayPal: [PAYPAL LINK]
- Cash/Check: [ADDRESS]

If you have any questions, let me know.

Best,  
**Irvin Avitia**  
[Company Name]  
[Phone] | [Email]

## Quick Notes

- Replace all `[BRACKETED]` placeholders before sending
- Adjust tone based on your relationship with the client
- For recurring clients, consider a softer approach on first reminder
- Save payment details in your invoicing software to auto-populate
