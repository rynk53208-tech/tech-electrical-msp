## Checklist Completion Tips

1. **Schedule a kickoff call** to walk through the checklist together
2. **Set a 5-day deadline** for client to provide all documents
3. **Create a shared folder** (OneDrive, Google Drive) for document collection
4. **Use your PSA tool** to track checklist completion as onboarding tickets
5. **Escalate early** if items are missing — don't wait until implementation
