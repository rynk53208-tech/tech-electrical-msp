## Implementation Timeline

### Week 1: Preparation
- [ ] Review all 5 documents with your team
- [ ] Customize templates with your company name, contacts, portal URL
- [ ] Update email templates with your branding/logo
- [ ] Identify who handles each stage (who sends emails? who does kickoff call?)
- [ ] Set up automation in CRM (trigger emails on contract signed date)

### Week 2: Soft Launch
- [ ] Use with next 3 new clients
- [ ] Collect feedback from your team
- [ ] Identify what's working, what needs tweaking
- [ ] Refine templates based on real-world usage

### Week 3+: Full Rollout
- [ ] Apply to all new MSP/MSSP clients
- [ ] Train your team on the process
- [ ] Measure: How many clients renew? NPS score? Upsell rate?

## Common Pitfalls (Avoid These)

❌ **Don't:** Send all 3 welcome emails in one batch  
✅ **Do:** Space them out (Day 0, Day 2, Day 7)

❌ **Don't:** Skip the 5-day checklist deadline  
✅ **Do:** Set firm dates and follow up aggressively

❌ **Don't:** Do first week discovery without preparation  
✅ **Do:** Have checklist complete before kickoff call

❌ **Don't:** Disappear after Day 30  
✅ **Do:** Monthly checkpoints + quarterly business reviews

❌ **Don't:** Surprise clients with bills or scope changes  
✅ **Do:** Over-communicate pricing, scope, timelines upfront

## First Client Walkthrough

**For your first onboarding with this system:**

1. Pick your "easiest" client (cooperative, responsive, stable)
2. Walk through all 5 documents with your team
3. Have the Account Manager own the process end-to-end
4. Document what works, what feels clunky
5. Refine before rolling out to all clients

**Expected timeline:** 30 minutes to customize, 2-3 hours to fully implement per client
