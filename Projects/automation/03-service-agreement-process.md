# Service Agreement Signing Process
## Professional Contract Workflow for MSP Clients

This document outlines the step-by-step process for getting new clients to sign and return their service agreements.

---

## Workflow Overview

```
┌─────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐    ┌─────────────┐
│   Prepare   │───▶│    Send     │───▶│   Review    │───▶│   Execute   │
│   Contract  │    │   Contract  │    │  & Sign     │    │  & Archive  │
└─────────────┘    └─────────────┘    └─────────────┘    └─────────────┘
     1-2 days          Day 0             Days 1-5           Day 5-7
```

## Step 1: Prepare the Contract

### Before Sending
- [ ] **Select service tier** — Confirm SLA level with client (Basic/Standard/Premium/Custom)
- [ ] **Verify scope** — Ensure all services discussed are captured in the agreement
- [ ] **Check pricing** — Confirm one-time setup fees, monthly recurring revenue, and any additional costs
- [ ] **Add client details** — Company name, address, contact information
- [ ] **Include exhibits** — Attach relevant schedules (A: Service List, B: SLA Terms, C: Acceptable Use)
- [ ] **Review for accuracy** — Double-check names, dates, and figures

### Contract Package Should Include:
1. **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** — Main contract terms
2. **Service Schedule/Exhibit A** — Detailed services included
3. **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** — Response times, uptime guarantees
4. **Acceptable Use Policy** — What's covered, what's not
5. **Privacy Policy** — Data handling commitments
6. **Proposal/Quote** — Summary of services and pricing

## Step 2: Send the Contract

### Delivery Method
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **DocuSign/HelloSign** | E-signatures, legally binding, trackable | Monthly cost |
| **PDF with wet signature** | Simple, no software needed | Manual follow-up, slower |
| **CRM-integrated signing** | Auto-tracked in PSA | Requires integration |
| **Physical mail** | Works for resistant clients | Slowest option |

### Sending Best Practices
- [ ] Send from **your professional email** (not noreply@)
- [ ] Include a **personalized cover note** explaining next steps
- [ ] Set a **clear deadline** (e.g., "Please review and sign by [Date]")
- [ ] Provide your **direct contact** for questions
- [ ] Explain **what happens next** once signed

### Sample Cover Email:

> **Subject:** Your Service Agreement from [Your Company]
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Attached is your service agreement for the [Service Tier] plan we discussed.
>
> **What's included:**
> - Managed IT services as outlined in our proposal
> - [List key services: 24/7 monitoring, help desk, security, etc.]
> - Monthly retainer of $[Amount]
>
> **Next steps:**
> 1. Review the agreement
> 2. Sign using the link below
> 3. I'll receive an automatic notification
> 4. We schedule your onboarding kickoff
>
> **Questions?** Call me at [Phone] or reply to this email.
>
> Please sign by [Deadline Date] to secure your onboarding slot.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Step 3: Follow Up

### Timeline for Follow-ups

| Day | Action | Method |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Day 0 | Initial send | Email with signing link |
| Day 2 | First follow-up | Email — "Just checking if you had questions" |
| Day 4 | Second follow-up | Phone call — addressing concerns |
| Day 6-7 | Final notice | Email with deadline |

### Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Need to review with my lawyer" | "Absolutely, that's smart. Can we schedule a call to answer any questions your legal team has?" |
| "Need to compare other options" | "I understand. What specific areas would you like to compare? I can address any concerns." |
| "Price is higher than expected" | "Let's review what's included — I want to make sure you're getting value. Can we walk through the services together?" |
| "Just need more time" | "No problem. What date works for you to make a decision? I'll follow up then." |
| "We haven't signed yet" | "I understand. What would help move this forward? Is there anything I can clarify or adjust?" |

## Step 4: Execute & Archive

### Once Signed
- [ ] **Receive automatic notification** (if using e-signature)
- [ ] **Download fully executed contract** (PDF with signatures)
- [ ] **Save to client file** in your PSA/CRM (e.g., ConnectWise, Autotask, Syncro)
- [ ] **Set up billing** in accounting software
- [ ] **Trigger onboarding workflow** — Send welcome email, create onboarding tickets

### Archiving Requirements
- Keep signed agreements for **minimum 7 years** (or per local regulations)
- Store in **cloud backup** (not just local)
- Organize by: **Client Name > Contracts > [Year]**
- Maintain **audit trail** of all contract versions
