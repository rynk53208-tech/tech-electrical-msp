## Automation Notes

- **CRM Integration:** These can be triggered via CRM automations (e.g., in AirTable, HubSpot, or similar)
- **Personalization:** Replace bracketed fields [Name], [Project Type], etc. from your CRM
- **Tone:** Keep it direct. No "I hope this email finds you well." Irvin's brand is sharp, competent, no-fluff
- **Timing:** Adjust based on your sales cycle—this one's tuned for SMB/consulting pace
