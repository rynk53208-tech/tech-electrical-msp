# Welcome Email Sequence
## Automated 3-Email Onboarding Flow

Send these emails in sequence after contract signing:
- **Email 1:** Send immediately (Day 0) - Welcome & Next Steps
- **Email 2:** Send 2 days later (Day 2) - Checklist Reminder
- **Email 3:** Send 7 days later (Day 7) - First Week Check-in

---

## EMAIL 1: Welcome & Next Steps (Day 0)

**Subject:** Welcome to [Your Company]! Here's Your Onboarding Roadmap

---

Dear [Client Name],

Welcome aboard! We're thrilled to have you as a client of [Your Company]. Your decision to partner with us for your IT and cybersecurity needs is a big step toward protecting and streamlining your business operations.

**What happens next:**

Within the next 24-48 hours, you'll receive a call from [Account Manager Name], your dedicated onboarding specialist. They'll walk you through:

- [ ] Reviewing your completed onboarding checklist
- [ ] Scheduling your first technical discovery session
- [ ] Setting up your client portal access
- [ ] Introducing you to your dedicated support team

**Your onboarding checklist:**
We've prepared a checklist of documents and information we'll need from your team. Please review it attached to this email or [click here for the digital version].

**Your client portal:**
Visit [portal.yourcompany.com] to access:
- Service tickets and status
- Monthly reports
- Documentation and knowledge base
- Billing and invoices

Questions before your onboarding call? Reply to this email or call us directly at [Phone Number].

We look forward to working with you!

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Company]
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]

## Customization Notes

**Bracketed fields to replace:**
- `[Your Company]` — Your MSP/MSSP business name
- `[Client Name]` — Client's contact name
- `[Account Manager Name]` — Your team member handling onboarding
- `[Your Name]` — Sender's name
- `[Your Title]` — Sender's job title
- `[Phone Number]` — Your contact number
- `[Email]` — Your email address
- `[Website]` — Your website URL
- `[portal.yourcompany.com]` — Your client portal URL

**Automation tips:**
- Connect to CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, ConnectWise, etc.)
- Set up triggered sends on contract signed date
- Include personalization tokens for client details
- Add your email signature with logo

**Legal considerations:**
- Include unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM compliant)
- Add physical address
- Review for accuracy before sending
