# Lead Conversion Workflow ⚡

**For:** Irvin Avitia's Tech & Electrical Services  
**Purpose:** Systematic approach to convert inquiries into paying clients

---

## 1. Lead Capture Process

### Sources to Track
- Website contact form
- Google Business Profile inquiries
- Facebook/Nextdoor local ads
- Referrals (track source)
- Cold outreach (if applicable)
- Word of mouth

### Capture Requirements
Every lead must capture:
- **Name** (first + last)
- **Phone number** (primary contact)
- **Email** (for quotes/documentation)
- **Service needed** (from predefined list)
- **Urgency** (ASAP, this week, this month, just researching)
- **Source** (where they found you)
- **Notes** (anything they mention about the problem)

### Immediate Response (Within 15 min if possible)
- SMS or call: "Hey [name], this is Irvin. Got your request about [service]. Can I ask a few quick questions to get you sorted?"
- If after hours: "Thanks for reaching out. I'm [name] — I'll follow up first thing tomorrow. Quick question: is this urgent or can it wait until [day]?"

## 2. Qualification Steps

### Quick Qualify (5 min call or text)
1. **Confirm the problem** — "So you're seeing [symptom] on the [device/system]?"
2. **Scope it** — "How many devices?" / "How long has this been going on?"
3. **Budget check** — "Do you have a budget in mind, or should I give you a range?"
4. **Timeline** — "When do you need this done?"
5. **Decision maker** — "Are you the one making the call on this?"

### Qualify OUT (Not a fit)
- Can't afford your minimum (set one: e.g., $75 minimum for repair)
- Looking for free advice only
- Wants you to compete on price against big box stores (use your expertise pitch)
- Unrealistic timeline you can't meet
- Scope outside your services (refer out or decline)

### Qualify IN (Hot lead)
- Has a clear problem you solve
- Budget aligns with your pricing
- Timeline is reasonable
- Can make decision / owner is involved
- Has seen your work or has a referral

### Hot Lead Actions
- Schedule on-site assessment or remote consultation same-day or next-day
- Send preliminary quote if scope is clear
- Mention next steps clearly

## 3. Follow-Up Schedule

### Template: Lead Response Timeline

| Stage | When | Action | Method |
|-------|------|--------|--------|
| **Initial** | Within 15 min | Acknowledge lead, ask qualifying question | SMS |
| **Follow-up 1** | If no response in 2 hours (business hours) | "Hey [name], just checking if you got my last msg. Still need help with [issue]?" | SMS |
| **Follow-up 2** | 24 hours later | "Hi [name], following up on [service]. I have availability [day/time]. Let me know." | SMS + Email |
| **Follow-up 3** | 72 hours later | "Hey [name], wanted to touch base one more time. If you've found someone else, no worries — but if you still need help, I'm here." | SMS |
| **Close** | 7 days after last contact | Remove from active pipeline, mark as "Nurture" | N/A |

### Rules
- **Maximum 3 active follow-ups** before marking as cold
- **Never spam** — one message per channel per touch
- **Use voicemails** — leave a 15-sec message with name, number, and callback offer
- **Log everything** — track in CRM or spreadsheet (date, method, response)

### Nurture List (Cold Leads)
- Add to email list (if opted in)
- Occasional value-add: "Hey, we just posted a tip on [relevant topic] — might help with [their issue]"
- Check in every 90 days

## 4. Closing Process

### Before Closing
1. **Present solution** — Clear scope, timeline, pricing
2. **Explain value** — Why your service is worth the cost (warranty, expertise, Northrop background, board-level skills)
3. **Get commitment** — "Shall I book you in for [date]?"

### Closing Techniques
- **The close:** "I can have this done by [date]. Card or invoice?"
- **The urgency close:** "I have one slot left this week — want to lock it in?"
- **The referral close:** "While we're scheduling — do you know anyone else who might need this?"

### Post-Close
1. Send invoice or agreement immediately
2. Confirm appointment/time
3. Send reminder day before
4. Show up, do the work, over-deliver

### Post-Job
- Ask for review (Google, Facebook)
- Ask for referral: "Know anyone else who needs tech help?"
