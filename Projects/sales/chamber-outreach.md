# Temecula Valley Chamber of Commerce - Outreach Plan

## Why Join the Chamber?

- **Networking:** Access to monthly mixers, networking events, and business connections
- **Credibility:** Member directory listing builds trust
- **Leads:** 83% of chamber members generate leads through events
- **Resources:** Access to business resources, advocacy, and discounts

## How to Join

1. **Visit:** temecula.org (Temecula Valley Chamber)
2. **Membership levels:** $300-1,500/year depending on business size
3. **Process:** Online application, pay dues, attend orientation

## Upcoming Events to Target

- **Monthly Mixers:** Best for quick networking, bring 30-50 business cards
- **Business After Hours:** Evening events at host businesses
- **Power Breakfasts:** Early morning networking with speakers

## Pitch Deck for Chamber Presentations

### 1-Minute Elevator Pitch
> "Tech & Electrical Services provides enterprise-grade cybersecurity and IT support to small businesses in Temecula. With my background at Northrop Grumman, we bring Fortune 500 security to local businesses at SMB prices. We specialize in auto shops, medical offices, and retail—any business that needs their data protected but can't afford a full IT department."

### Services to Highlight
- Managed IT Services ($99-199/mo)
- Cybersecurity Starter Pack ($1,500-3,000)
- MDR Lite ($199/mo)
- Computer Repair & Upgrades
- Electrical Services

### Call to Action
- "Schedule a free 30-minute IT assessment"
- "Mention this event for $200 off your first MSP contract"
