## Outreach Strategy Notes

- **Peak hours for calls:** 9-11 AM, 2-4 PM (avoid lunch 12-1 PM)
- **Best contact method:** Phone to practice manager/office manager
- **Decision maker:** Office Manager or Practice Owner
- **Typical needs:** IT support, cybersecurity, accounting/billing software, practice management systems

### Common Service Opportunities

1. **Cybersecurity & HIPAA Compliance** - Healthcare data protection increasingly critical
2. **Practice Management Software** - Scheduling, billing, patient records
3. **Network/IT Support** - Server maintenance, backup systems, cloud solutions
4. **Computer Repair & Hardware** - Workstations, printers, networking equipment
5. **Electrical Services** - Office upgrades, lighting, power systems
