## 3. WHAT TO PITCH AT MIXERS

### For Irvin's Tech Services
**Strong Pitches:**
1. **Cybersecurity Assessment** (30-sec elevator pitch)
   - "We help local businesses protect against cyber threats with rapid security audits and MSP solutions"
   - Appeal: Business owners fear data breaches
   - CTA: "Let's grab coffee to discuss your tech security posture"

2. **Managed IT Services** (MSP)
   - "Reduce IT costs and headaches with our managed services—predictable monthly pricing, 24/7 support"
   - Appeal: Predictability + peace of mind
   - CTA: "Free IT health check"

3. **Custom PC/Workstation Builds**
   - "Need high-performance custom builds? We specialize in configuring systems for your exact needs"
   - Appeal: Small businesses often buy off-the-shelf; custom = competitive edge
   - CTA: "Schedule a free consultation"

4. **Board-Level Hardware Repair** (Differentiatior!)
   - "Unlike the big chains, we repair at the component level—no expensive replacements"
   - Appeal: Cost savings + faster turnaround
   - CTA: "Refer your broken devices; we'll diagnose for free"

5. **Software Development/Custom Applications**
   - "Automate your business processes with custom software tailored to your workflow"
   - Appeal: Efficiency, automation, cost reduction
   - CTA: "Let's discuss your workflow"

### Pitch Tips for Mixers
- Lead with **business value** (cost savings, revenue growth, risk reduction)
- Keep it to **20-30 seconds** (people network quickly)
- **Ask for the conversation**, not the sale
- Offer a **small incentive** (free assessment, discount on first service)
- Hand over **business card** with QR code linking to case studies
- Follow up within **48 hours** with personalized note

### Target Audience at Mixers
- **Small business owners** (1-50 employees) - Primary target
- **Professional service providers** (accountants, attorneys, insurance)
- **Real estate/property management** - High need for IT
- **Retail/restaurants** - Growing tech needs (POS, inventory)
- **Medical/dental offices** - HIPAA compliance, EHR support
- **Construction firms** - Growing tech adoption

## 4. NETWORKING STRATEGY

### Pre-Event
1. Research attendees if possible (ask chamber for list)
2. Prepare 2-3 industry-specific pitches
3. Print fresh business cards
4. Identify 5-6 target businesses to connect with
5. Set personal goal: "Meet 10 people, follow up with 5"

### At the Event
- **Arrive early** (first 10-15 min, less crowded)
- **Smile, listen, ask questions** (people love being heard)
- **Avoid booth camping** - rotate, meet new people
- **Look for pain points** in conversations (IT problems come up naturally)
- **Exchange contact info** - phone + email if possible
- **Offer value first** - "I help local businesses with cybersecurity. Happy to do a quick assessment"

### Post-Event (48 hours)
- Send personalized LinkedIn/email: "Great meeting you at Chamber mixer, specifically loved hearing about [their business]. Would love to discuss [relevant service]"
- Include one relevant resource (article, tip, case study)
- Set calendar reminder for 2-week follow-up if no response
