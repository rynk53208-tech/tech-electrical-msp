## 3. RESPONSE TIME GUARANTEES

### 3.1 Priority Definitions & Response Targets

| Priority | Description | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|----------|-------------|-------|----------|---------|
| **P1 - Critical** | System down, security breach, complete outage | 4 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| **P2 - High** | Major function impaired, significant performance degradation | 8 hours | 2 hours | 1 hour |
| **P3 - Medium** | Workaround available, partial functionality loss | 24 hours | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| **P4 - Low** | Minor issue, feature request, general inquiry | 72 hours | 8 hours | 4 hours |

### 3.2 Response Time Measurement

- **Start Time:** Timestamp when Client submits ticket via approved channel (email, portal, phone)
- **Acknowledgment:** Provider confirms receipt and assigns technician
- **On-Site Response:** For on-site services, technician arrival at Client location
- **Business Hours:** Standard: Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM PST. Premium includes 24/7 coverage.

### 3.3 Response Time Exclusions

Response times do not apply when delays are caused by:
- Client's failure to provide required access, credentials, or information
- Force majeure events (natural disasters, widespread outages)
- Third-party vendor issues outside Provider's control
- Scheduled maintenance (with 72-hour advance notice)

### 4.2 Uptime Calculation

```
Uptime % = (Total Minutes in Month - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month × 100
```

**Excluded from Downtime:**
- Scheduled maintenance (during approved maintenance windows)
- Client-initiated changes or downtime
- Third-party service failures
- Force majeure events

## 5. SLA BREACH REMEDIES

### 5.1 Service Credits

When Provider fails to meet SLA commitments, Client shall receive credits applied to next invoice:

| Breach Type | Credit |
|-------------|--------|
| Response Time Miss (P1) | 10% of monthly service fee per incident |
| Response Time Miss (P2) | 5% of monthly service fee per incident |
| Uptime Miss (per 1% below target) | 10% of monthly service fee |
| Multiple breaches in same month | Capped at tier limit (see Section 2) |

### 5.2 Credit Request Process

1. Client submits credit request within 10 business days of incident
2. Request must include: ticket number, timestamp, impact description
3. Provider reviews and responds within 5 business days
4. Credit applied to next invoice if approved

### 5.3 Limitations

- **Maximum Credits:** As specified in Section 2 (10%/25%/50% cap)
- **Consequential Damages:** Service credits are Client's sole and exclusive remedy for SLA breaches
- **Exclusions Apply:** Credits do not apply during excluded periods (Section 3.3, 4.2)

### 5.4 Escalation for Chronic Breaches

If Provider fails to meet SLA for **3 consecutive months**, Client may:
- Request a performance review meeting
- Negotiate modified terms
- Terminate agreement per MSA provisions
